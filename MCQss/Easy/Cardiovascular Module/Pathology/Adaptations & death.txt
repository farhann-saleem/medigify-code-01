Question: Irreversible myocardial injury occurs after approximately:
  A. 10 minutes
  B. 30 minutes
  C. 1 hour
  D. 4 hours
  E. 5 minutes
  Correct Option: B
  Explanation:
 •	Correct (B): Irreversible myocardial damage begins after about 20–30 minutes of ischemia.
 •	Incorrect (A): 10 minutes is still reversible injury phase.
 •	Incorrect (C): 1 hour is beyond initial irreversible threshold.
 •	Incorrect (D): 4 hours represents established infarction.
 •	Incorrect (E): 5 minutes is too early for irreversible injury.
