Question 19
Which functional component of the systemic circulation contains the highest percentage of the total blood volume (approximately 64%) under normal resting conditions?
A. Capillaries
B. Pulmonary circulation
C. Systemic arteries
D. Venae cavae
E. Veins, venules, and venous sinuses
Correct Option: E
Explanation:
Correct (E): Veins, venules, and venous sinuses serve as capacitance vessels and contain approximately 64% of total blood volume at rest.
Incorrect (A): Capillaries contain only a small fraction of circulating blood volume.
Incorrect (B): The pulmonary circulation contains substantially less blood than the systemic venous system.
Incorrect (C): Systemic arteries are low-capacitance vessels and store relatively little blood.
Incorrect (D): The venae cavae alone do not contain as much blood as the entire venous system.

Question: What is the mathematical definition of vascular compliance (capacitance)?
A. Change in pressure divided by change in volume (ΔP / ΔV)
B. Change in volume divided by change in pressure (ΔV / ΔP)
C. Flow divided by resistance (F / R)
D. Pressure multiplied by resistance (P × R)
E. Velocity divided by cross-sectional area (v / A)
Correct Option: B
Explanation:
Correct (B): Vascular compliance is defined as ΔV / ΔP, representing volume change per unit pressure change.
Incorrect (A): ΔP / ΔV represents elastance, not compliance.
Incorrect (C): F / R describes Ohm-like flow relation, not vessel compliance.
Incorrect (D): P × R has no physiological definition in compliance.
Incorrect (E): v / A is not a compliance parameter but relates to flow geometry.
