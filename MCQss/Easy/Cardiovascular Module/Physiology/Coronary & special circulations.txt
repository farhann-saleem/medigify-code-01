Question: What is the primary regulator of coronary blood flow under normal physiological conditions?
A. Sympathetic alpha receptor activity
B. Myocardial oxygen demand and metabolic activity
C. Plasma estrogen concentration
D. Renal filtration rate
E. Systemic hemoglobin concentration alone
Correct Option: B
Explanation:
Correct (B): Coronary blood flow is tightly matched to myocardial oxygen demand via metabolic autoregulation.
Incorrect (A): Alpha constriction is overridden by metabolic control.
Incorrect (C): Estrogen is not a primary acute regulator.
Incorrect (D): Renal function is unrelated to coronary flow.
Incorrect (E): Hemoglobin affects oxygen content, not primary flow regulation.

Question: Which substance is a key vasodilator released by cardiac muscle during hypoxia?
A. Acetylcholine
B. Adenosine
C. Angiotensin II
D. Calcium ions
E. Nitric oxide from endothelium exclusively
Correct Option: B
Explanation:
Correct (B): Adenosine is released during myocardial hypoxia and causes potent coronary vasodilation.
Incorrect (A): Acetylcholine is not the primary metabolic vasodilator.
Incorrect (C): Angiotensin II is vasoconstrictive.
Incorrect (D): Calcium increases contraction, not vasodilation.
Incorrect (E): Nitric oxide is endothelial, not myocardial hypoxia-specific.
