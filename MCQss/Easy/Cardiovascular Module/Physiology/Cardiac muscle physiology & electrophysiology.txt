What is the normal resting membrane potential of a ventricular cardiac muscle fiber?
 Options:
 A. -100 to -110 mV
 B. -95 to -100 mV
 C. -85 to -95 mV
 D. -70 to -75 mV
 E. -55 to -60 mV
 Correct Option: C
 Explanation:
 • Correct (C): The normal resting membrane potential of a ventricular cardiac muscle fiber is approximately -85 to -95 mV due to high potassium permeability at rest.
 • Incorrect (A): -100 to -110 mV is more negative than the normal ventricular resting membrane potential.
 • Incorrect (B): -95 to -100 mV is slightly more negative than the typical physiological range.
 • Incorrect (D): -70 to -75 mV is insufficiently negative for normal ventricular cardiac muscle fibers.
 • Incorrect (E): -55 to -60 mV is closer to threshold potentials than the resting membrane potential.

What is the velocity of conduction of the cardiac impulse in the Purkinje fibers?
 Options:
 A. 0.03 to 0.05 m/sec
 B. 0.3 to 0.5 m/sec
 C. 1.0 to 1.4 m/sec
 D. 1.5 to 4.0 m/sec
 E. 10 to 20 m/sec
 Correct Option: D
 Explanation:
 • Correct (D): Purkinje fibers conduct impulses at 1.5 to 4.0 m/sec, ensuring rapid activation of the ventricles.
 • Incorrect (A): 0.03 to 0.05 m/sec corresponds more closely to AV nodal conduction.
 • Incorrect (B): 0.3 to 0.5 m/sec is typical of atrial and ventricular muscle fibers.
 • Incorrect (C): 1.0 to 1.4 m/sec is slower than the usual Purkinje fiber conduction velocity.
 • Incorrect (E): 10 to 20 m/sec exceeds physiological conduction velocities in the cardiac conduction system.

C. -70 to -75 mV
D. -85 to -95 mV
E. -95 to -100 mV

ventricular fibers.

potential.

Question: Which ion is primarily responsible for the plateau phase (Phase 2) of the ventricular
action potential?
A. Calcium ion
B. Chloride ion
C. Magnesium ion
D. Potassium ion
E. Sodium ion
Correct Option: A
Explanation:
Correct (A): Slow inward entry of calcium ion through L-type calcium channels produces the
plateau phase.
Incorrect (B): Chloride ion does not play a major role in generating the plateau phase.
Incorrect (C): Magnesium ion is important enzymatically but does not create the plateau phase.
Incorrect (D): Potassium ion efflux contributes mainly to repolarization.
Incorrect (E): Sodium ion influx is responsible for rapid depolarization (Phase 0), not the
plateau.
