Question: Where is the vasomotor center located?
A. Cerebral cortex and associated white matter tracts
B. Cerebellar hemispheres and deep nuclei
C. Hypothalamus and limbic autonomic integration centers
D. Medulla oblongata and lower third of the pons
E. Midbrain periaqueductal gray region exclusively
Correct Option: D
Explanation:
Correct (D): The vasomotor center is located in the medulla oblongata with contributions from the lower pons, regulating sympathetic vasoconstrictor tone.
Incorrect (A): The cerebral cortex modulates higher autonomic function but is not the primary vasomotor center.
Incorrect (B): The cerebellum coordinates movement, not autonomic vascular tone.
Incorrect (C): The hypothalamus integrates autonomic responses but is not the primary vasomotor center.
Incorrect (E): The periaqueductal gray is involved in pain and autonomic modulation, not primary vasomotor control.

Question: What is the most common cause of hypovolemic shock in clinical practice?
A. Acute myocardial infarction of left ventricle
B. Massive hemorrhage (loss of blood)
C. Severe allergic reaction to peanuts
D. Spinal cord injury above T6
E. Severe dehydration from acute gastroenteritis
Correct Option: B
Explanation:
Correct (B): Massive hemorrhage (loss of blood) is the most common cause of hypovolemic shock due to rapid intravascular volume depletion.
Incorrect (A): Acute myocardial infarction of left ventricle causes cardiogenic shock, not hypovolemic shock.
Incorrect (C): Severe allergic reaction to peanuts causes anaphylactic shock via histamine-mediated vasodilation.
Incorrect (D): Spinal cord injury above T6 causes neurogenic shock from loss of sympathetic tone.
Incorrect (E): Severe dehydration from acute gastroenteritis can cause hypovolemic shock but is less common than hemorrhage in clinical practice.

Question: Which type of shock occurs when a severe allergic reaction triggers widespread mast cell degranulation and massive histamine release?
A. Anaphylactic shock
B. Cardiogenic shock

E. Septic shock
Correct Option: A
Explanation:
Correct (A): Anaphylactic shock results from severe allergic reactions causing mast cell degranulation and massive histamine release leading to systemic vasodilation.
Incorrect (B): Cardiogenic shock is due to pump failure, not allergic mediator release.
Incorrect (C): Endotoxic shock is triggered by endotoxins from Gram-negative bacteria.
Incorrect (D): Neurogenic shock results from loss of sympathetic tone, not histamine release.
Incorrect (E): Septic shock is due to systemic infection and cytokine storm, not immediate allergic mechanisms.
