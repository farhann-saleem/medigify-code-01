Question: What event is responsible for producing the second heart sound (S2)?
 A. Closure of atrioventricular valves
 B. Closure of semilunar valves
 C. Opening of mitral valve
 D. Atrial contraction
 E. Opening of aortic valve
 Correct Option: B
 Explanation:
 • Correct (B): S2 is produced by closure of aortic and pulmonary valves at end systole.
 • Incorrect (A): AV valve closure produces S1.
 • Incorrect (C): Mitral valve opening is silent in normal physiology.
 • Incorrect (D): Atrial contraction produces the P wave, not heart sounds.
 • Incorrect (E): Valve opening is not associated with S2 sound.
