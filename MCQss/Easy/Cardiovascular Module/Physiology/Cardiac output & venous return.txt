Question: How is circulatory shock defined?
 A. Emotional response to trauma
 B. Generalized tissue hypoperfusion leading to cellular hypoxia
 C. Severe hypertension above 180/120 mmHg
 D. Primary electrical failure of the heart
 E. Excessive oxygen delivery to tissues
 Correct Option: B
 Explanation:
 • Correct (B): Shock is systemic inadequate perfusion causing cellular hypoxia and organ dysfunction.
 • Incorrect (A): Emotional shock is non-medical usage.
 • Incorrect (C): Hypertension is opposite physiological state.
 • Incorrect (D): Electrical failure is cardiac arrest, not shock.
 • Incorrect (E): Shock is oxygen deficit, not excess delivery.

Question: The "Frank-Starling Law" is fundamentally based on which cellular property?

Explanation:

Incorrect (B): Mitochondrial density does not explain the acute length-tension relationship.
Incorrect (C): Sodium channel inactivation is unrelated to Starling's law.

Frank-Starling response.
