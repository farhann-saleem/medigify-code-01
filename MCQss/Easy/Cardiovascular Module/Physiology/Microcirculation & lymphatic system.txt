Question: Left-sided heart failure primarily leads to fluid accumulation and congestion in which organ system?
 A. Brain ventricular system
 B. Hepatic portal venous system
 C. Pulmonary circulation
 D. Systemic venous circulation
 E. Peritoneal cavity
 Correct Option: C
 Explanation:
 • Correct (C): Left ventricular failure causes blood to back up into pulmonary veins, increasing pulmonary capillary hydrostatic pressure and pulmonary edema.
 • Incorrect (A): Brain ventricular system is unrelated to cardiac pump failure congestion.
 • Incorrect (B): Hepatic portal congestion is more associated with right-sided heart failure.
 • Incorrect (D): Systemic venous congestion is a feature of right-sided failure.
 • Incorrect (E): Peritoneal ascites results mainly from systemic venous pressure elevation.

Question: What is the dominant mechanism for the exchange of nutrients, gases, and water across the capillary membrane between plasma and interstitium?
A. Active vesicular transport across endothelial cells
B. Bulk flow filtration through intercellular clefts
C. Primary active transport via ATP-dependent pumps
D. Simple passive diffusion down concentration gradients
E. Facilitated diffusion via carrier-mediated endothelial channels
Correct Option: D
Explanation:
Correct (D): Diffusion is the main mechanism for gases and nutrients across capillaries.
Incorrect (A): Vesicular transport is minor for large molecules only.
Incorrect (B): Bulk flow mainly handles fluid, not solute exchange.
Incorrect (C): No ATP-dependent pumping dominates capillary exchange.
Incorrect (E): Carrier-mediated transport is not primary in capillaries.

Question: Which Starling force is primarily generated by dissolved plasma proteins (mainly albumin) that cannot pass through the capillary pores?
A. Capillary hydrostatic pressure
B. Interstitial hydrostatic pressure
C. Interstitial oncotic pressure
D. Plasma colloid osmotic pressure
E. Capillary filtration coefficient
Correct Option: D
Explanation:
Correct (D): Plasma proteins create plasma colloid osmotic pressure pulling fluid into capillaries.
Incorrect (A): Hydrostatic pressure pushes fluid outward.
Incorrect (B): Interstitial pressure is tissue fluid pressure.
Incorrect (C): Refers to interstitial proteins, not plasma.
Incorrect (E): Filtration coefficient is permeability × surface area, not a force.

Question: What specialized anatomical structures prevent backflow and enable the lymphatic system to function as an active fluid pump?
A. Aquaporin water channels in lymphatic endothelium
B. Fenestrated endothelial pores in lymphatic capillaries
C. One-way valves and contractile lymphangions
D. Precapillary sphincters regulating lymph entry
E. Tight junctional complexes preventing leakage
Correct Option: C
Explanation:
Correct (C): Valves and lymphangions ensure unidirectional lymph propulsion.
Incorrect (A): Aquaporins mediate water transport, not pumping.
Incorrect (B): Fenestrations allow entry, not pumping.
Incorrect (D): Precapillary sphincters regulate blood flow, not lymph flow.
Incorrect (E): Tight junctions are not primary lymph pump structures.

Question: Under basal conditions, why does the body avoid progressive swelling despite continuous capillary filtration?
A. Fluid evaporates through epidermal diffusion
B. Renal tubules directly drain interstitial fluid
C. Lymphatics return filtered fluid to venous circulation
D. Capillaries continuously reabsorb all filtered fluid
E. Erythrocytes absorb excess interstitial water
Correct Option: C
Explanation:
Correct (C): Lymphatic drainage returns excess filtered fluid to circulation.
Incorrect (A): Evaporation is negligible in fluid balance.
Incorrect (B): Kidneys do not directly drain interstitium.
Incorrect (D): Not all fluid is reabsorbed at capillary level.
Incorrect (E): RBCs do not regulate interstitial fluid volume.
