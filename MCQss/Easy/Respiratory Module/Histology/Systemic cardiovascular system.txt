Question: The type of vessels present in tissues where there is extensive molecular exchange with blood are:
A. continuous capillaries
B. fenestrated capillaries
C. meta-arterioles
D. postcapillary venules
E. sinusoidal capillaries
Correct Option: B
Explanation:
Correct (B): Fenestrated capillaries contain endothelial pores that permit high-volume molecular exchange in organs like the kidneys and endocrine glands.
Incorrect (A): Continuous capillaries feature tight junctions and a continuous endothelium that restricts extensive molecular exchange to regulated transcytosis.
Incorrect (C): Meta-arterioles are transitional vascular channels containing smooth muscle sphincters that regulate capillary bed perfusion rather than serving as primary exchange sites.
Incorrect (D): Postcapillary venules possess an endothelium specialized for leukocyte emigration and fluid fluid-shift responsiveness, not rapid molecular exchange.
Incorrect (E): Sinusoidal capillaries permit whole-cell and macromolecular transit through large open gaps, going beyond standard molecular exchange.

Question: Which of the following layers in the wall of a medium-sized artery contains smooth muscle:
A. internal elastic membrane
B. subendothelial layer
C. tunica adventitia
D. tunica intima
E. tunica media
Correct Option: E
Explanation:
Correct (E): The tunica media of medium-sized arteries consists of multiple concentric layers of smooth muscle cells responsible for vasoconstriction and vasodilation.
Incorrect (A): The internal elastic membrane is a fenestrated sheet composed entirely of elastic fibers, completely lacking smooth muscle cells.
Incorrect (B): The subendothelial layer is a structural subcomponent of the tunica intima consisting of loose connective tissue and occasional fibroblasts.
Incorrect (C): The tunica adventitia is the outer connective tissue sheath primarily composed of fibroblasts, collagen fibers, and elastic fibers.
Incorrect (D): The tunica intima is the innermost layer lined by a simple squamous endothelium and its underlying subendothelial connective tissue.

Question: Which layer is absent in tunica intima of an artery:
A. basement membrane
B. endothelium
C. external elastic lamina
D. internal elastic lamina
E. subendothelial connective tissue
Correct Option: C
Explanation:
Correct (C): The external elastic lamina forms the boundary between the tunica media and tunica adventitia, meaning it is entirely absent in tunica intima of an artery.
Incorrect (A): A thin basement membrane is a native component supporting the simple squamous endothelial layer within the tunica intima.
Incorrect (B): The endothelium is the innermost single layer of simple squamous cells that directly lines the lumen of the tunica intima.
Incorrect (D): The internal elastic lamina forms the outermost boundary zone of the tunica intima, structurally demarcating it from the tunica media.
Incorrect (E): The subendothelial connective tissue is a loose connective tissue layer situated directly beneath the endothelial basement membrane within the intima.

Question: Purkinje fibers:

Correct Option: B
Explanation:

Incorrect (A): They are highly specialized modifications of cardiac muscle tissue and are never found in smooth and cardiac muscle mixed or within smooth muscle walls.

Incorrect (D): They are structurally modified cardiomyocytes specialized for rapid electrical conduction and do not serve functionally as connective tissue.

Question: The distribution of blood to different regions and organs of the body is performed by the:
A. arterioles
B. capillaries
C. elastic arteries
D. muscular arteries
E. postcapillary venules
Correct Option: D
Explanation:
Correct (D): Muscular arteries possess a prominent smooth muscle media that changes luminal diameter to regulate the distribution of blood to different regions and organs of the body.
Incorrect (A): Arterioles are microvascular resistance vessels that control systemic blood pressure and local perfusion into individual capillary beds, not macro-distribution to organs.
Incorrect (B): Capillaries function purely as the microvascular site for metabolic exchange between blood and interstitial fluid, lacking any macro-distribution capacity.
Incorrect (C): Elastic arteries serve as highly compliant conduit vessels that cushion high systolic pressures near the heart, rather than distributing blood to specific organs.
Incorrect (E): Postcapillary venules serve as the primary site for metabolic fluid return and inflammatory leukocyte emigration, acting downstream of the distribution network.

Incorrect (D): The tunica media forms the bulk of the vascular wall in muscular arteries, making the statement that the tunica adventitia is its thickest coat incorrect.

Question: Which vessel contain equal number of elastic and muscle fibers:
A. capillaries
B. large arteries
C. medium size arteries
D. postcapillary venules
E. small arterioles
Correct Option: B
Explanation:
Correct (B): Large arteries (elastic/conduit arteries like the aorta) feature a tunica media where fenestrated elastic lamellae alternate with smooth muscle layers in roughly equal proportions.
Incorrect (A): Capillaries are simple endothelial tubes supported by a basal lamina, meaning they contain zero elastic and muscle fibers.
Incorrect (C): Medium size arteries (muscular arteries) possess a tunica media that is predominantly composed of smooth muscle cells, with minimal elastic fibers.
Incorrect (D): Postcapillary venules have thin walls composed of endothelium and pericytes, entirely lacking an alternating layer of elastic and muscle fibers.
Incorrect (E): Small arterioles have walls consisting almost entirely of one or two circular layers of smooth muscle cells, with negligible elastic fibers.

Question: Each of the following statements concerning cardiac muscle cell is true except:

B. they are abundant in the walls of the ventricles.
C. they are abundant in the walls of the atria.

E. they have less sarcoplasmic reticulum than skeletal muscle cells.
Correct Option: A
Explanation:
Correct (A): This statement is false because cardiac muscle cells form the myocardium, whereas the epicardium is the outer visceral layer composed of connective tissue and adipose tissue.
Incorrect (B): It is true that they are abundant in the walls of the ventricles, forming the thick muscular pumping chambers of the heart.
Incorrect (C): It is true that they are abundant in the walls of the atria, comprising the thinner myocardial walls of the receiving chambers.
Incorrect (D): It is true that they contain T tubules located at the Z-lines to facilitate synchronous intracellular calcium release during contraction.
Incorrect (E): It is true that they have less sarcoplasmic reticulum than skeletal muscle cells, making them partly dependent on extracellular calcium ions.

Question: Fenestrated capillaries are found in:
A. bone marrow
B. endocrine glands
C. liver
D. lungs
E. spleen
Correct Option: B
Explanation:
Correct (B): Fenestrated capillaries are found in endocrine glands to permit the rapid, unobstructed entry of secreted hormones into the circulating bloodstream.
Incorrect (A): Bone marrow contains sinusoidal capillaries with large intercellular gaps to accommodate the passage of newly formed blood cells into circulation.
Incorrect (C): The liver is vascularized by discontinuous sinusoidal capillaries that allow direct contact between hepatocytes and blood plasma.
Incorrect (D): Lungs feature continuous capillaries with tight junctions to maintain a strict air-blood barrier and prevent fluid leakage into alveoli.
Incorrect (E): The spleen utilizes highly specialized discontinuous venous sinusoids to filter aging red blood cells out of the circulation.
