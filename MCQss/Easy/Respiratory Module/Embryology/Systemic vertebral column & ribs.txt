Question: Ribs are formed from which of the following:
A. ectodermal invagination
B. endodermal migration
C. lateral plate mesoderm
D. sclerotome portion of paraxial mesoderm
E. somatopleuric portion of lateral plate mesoderm
Correct Option: D
Explanation:
* Correct (D): The ribs develop from the sclerotome portion of paraxial mesoderm, which migrates into the body wall during embryonic development.
* Incorrect (A): Ectodermal invagination gives rise to structures like the neural tube and various glands, not the bony thoracic skeleton.
* Incorrect (B): Endodermal migration forms the epithelial linings of the respiratory and gastrointestinal tracts, rather than musculoskeletal components.
* Incorrect (C): Lateral plate mesoderm forms the dermis, body wall parietes, and limb skeleton, but not the ribs.
* Incorrect (E): The somatopleuric portion of lateral plate mesoderm contributes to the sternum and limb bones, rather than the ribs.
