Question 1
 Question: A 12-year-old girl, a known patient with asthma, presents to the emergency room with cough, dyspnea, and wheezing after visiting a riding stable. Which of the following is the most appropriate drug to rapidly reverse her bronchoconstriction?
 A. Inhaled albuterol
  B. Inhaled beclomethasone
  C. Inhaled fluticasone
  D. Inhaled salmeterol
  E. Intravenous propranolol
 Correct Option: A
 Explanation:
 •	Correct (A): Inhaled albuterol is a short-acting β2-agonist that rapidly produces bronchodilation and is the drug of choice for acute bronchospasm.
 •	Incorrect (B): Inhaled beclomethasone is an inhaled corticosteroid used for long-term control, not immediate relief.
 •	Incorrect (C): Inhaled fluticasone has anti-inflammatory effects but does not rapidly reverse bronchoconstriction.
 •	Incorrect (D): Inhaled salmeterol is a long-acting β2-agonist with a slower onset and is not used for acute attacks.
 •	Incorrect (E): Intravenous propranolol is a nonselective β-blocker that can worsen bronchospasm.
 Tags: Easy, Pharmacology, Respiratory Pathology, Anti-asthmatics

Question 2
 Question: Theophylline is believed to benefit patients with asthma by exerting all of the following actions except:
 A. Augmentation of diaphragmatic contractility
  B. Bronchodilatation
  C. Inhibition of antigen-antibody reaction
  D. Inhibition of inflammatory mediator release
  E. Phosphodiesterase inhibition
 Correct Option: C
 Explanation:
 •	Correct (C): Theophylline does not inhibit the antigen-antibody reaction; its effects are mainly bronchodilation and modulation of inflammation.
 •	Incorrect (A): Augmentation of diaphragmatic contractility improves respiratory muscle performance.
 •	Incorrect (B): Bronchodilatation is a major therapeutic effect of theophylline.
 •	Incorrect (D): Inhibition of inflammatory mediator release contributes to its antiasthmatic action.
 •	Incorrect (E): Phosphodiesterase inhibition increases intracellular cAMP and promotes bronchodilation.

Question 3
 Question: Which of the following is a leukotriene receptor antagonist used in the management of asthma?
 A. Albuterol
  B. Aminophylline
  C. Ipratropium
  D. Montelukast

 Correct Option: D
 Explanation:

 •	Incorrect (B): Aminophylline is a methylxanthine bronchodilator.
 •	Incorrect (C): Ipratropium is an antimuscarinic bronchodilator.
 •	Incorrect (E): Theophylline acts mainly through phosphodiesterase inhibition and adenosine receptor antagonism.
