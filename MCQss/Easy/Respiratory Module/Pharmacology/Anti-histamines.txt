Question 2: Which antihistamine drug should not be prescribed to individuals working in jobs in which wakefulness is critical:
  A. cimetidine
  B. diphenhydramine
  C. famotidine
  D. nizatidine
  E. ranitidine
  Correct Option: B
  Explanation:
 •	Correct (B): diphenhydramine causes marked CNS sedation due to first-generation H1 blockade.
 •	Incorrect (A): cimetidine is an H2 blocker, not a sedating H1 antihistamine.
 •	Incorrect (C): famotidine is an H2 receptor antagonist with minimal CNS effects.
 •	Incorrect (D): nizatidine is an H2 blocker and does not impair wakefulness.
 •	Incorrect (E): ranitidine is an H2 blocker and is not sedating like H1 antihistamines.
