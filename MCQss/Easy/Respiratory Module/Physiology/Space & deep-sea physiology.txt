

Question 1:
 At an altitude of about 10,000 feet, the atmospheric PO₂ is approximately?
 A. 100 mmHg
 B. 110 mmHg
 C. 120 mmHg
 D. 130 mmHg
 E. 140 mmHg
 Correct Option: B
 Explanation:
 • Correct (B): 110 mmHg reflects the reduced partial pressure of oxygen at ~10,000 feet altitude.
 • Incorrect (A): 100 mmHg underestimates the actual atmospheric PO₂ at this altitude.
 • Incorrect (C): 120 mmHg is too high for 10,000 feet, closer to lower altitudes.
 • Incorrect (D): 130 mmHg corresponds to near sea-level values, not high altitude.
 • Incorrect (E): 140 mmHg is essentially sea-level atmospheric PO₂, not altitude.

Question 1
 Approximately 4 L of nitrogen is dissolved in body tissues:
 A. at 33 m depth.
 B. at 100 m depth.
 C. at 200 m depth.
 D. at sea level.
 E. at very high altitude.
 Correct Option: D
 Explanation:
 • Correct (D): At sea level, normal atmospheric pressure maintains approximately 4 L of nitrogen dissolved in body tissues at equilibrium.
 • Incorrect (A): At 33 m depth, increased pressure causes substantially more nitrogen to dissolve in tissues.
 • Incorrect (B): At 100 m depth, nitrogen dissolution is far greater than the amount present at sea level.
 • Incorrect (C): At 200 m depth, extremely high pressure results in much higher tissue nitrogen content.
 • Incorrect (E): At very high altitude, reduced atmospheric pressure decreases dissolved nitrogen in tissues.

Question 3
 Most common symptom of decompression sickness:
 A. bends.
 B. chokes.
 C. dizziness.
 D. headache.
 E. nausea.
 Correct Option: A
 Explanation:
 • Correct (A): The bends, characterized by joint and muscle pain, is the most common manifestation of decompression sickness.
 • Incorrect (B): Chokes is a less common and more severe pulmonary manifestation.
 • Incorrect (C): Dizziness may occur but is not the most frequent symptom.
 • Incorrect (D): Headache can be present but is less common than the bends.
 • Incorrect (E): Nausea may accompany decompression sickness but is not its predominant presentation.

Question 4
 85–90% people with decompression sickness have:
 A. chokes.
 B. dizziness.
 C. joint pain.
 D. nausea.
 E. skin itching.
 Correct Option: C
 Explanation:
 • Correct (C): Joint pain is the classic manifestation of the bends and occurs in approximately 85–90% of decompression sickness cases.
 • Incorrect (A): Chokes occurs in a minority of patients with pulmonary involvement.
 • Incorrect (B): Dizziness is less frequent and usually reflects vestibular involvement.
 • Incorrect (D): Nausea is a nonspecific symptom and occurs less commonly.
 • Incorrect (E): Skin itching may occur due to cutaneous nitrogen bubbles but is not present in most cases.
