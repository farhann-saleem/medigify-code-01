Question: Which statement is true regarding the bronchial circulation?
A. It arises from the aorta and enters the lungs at the hilum
B. It causes venous mixing of blood
C. It is regulated only by the sympathetic nervous system
D. It is responsible for supplying oxygenated blood to the rest of the body
E. It is responsible for the entire gas exchange process of the respiratory system
Correct Option: A
Explanation:
​Correct (A): The bronchial arteries originate as systemic branches of the thoracic aorta and enter the lung parenchyma via the hilum to supply the conducting airways.
​Incorrect (B): The bronchial circulation itself does not cause venous mixing, though its anatomical shunting into the pulmonary veins subsequently results in venous mixing of blood.
​Incorrect (C): It is regulated by both the sympathetic nervous system and local metabolic factors, rather than being regulated only by the sympathetic nervous system.
​Incorrect (D): It supplies the lung's own non-respiratory tissues, whereas the left ventricle and systemic arterial system are responsible for supplying oxygenated blood to the rest of the body.
​Incorrect (E): The pulmonary circulation, not the bronchial circulation, is responsible for the entire gas exchange process of the respiratory system.
