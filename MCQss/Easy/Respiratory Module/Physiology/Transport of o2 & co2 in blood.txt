Question: 97% of oxygen from lungs to tissues is carried in chemical combination with:
A. carbon dioxide
B. hemoglobin
C. hydrogen ions
D. myoglobin
E. plasma proteins
Correct Option: B
Explanation:
 * Correct (B): Hemoglobin inside erythrocytes binds oxygen reversibly to transport 97% of it to tissues, while the remaining 3% is dissolved in plasma.
 * Incorrect (A): Carbon dioxide is a waste product of cellular respiration and does not carry oxygen.
 * Incorrect (C): Hydrogen ions lower blood pH and promote oxygen unloading rather than binding or transporting it.
 * Incorrect (D): Myoglobin functions as an oxygen storage reservoir within muscle tissue, not as a systemic transporter from the lungs.
 * Incorrect (E): Plasma proteins account for only a negligible fraction of oxygen binding, transport, and buffering compared to hemoglobin.

Question: Partial pressure at which hemoglobin is 50% saturated with oxygen is:
A. 6 mmHg
B. 15 mmHg
C. 26 mmHg
D. 35 mmHg
E. 40 mmHg
Correct Option: C
Explanation:
 * Correct (C): P_{50} represents the oxygen partial pressure at which hemoglobin is 50% saturated, which normally equals 26 mmHg under standard physiological conditions.
 * Incorrect (A): A value of 6 mmHg is critically low and does not correlate with standard human hemoglobin P_{50} parameters.
 * Incorrect (B): A value of 15 mmHg reflects an abnormally high oxygen affinity, characteristic of specialized pigment systems or extreme left shifts.
 * Incorrect (C): A value of 35 mmHg indicates a marked rightward shift, reducing hemoglobin's affinity for oxygen well beyond the physiological norm.
 * Incorrect (E): A value of 40 mmHg corresponds to the standard partial pressure of oxygen in mixed venous blood, not the P_{50}.

Question: Carbon dioxide is transported from tissues to lungs in the blood mainly as:
A. bicarbonate in the plasma
B. carbaminohemoglobin in red blood cells
C. carbaminoproteins in the plasma
D. dissolved gas in the plasma
E. dissolved gas in red blood cells
Correct Option: A
Explanation:
 * Correct (A): Roughly 70% of all carbon dioxide is converted into bicarbonate ions within erythrocytes and then carried back to the lungs dissolved in the plasma.
 * Incorrect (B): Carbaminohemoglobin in red blood cells accounts for only about 23% of total systemic carbon dioxide transport.
 * Incorrect (C): Carbaminoproteins in the plasma represent a minor transport pool, binding only a tiny percentage of the total waste carbon dioxide.
 * Incorrect (D): Dissolved gas in the plasma makes up only about 7% of the total carbon dioxide carried in the systemic circulation.
 * Incorrect (E): Dissolved gas in red blood cells constitutes a negligible fraction of the overall transport, as most gas is rapidly hydrated or bound.

Question: Reaction between water and carbon dioxide within red blood cells is catalyzed by:
A. carbonic anhydrase
B. catalase
C. collagenase
D. peroxidase
E. synthetase
Correct Option: A
Explanation:
 * Correct (A): Carbonic anhydrase accelerates the reversible hydration of carbon dioxide into carbonic acid inside erythrocytes by several thousand-fold.
 * Incorrect (B): Catalase is an antioxidant enzyme responsible for breaking down toxic hydrogen peroxide into water and oxygen.
 * Incorrect (C): Collagenase is a proteolytic enzyme that breaks down collagen peptide bonds within the extracellular matrix.
 * Incorrect (D): Peroxidase neutralizes organic peroxides and is not involved in the reversible hydration of gaseous carbon dioxide.
 * Incorrect (E): Synthetase enzymes utilize ATP condensation energy to link molecules together, which is completely unrelated to gas hydration reactions.
