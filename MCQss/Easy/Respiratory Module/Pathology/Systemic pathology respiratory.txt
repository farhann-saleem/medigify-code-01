 Question: Type of emphysema commonly associated with cigarette smoking:
  A. centriacinar.
  B. distal acinar.
  C. irregular acinar.
  D. panacinar.
  E. paraseptal acinar.
 Correct Option: A
 Explanation:
 •	Correct (A): Centriacinar emphysema predominantly affects respiratory bronchioles in the upper lobes and is strongly associated with cigarette smoking.
 •	Incorrect (B): Panacinar emphysema involves the entire acinus and is classically associated with α1-antitrypsin deficiency.
 •	Incorrect (C): Distal acinar emphysema mainly affects distal acini near pleura and is linked to spontaneous pneumothorax.
 •	Incorrect (D): Panacinar emphysema is not the emphysema type most commonly caused by cigarette smoking.
 •	Incorrect (E): Paraseptal acinar emphysema affects peripheral alveoli adjacent to septa rather than central acini.
 Tags: Easy, Pathology, Respiratory Module, Systemic Pathology: Respiratory

 Question: Which of the following does not cause airway narrowing in an asthma attack:
  A. airway edema.
  B. bronchospasm.
  C. destruction of airways.
  D. mucus hypersecretion.
  E. smooth muscle hypertrophy.
 Correct Option: C
 Explanation:
 •	Correct (C): Destruction of airways is not a mechanism of acute airway narrowing in asthma.
 •	Incorrect (A): Airway edema narrows the bronchial lumen during asthma attacks.
 •	Incorrect (B): Bronchospasm is a major cause of reversible airflow obstruction.
 •	Incorrect (D): Mucus hypersecretion contributes to luminal obstruction.
 •	Incorrect (E): Smooth muscle hypertrophy promotes airway narrowing and hyperresponsiveness.

 Question: Honeycomb appearance of lung is a characteristic finding of:
  A. anthracosis.
  B. idiopathic pulmonary fibrosis.
  C. sarcoidosis.
  D. silicosis.
  E. talcosis.
 Correct Option: B
 Explanation:
 •	Correct (B): Idiopathic pulmonary fibrosis characteristically produces end-stage honeycomb lung due to extensive interstitial fibrosis.
 •	Incorrect (A): Anthracosis causes carbon pigment deposition without honeycomb change.
 •	Incorrect (C): Sarcoidosis is characterized by noncaseating granulomas rather than classic honeycomb lung.
 •	Incorrect (D): Silicosis produces silicotic nodules and upper-lobe fibrosis.
 •	Incorrect (E): Talcosis causes foreign-body granulomatous reactions rather than honeycomb lung.

 Correct Option: A
 Explanation:

 •	Incorrect (C): Obstructive diseases do not characteristically normalize the ratio.
 •	Incorrect (D): The ratio shows a consistent tendency to decrease in obstruction.
 •	Incorrect (E): A normal ratio does not define classic obstructive physiology.

  B. leukotrienes.
  C. neutrophils.
  D. prostaglandins.
  E. thromboxane A2.
 Correct Option: A
 Explanation:
 •	Correct (A): Histamine is an immediate preformed mediator released during the early asthmatic response.
 •	Incorrect (B): Leukotrienes are important but are synthesized after activation rather than released first.
 •	Incorrect (C): Neutrophils are inflammatory cells, not mediators.
 •	Incorrect (D): Prostaglandins are generated later from arachidonic acid metabolism.
 •	Incorrect (E): Thromboxane A2 is not the principal initial mediator of asthma.

 Question: Curschmann spirals are seen in sputum in:
  A. bronchial asthma.
  B. bronchiectasis.
  C. bronchopneumonia.
  D. tuberculosis.
  E. viral bronchiolitis.
 Correct Option: A

 •	Incorrect (B): Bronchiectasis typically produces purulent sputum without Curschmann spirals.
 •	Incorrect (C): Bronchopneumonia is characterized by neutrophilic exudates.
 •	Incorrect (D): Tuberculosis is associated with granulomatous inflammation.
 •	Incorrect (E): Viral bronchiolitis does not classically produce Curschmann spirals.

 Question: What is the common cause of panacinar emphysema?
  A. Alcoholism.
  B. Alpha-1 antitrypsin deficiency.
  C. Cigarette smoking.
  D. Coal dust exposure.
  E. Silica dust exposure.
  Correct Option: B
 Explanation:
 •	Correct (B): Alpha-1 antitrypsin deficiency causes unchecked protease activity leading to diffuse panacinar emphysema, especially in the lower lobes.
 •	Incorrect (A): Alcoholism is not a recognized primary cause of panacinar emphysema.
 •	Incorrect (C): Cigarette smoking is most strongly associated with centriacinar emphysema rather than panacinar emphysema.
 •	Incorrect (D): Coal dust exposure is linked to coal workers’ pneumoconiosis, not panacinar emphysema.
 •	Incorrect (E): Silica dust exposure causes silicosis and pulmonary fibrosis rather than panacinar emphysema.
