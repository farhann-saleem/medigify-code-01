
Question: In which type of inhibition do inhibitor and substrate bind reversibly to the active site?
A. Competitive inhibition
B. Irreversible inhibition
C. Mixed inhibition
D. Non-competitive inhibition
E. Uncompetitive inhibition
Correct Option: A
Explanation:
Correct (A): Both compete for active site reversibly
Incorrect (B): Irreversible inhibition permanently inactivates enzyme
Incorrect (C): Mixed binds enzyme and ES complex differently
Incorrect (D): Non-competitive binds allosteric site
Incorrect (E): Uncompetitive binds only ES complex
Tags: Easy, Biochemistry, Foundation, Enzyme Kinetics & Regulation

Question: The main function of enzyme is to:
A. change equilibrium constant
B. decrease activation energy
C. increase activation energy
D. increase free energy change of reaction
E. increase product energy level
Correct Option: B
Explanation:
Correct (B): Enzymes accelerate biochemical reactions by decreasing activation energy without altering reaction thermodynamics.
Incorrect (A): Enzymes do not change the equilibrium constant of a reaction.
Incorrect (C): Increasing activation energy would slow rather than accelerate the reaction.
Incorrect (D): Enzymes do not alter the free energy change of reaction.
Incorrect (E): Enzymes do not increase product energy level; they only facilitate transition state formation.
Tags: Easy, Biochemistry, Foundation, Enzyme Kinetics & Regulation

Question: Enzymes catalyze reaction by:
A. alter equilibrium constant
B. decrease activation energy
C. decrease enzyme concentration
D. increase activation energy
E. increase Km
Correct Option: B
Explanation:
Correct (B): Enzymes catalyze reactions by lowering activation energy, thereby increasing reaction rate.
Incorrect (A): Enzymes do not alter equilibrium constant because they affect forward and reverse reactions equally.
Incorrect (C): Decreasing enzyme concentration reduces reaction velocity.
Incorrect (D): Increasing activation energy would inhibit the reaction.
Incorrect (E): Increasing Km reflects lower substrate affinity, not the catalytic mechanism itself.
Tags: Easy, Biochemistry, Foundation, Enzyme Kinetics & Regulation

Question: In competitive inhibition, the inhibitor:

B. binds enzyme active site reversibly competing with substrate
C. binds enzyme-substrate complex
D. binds substrate
E. irreversibly binds enzyme

Explanation:

Incorrect (A): Allosteric binding is characteristic of non-competitive inhibition.
Incorrect (C): Binding to enzyme-substrate complex is typical of uncompetitive inhibition.
Incorrect (D): Competitive inhibitors interact with the enzyme rather than substrate itself.
Incorrect (E): Competitive inhibition is usually reversible, not irreversible.

C. four times
D. five times
E. ten times
Correct Option: A
Explanation:

Incorrect (B): Threefold increase exceeds the typical Q10 effect for enzymes.
Incorrect (C): Fourfold increase is not the standard biochemical rule.
Incorrect (D): Fivefold increase would occur only under exceptional conditions.
Incorrect (E): Tenfold increase is far beyond the expected temperature effect.
