Question: Plasma membrane carries out all of the following functions, EXCEPT:
A. cell-cell recognition.
B. cell receptors.
C. energy generation.
D. pinocytosis.
E. transport mechanisms.
Correct Option: C
Explanation:
Correct (C): Energy generation through oxidative phosphorylation occurs mainly in mitochondria, not in the plasma membrane.
Incorrect (A): Cell-cell recognition is mediated by membrane glycoproteins and glycolipids.
Incorrect (B): Cell receptors are integral membrane proteins involved in signal transduction.
Incorrect (D): Pinocytosis is a membrane-dependent process for the uptake of extracellular fluid.
Incorrect (E): Transport mechanisms such as channels and carriers are major plasma membrane functions.
Tags: Easy, Biochemistry, Foundation, Cell Structure & Organelles

Question: The major functions of the Golgi apparatus are:
A. detoxification of substances and removal of waste products.
B. diffusion and osmosis.
C. packaging, sorting, and modification of new molecules.
D. synthesis of new proteins for the cytoplasm.
E. synthesis of nucleic acids.
Correct Option: C
Explanation:
Correct (C): The Golgi apparatus modifies, sorts, and packages proteins and lipids for secretion or intracellular use.
Incorrect (A): Detoxification is mainly performed by smooth endoplasmic reticulum and peroxisomes.
Incorrect (B): Diffusion and osmosis are membrane transport processes, not Golgi functions.
Incorrect (D): Protein synthesis occurs on ribosomes, especially rough endoplasmic reticulum.
Incorrect (E): Nucleic acid synthesis primarily occurs in the nucleus.
Tags: Easy, Biochemistry, Foundation, Cell Structure & Organelles

Question: Phospholipids are important cell membrane components because they:
A. are amphipathic molecules.
B. can form a bilayer in oil-water systems.
C. contains both polar and non-polar groups.
D. participates in membrane structure formation.
E. stabilize membrane fluidity.
Correct Option: C
Explanation:
Correct (C): Phospholipids contain both polar and non-polar groups, allowing formation of selectively permeable membrane bilayers.
Incorrect (A): Amphipathic nature describes phospholipids but does not specifically explain membrane structural behavior as directly as the presence of polar and non-polar groups.
Incorrect (B): Bilayer formation is a consequence of their dual chemical nature rather than the primary defining feature asked for.
Incorrect (D): Participation in membrane structure formation is a general effect, not the fundamental property responsible.
Incorrect (E): Membrane fluidity is mainly stabilized by cholesterol rather than phospholipids alone.
Tags: Easy, Biochemistry, Foundation, Cell Structure & Organelles

Question: Which of the following is NOT related to membranes:
A. cholesterol esters.
B. membrane impermeability to polar substances.
C. membrane integrity.
D. membrane oligosaccharides.
E. membrane phospholipids.
Correct Option: A
Explanation:
Correct (A): Cholesterol esters are storage forms of cholesterol and are not structural membrane components.
Incorrect (B): Membrane impermeability to polar substances is a key property of lipid bilayers.
Incorrect (C): Membrane integrity depends on proper lipid and protein organization.
Incorrect (D): Membrane oligosaccharides are involved in recognition and signaling functions.
Incorrect (E): Membrane phospholipids form the basic structural framework of biological membranes.
Tags: Easy, Biochemistry, Foundation, Cell Structure & Organelles

Question: Na+ - K+ ATPase is the marker enzyme of:
A. cytosol.
B. mitochondria.
C. nucleus.
D. plasma membrane.
E. ribosome.
Correct Option: D
Explanation:
Correct (D): Na+ - K+ ATPase is an integral plasma membrane enzyme responsible for maintaining ionic gradients.
Incorrect (A): Cytosol contains soluble metabolic enzymes, not membrane ATPases.
Incorrect (B): Mitochondria contain enzymes of oxidative phosphorylation rather than Na+ - K+ ATPase.
Incorrect (C): The nucleus contains enzymes related to DNA replication and transcription.
Incorrect (E): Ribosomes are involved in protein synthesis and lack ATPase transport enzymes.
Tags: Easy, Biochemistry, Foundation, Cell Structure & Organelles

Question: Which of the following statements is NOT correct for biological membranes:
A. free movement of proteins across the membrane.
B. prevention of free diffusion of ionic solutes.
C. release of proteins when damaged.
D. signal reception and transmission.
E. site for biochemical reactions.
Correct Option: A
Explanation:
Correct (A): Proteins in biological membranes have restricted lateral mobility and do not move freely across the membrane bilayer.
Incorrect (B): Biological membranes prevent free diffusion of ionic solutes because of their hydrophobic core.
Incorrect (C): Membrane damage can cause leakage and release of intracellular proteins.
Incorrect (D): Membranes contain receptors involved in signal reception and transmission.
Incorrect (E): Many biochemical reactions occur on membrane surfaces through membrane-bound enzymes.
Tags: Easy, Biochemistry, Foundation, Cell Structure & Organelles

Question: Active transport differs from passive transport in that:
A. It depends primarily on diffusion and osmosis.
B. It primarily depends on diffusion.
C. It requires a carrier but no energy.
D. It requires energy but no transport carrier.
E. It requires energy, usually in the form of ATP.
Correct Option: E
Explanation:
Correct (E): Active transport requires energ,y usually in the form of ATP, to move substances against concentration gradients.
Incorrect (A): Diffusion and osmosis are passive processes that do not require metabolic energy.
Incorrect (B): Active transport is not driven primarily by diffusion.
Incorrect (C): Carrier-mediated transport without energy describes facilitated diffusion.
Incorrect (D): Most active transport mechanisms require both energy and transport carriers.
Tags: Easy, Biochemistry, Foundation, Cell Structure & Organelles

Question: Protein synthesis in the cell occurs at the:
A. endoplasmic reticulum
B. golgi apparatus
C. lysosome
D. mitochondria
E. ribosomes
Correct Option: E

Incorrect (A): Rough ER assists but does not perform translation itself.
Incorrect (B): Golgi modifies and packages proteins.
Incorrect (C): Lysosomes perform degradation.
Incorrect (D): Mitochondria generate ATP, not protein synthesis machinery.
