Question: In sickle cell anaemia glutamic acid is replaced by:
 A. aspartic acid
 B. histidine
 C. lysine
 D. valine
 E. arginine
 Correct Option: D
 Explanation:
 Correct (D): Valine substitution creates hydrophobic patch causing HbS polymerization
 Incorrect (A): Aspartic acid is still acidic, does not cause sickling
 Incorrect (B): Histidine change is unrelated to HbS mutation
 Incorrect (C): Lysine is basic, but not involved in HbS mutation
 Incorrect (E): Arginine substitution is not seen in sickle cell disease
