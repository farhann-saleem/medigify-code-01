
Question: Which of the following vitamin is a component of Coenzyme A?
A. Folic acid
B. Nicotinic acid
C. Pantothenic acid
D. Pyridoxine
E. Riboflavin
Correct Option: C
Explanation:
Correct (C): Pantothenic acid (B5) is a structural part of CoA
Incorrect (A): Folic acid forms tetrahydrofolate, not CoA
Incorrect (B): Nicotinic acid forms NAD/NADP
Incorrect (D): Pyridoxine forms PLP for amino acid metabolism
Incorrect (E): Riboflavin forms FAD/FMN

Question: A coenzyme required in the transfer of single carbon moieties is:
A. biotin
B. coenzyme A
C. lipoic acid
D. pyridoxal phosphate
E. tetrahydrofolate
Correct Option: E
Explanation:
Correct (E): Tetrahydrofolate transfers single carbon moieties in nucleotide and amino acid metabolism.
Incorrect (A): Biotin functions mainly in carboxylation reactions involving CO₂ transfer.
Incorrect (B): Coenzyme A transfers acyl groups rather than single carbon moieties.
Incorrect (C): Lipoic acid participates in oxidative decarboxylation reactions.
Incorrect (D): Pyridoxal phosphate is primarily involved in amino acid transamination.

Question: All aminotransferases require the coenzyme:
A. biotin
B. FAD
C. NAD
D. pyridoxal phosphate
E. thiamine pyrophosphate
Correct Option: D
Explanation:
Correct (D): Pyridoxal phosphate, derived from vitamin B₆, is the essential coenzyme for aminotransferase reactions.
Incorrect (A): Biotin functions in carboxylation reactions.
Incorrect (B): FAD mainly participates in oxidation-reduction reactions.
Incorrect (C): NAD acts as an electron carrier in dehydrogenase reactions.
Incorrect (E): Thiamine pyrophosphate is involved in oxidative decarboxylation.
Tags: Easy, Biochemistry, Foundation, Vitamins

Question: A coenzyme required in the transfer of single carbon moieties is:
A. biotin
B. folinic acid
C. lipoic acid
D. pyridoxal phosphate
E. tetrahydrofolate
Correct Option: E
Explanation:
Correct (E): Tetrahydrofolate carries and transfers single carbon moieties in metabolic reactions.
Incorrect (A): Biotin transfers carbon dioxide rather than one-carbon units.
Incorrect (B): Folinic acid is a derivative of folate but not the principal active carrier form.
Incorrect (C): Lipoic acid functions in oxidative decarboxylation complexes.
Incorrect (D): Pyridoxal phosphate is involved in amino acid metabolism.
