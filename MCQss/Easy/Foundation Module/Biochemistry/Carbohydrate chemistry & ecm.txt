Question: Conversion of an aldose sugar to a ketose sugar is catalyzed by:
 A. Lyases
 B. Hydrolases
 C. Transferases
 D. Isomerases
 E. Oxidoreductases
 Correct Option: D
 Explanation:
 Correct (D): Isomerases convert aldose ↔ ketose forms
 Incorrect (A): Lyases break bonds
 Incorrect (B): Hydrolases use water
 Incorrect (C): Transferases move groups
 Incorrect (E): Oxidoreductases involve electron transfer
