
Question: Glutathione is:
A. Decapeptide
B. Hexapeptide
C. Octapeptide
D. Pentapeptide
E. Tripeptide
Correct Option: E
Explanation:
Correct (E): Glutathione is a tripeptide composed of glutamate, cysteine, and glycine.
Incorrect (A): Decapeptide contains ten amino acids, unlike glutathione.
Incorrect (B): Hexapeptide contains six amino acids, not three.
Incorrect (C): Octapeptide contains eight amino acids, not three.
Incorrect (D): Pentapeptide contains five amino acids, not three.

Question: Which standard amino acid contains an imino group?
A. Arginine
B. Glycine
C. Lysine
D. Proline
E. Valine
Correct Option: D
Explanation:
Correct (D): Proline contains a secondary amino (imino) group within its cyclic structure.
Incorrect (A): Arginine contains a guanidino group, not an imino group.
Incorrect (B): Glycine has a primary amino group.
Incorrect (C): Lysine contains a primary amino side chain.
Incorrect (E): Valine has a branched aliphatic side chain without an imino group.

A. cysteine
B. cystine
C. homocysteine
D. methionine
E. taurine
Correct Option: D
Explanation:
Correct (D): Methionine contains thioether sulfur as a standard coded amino acid.
Incorrect (A): Cysteine contains thiol but is not the primary dietary essential sulfur amino acid.
Incorrect (B): Cystine is oxidized dimer of cysteine, not a standard amino acid.
Incorrect (C): Homocysteine is metabolic intermediate, not proteinogenic.
Incorrect (E): Taurine is non-protein amino sulfonic acid.

Question: With the exception of glycine, all amino acids found in proteins are:
A. dextrorotary
B. of the D-configuration
C. of the L-configuration
D. optically inactive
E. racemic mixtures
Correct Option: C
Explanation:
Correct (C): Proteinogenic amino acids are exclusively L-isomers.
Incorrect (A): Optical rotation is not universally dextrorotary in L-amino acids.
Incorrect (B): D-configuration is non-proteinogenic in humans.
Incorrect (D): Most amino acids are optically active.
Incorrect (E): Biological systems do not use racemic mixtures in proteins.
