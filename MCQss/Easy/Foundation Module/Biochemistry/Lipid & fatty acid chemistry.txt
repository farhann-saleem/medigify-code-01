Question: Phospholipids are important cell membrane components because they:
 A. are amphipathic molecules.
 B. can form a bilayer in oil-water systems.
 C. contains both polar and non-polar groups.
 D. participates in membrane structure formation.
 E. stabilize membrane fluidity.
 Correct Option: C
 Explanation:
 Correct (C): Phospholipids contain both polar and non-polar groups, allowing formation of selectively permeable membrane bilayers.
 Incorrect (A): Amphipathic nature describes phospholipids but does not specifically explain membrane structural behavior as directly as the presence of polar and non-polar groups.
 Incorrect (B): Bilayer formation is a consequence of their dual chemical nature rather than the primary defining feature asked for.
 Incorrect (D): Participation in membrane structure formation is a general effect, not the fundamental property responsible.
 Incorrect (E): Membrane fluidity is mainly stabilized by cholesterol rather than phospholipids alone.
