
Question: Serum acid phosphatase is raised in which condition?
A. Acute pancreatitis
B. Acute myocardial infarction
C. Carcinoma of prostate
D. Osteoblastic sarcoma
E. Obstructive jaundice
Correct Option: C
Explanation:
Correct (C): Prostate carcinoma elevates acid phosphatase (classical marker)
Incorrect (A): Pancreatitis raises amylase/lipase
Incorrect (B): MI raises CK-MB/troponin
Incorrect (D): Osteoblastic lesions raise alkaline phosphatase
Incorrect (E): Obstructive jaundice raises conjugated bilirubin/ALP

Question: All of the following are liver enzyme markers except:
A. Alkaline phosphatase
B. Alanine transaminase
C. Aspartate transaminase
D. Gamma glutamyl transferase
E. Amylase
Correct Option: E
Explanation:
Correct (E): Amylase is pancreatic, not liver enzyme
Incorrect (A): ALP rises in cholestasis
Incorrect (B): ALT is hepatocellular marker
Incorrect (C): AST is hepatic + muscle marker
Incorrect (D): GGT indicates hepatobiliary injury

Correct Option: C
Explanation:

Question: Alkaline phosphatase is increased in:
A. carcinoma head of pancreas
B. carcinoma of prostate
C. cholecystitis
D. pancreatitis
E. rickets
Correct Option: E
Explanation:
Correct (E): Alkaline phosphatase rises markedly in rickets due to increased osteoblastic activity during defective bone mineralization.
Incorrect (A): Carcinoma head of pancreas may elevate alkaline phosphatase only if biliary obstruction occurs, not as the classic association.
Incorrect (B): Carcinoma of prostate is more strongly associated with acid phosphatase as a tumor marker.
Incorrect (C): Cholecystitis may cause mild enzyme elevation but not the characteristic marked increase seen in bone disease.
Incorrect (D): Pancreatitis primarily elevates amylase and lipase rather than alkaline phosphatase.
Tags: Easy, Biochemistry, Foundation, Clinical Enzymology

Question: All of the following enzymes are important diagnostic markers of liver disease except:
A. alkaline phosphatase
B. alanine transaminase
C. amylase
D. aspartate transaminase
E. gamma glutamyl transferase
Correct Option: C
Explanation:
Correct (C): Amylase is primarily a marker of pancreatic disease rather than liver disease.
Incorrect (A): Alkaline phosphatase is elevated in cholestatic liver disorders.
Incorrect (B): Alanine transaminase is a sensitive indicator of hepatocellular injury.
Incorrect (D): Aspartate transaminase rises in liver cell damage.
Incorrect (E): Gamma glutamyl transferase is commonly elevated in hepatobiliary disease and alcoholism.
Tags: Easy, Biochemistry, Foundation, Clinical Enzymology

A. acid phosphatase
B. alanine transaminase
C. aldolase
D. creatine kinase
E. lipase
Correct Option: A
Explanation:
Correct (A): Acid phosphatase is a classic tumor marker associated with carcinoma of prostate.
Incorrect (B): Alanine transaminase is mainly a marker of liver injury.
Incorrect (C): Aldolase rises in muscle disorders rather than malignancy.
Incorrect (D): Creatine kinase indicates muscle or myocardial damage.
Incorrect (E): Lipase is primarily elevated in pancreatitis.

A rise in serum acid phosphatase indicates:
A. acute hepatitis
B. carcinoma of pancreas
C. carcinoma of prostate
D. mumps
E. recent myocardial infarction
Correct Option: C
Explanation:
Correct (C): Serum acid phosphatase is classically elevated in carcinoma of prostate, especially metastatic disease.
Incorrect (A): Acute hepatitis is more strongly associated with elevated transaminases.
Incorrect (B): Carcinoma of pancreas does not characteristically raise acid phosphatase.
Incorrect (D): Mumps commonly elevates salivary enzymes rather than acid phosphatase.
Incorrect (E): Recent myocardial infarction raises cardiac enzymes such as CK-MB and troponins.

The subunit composition of LDH1 isoenzyme of lactate dehydrogenase is:
A. HHHH
B. HHHM
C. HHMM
D. HMMM
E. MMMM
Correct Option: A
Explanation:
Correct (A): LDH1 isoenzyme of lactate dehydrogenase is composed entirely of four H subunits (HHHH).
Incorrect (B): HHHM corresponds to LDH2.
Incorrect (C): HHMM corresponds to LDH3.
Incorrect (D): HMMM corresponds to LDH4.
Incorrect (E): MMMM corresponds to LDH5.

Which one of the following enzymes is plasma-specific:
A. ceruloplasmin
B. coagulation factor X
C. lipoprotein lipase
D. plasmin
E. pseudocholinesterase
Correct Option: E
Explanation:
Correct (E): Pseudocholinesterase is a plasma-specific enzyme synthesized in the liver and normally present in plasma.
Incorrect (A): Ceruloplasmin is a plasma protein but not an enzyme.
Incorrect (B): Coagulation factor X is a clotting factor, not classified as a plasma-specific enzyme.
Incorrect (C): Lipoprotein lipase acts mainly on endothelial surfaces.
Incorrect (D): Plasmin is generated transiently during fibrinolysis rather than being plasma-specific.
Tags: Easy, Biochemistry, Foundation, Clinical Enzymology

Creatine phosphokinase (CPK) is NOT found in:
A. brain
B. kidneys
C. liver
D. myocardium
E. skeletal muscles
Correct Option: C
Explanation:
Correct (C): Creatine phosphokinase (CPK) is essentially absent in liver tissue.
Incorrect (A): Brain contains the CK-BB isoenzyme.
Incorrect (B): Kidneys contain measurable CPK activity.
Incorrect (D): Myocardium contains CK-MB.
Incorrect (E): Skeletal muscles contain abundant CK-MM.
