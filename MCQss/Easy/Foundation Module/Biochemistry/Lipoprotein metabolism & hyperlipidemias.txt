
Question: From the liver, triglycerides are transported to extrahepatic tissue by:
A. chylomicrons
B. vldl
C. hdl
D. ldl
E. idl
Correct Option: B
Explanation:
Correct (B): vldl carries endogenous triglycerides from liver to peripheral tissues.
Incorrect (A): chylomicrons transport dietary lipids from intestine.
Incorrect (C): hdl mediates reverse cholesterol transport.
Incorrect (D): ldl delivers cholesterol, not triglycerides.
Incorrect (E): idl is an intermediate remnant of vldl metabolism.

Question: Elevated plasma level of the following protects against atherosclerosis:
A. chylomicrons
B. vldl
C. hdl
D. ldl
E. idl
Correct Option: C
Explanation:
Correct (C): hdl removes cholesterol from tissues and protects against atherosclerosis.
Incorrect (A): chylomicrons transport dietary fat and are not protective.
Incorrect (B): vldl contributes to lipid deposition.
Incorrect (D): ldl is a major atherogenic lipoprotein.
Incorrect (E): idl is also atherogenic precursor of ldl.

Question: Which of the following is regarded as cardioprotective:
A. ldh
B. ldl
C. vldl
D. hdl
E. cholesterol
Correct Option: D
Explanation:
Correct (D): hdl removes cholesterol from peripheral tissues and reduces cardiovascular risk.
Incorrect (A): ldh is a tissue damage marker, not cardioprotective.
Incorrect (B): ldl promotes atherosclerotic plaque formation.
Incorrect (C): vldl contributes to triglyceride deposition.
Incorrect (E): cholesterol in excess is a risk factor, not protective.
