Question: Histamine is released from:
 A. dendritic cells.
 B. mast cells.
 C. monocytes.
 D. neutrophils.
 E. plasma cells.
 Correct Option: B
 Explanation:
 •	Correct (B): Mast cells store preformed histamine in cytoplasmic granules and rapidly release it during acute inflammation.
 •	Incorrect (A): Dendritic cells function primarily as antigen-presenting cells and are not major histamine stores.
 •	Incorrect (C): Monocytes produce cytokines but do not contain significant preformed histamine granules.
 •	Incorrect (D): Neutrophils are phagocytic cells and are not a major source of histamine.
 •	Incorrect (E): Plasma cells synthesize antibodies rather than inflammatory vasoactive amines.

Question: Vasodilation can be caused by:
 A. complement C3a.
 B. histamine.
 C. prostacyclin (PGI2).
 D. prostaglandin E2.
 E. thromboxane A2.
 Correct Option: B
 Explanation:
 •	Correct (B): Histamine is a potent vasoactive amine that causes rapid arteriolar vasodilation during acute inflammation.
 •	Incorrect (A): Complement C3a is primarily an anaphylatoxin that promotes mast cell degranulation rather than directly causing vasodilation.
 •	Incorrect (C): Prostacyclin (PGI2) can cause vasodilation but is not the classic immediate mediator emphasized in acute inflammation.
 •	Incorrect (D): Prostaglandin E2 contributes to vasodilation but is not the principal rapid mediator in this context.
 •	Incorrect (E): Thromboxane A2 causes vasoconstriction and platelet aggregation.
 Tags: Easy, Pathology, Foundation, Inflammation & Repair

Question: Which of the following is not a classic sign of acute inflammation:
 A. calor.
 B. collagen deposition.
 C. dolor.
 D. rubor.
 E. tumor.
 Correct Option: B
 Explanation:
 •	Correct (B): Collagen deposition is a feature of tissue repair and fibrosis rather than an acute inflammatory sign.
 •	Incorrect (A): Calor (heat) is a cardinal sign of acute inflammation.
 •	Incorrect (C): Dolor (pain) is a cardinal sign of acute inflammation.
 •	Incorrect (D): Rubor (redness) is a cardinal sign of acute inflammation.
 •	Incorrect (E): Tumor (swelling) is a cardinal sign of acute inflammation.
 Tags: Easy, Pathology, Foundation, Inflammation & Repair

Question: Prostaglandins produce:
 A. fever.
 B. pain.
 C. platelet aggregation.
 D. vasoconstriction.
 E. vasodilation.
 Correct Option: E
 Explanation:
 •	Correct (E): Prostaglandins, particularly PGE2 and PGI2, produce vasodilation during inflammation.
 •	Incorrect (A): Fever is caused mainly by PGE2 but is not the best answer among the listed isolated effects.
 •	Incorrect (B): Pain results from prostaglandin-mediated sensitization of nerve endings but is not the principal answer here.
 •	Incorrect (C): Platelet aggregation is mediated mainly by thromboxane A2.
 •	Incorrect (D): Vasoconstriction is not the typical action of inflammatory prostaglandins.
