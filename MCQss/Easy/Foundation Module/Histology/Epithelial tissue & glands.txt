Question: The oesophagus is lined by:
A. Pseudostratified columnar epithelium
B. Simple columnar epithelium
C. Simple cuboidal epithelium
D. Stratified squamous keratinized epithelium
E. Stratified squamous non-keratinized epithelium
Correct Option: E
Explanation:
Correct (E): The oesophagus requires a thick, multi-layered lining to withstand physical abrasion from swallowed food boluses without drying out, which is provided by stratified squamous non-keratinized epithelium.
Incorrect (A): Pseudostratified columnar epithelium primarily lines respiratory passages like the trachea rather than the digestive tract.
Incorrect (B): Simple columnar epithelium lines the gastrointestinal tract from the stomach to the rectum to optimize secretion and absorption.
Incorrect (C): Simple cuboidal epithelium lines small excretory ducts and kidney tubules where protection against severe abrasion is not required.
Incorrect (D): Stratified squamous keratinized epithelium forms the outer layer of the skin (epidermis) to protect against severe abrasion and desiccation.

Question: Which of the following is true about the structure of cilia:
A. Cilia are composed of intermediate filaments arranged in a 9+2 pattern.
B. Cilia are composed of microfilaments arranged in a 9+2 pattern.
C. Cilia are composed of microtubules arranged in a 8+2 pattern.
D. Cilia are composed of microtubules arranged in a 9+0 pattern.
E. Cilia are composed of microtubules arranged in a 9+2 pattern.
Correct Option: E
Explanation:
Correct (E): Motile cilia are axonemal structures composed of microtubules arranged in a 9+2 pattern, featuring nine peripheral doublets surrounding one central pair.
Incorrect (A): Cilia are composed of intermediate filaments arranged in a 9+2 pattern is incorrect because intermediate filaments do not form the motile core of cilia.
Incorrect (B): Cilia are composed of microfilaments arranged in a 9+2 pattern is incorrect because actin microfilaments form the structural core of microvilli, not motile cilia.
Incorrect (C): Cilia are composed of microtubules arranged in a 8+2 pattern is structurally incorrect as the peripheral ring always contains nine microtubule doublets.
Incorrect (D): Cilia are composed of microtubules arranged in a 9+0 pattern describes non-motile primary cilia used for chemosensation, rather than standard motile cilia.

Question: Kinocilia are made up of:
A. Intermediate filaments
B. Microbodies
C. Microfilaments
D. Microtubules
E. Ribosomes
Correct Option: D
Explanation:
Correct (D): Kinocilia are true motile cilia found in structures like the inner ear vestibule, and like all true cilia, their internal axoneme consists entirely of microtubules.
Incorrect (A): Intermediate filaments provide mechanical stability throughout the cytoplasm but do not form mobile specialized appendages like kinocilia.
Incorrect (B): Microbodies (peroxisomes) are intracellular metabolic organelles involved in fatty acid oxidation, completely unrelated to flagellar or ciliar structure.
Incorrect (C): Microfilaments are solid rods of actin that generate cellular contractile forces and anchor microvilli, rather than driving kinocilia motility.
Incorrect (E): Ribosomes are the enzymatic ribonucleoprotein complexes responsible for cellular protein synthesis and play no structural role in the core of cilia.

Question 12
Cell junction connecting basal lamina with epithelium:
A. desmosome
B. gap junction
C. hemidesmosome
D. junctional complex
E. zonula occludens
Correct Option: C
Explanation:
Correct (C): The hemidesmosome is a specialized asymmetrical anchoring junction that connects the intermediate filaments of an epithelial cell to the underlying basal lamina.
Incorrect (A): A desmosome links the intermediate filaments of one epithelial cell to those of an adjacent epithelial cell, not to the extracellular matrix.
Incorrect (B): A gap junction forms an aqueous pore between adjacent cells to allow ionic communication, playing no role in basal lamina attachment.
Incorrect (D): A junctional complex consists of an apical grouping of tight, adherent, and desmosomal junctions linking lateral cell membranes, not the basal boundary.
Incorrect (E): A zonula occludens is located at the apical-lateral border to seal paracellular transport paths between adjacent cells.
