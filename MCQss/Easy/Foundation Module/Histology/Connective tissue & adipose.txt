Question: Plasma cells:
A) are found mostly in lymphatic tissue
B) are specialized for production of humoral antibodies
C) contain few ribosomes
D) have a centrally placed nucleus
E) possess no rough endoplasmic reticulum
Correct Option: B
Explanation:
Correct (B): Plasma cells are terminally differentiated B lymphocytes exclusively specialized for the massive synthesis and secretion of humoral antibodies.
Incorrect (A): are found mostly in lymphatic tissue is incorrect because plasma cells are widely distributed throughout the lamina propria of the gastrointestinal and respiratory tracts, not just restricted to lymphatic tissue.
Incorrect (C): contain few ribosomes is incorrect because they contain an immense population of ribosomes organized into rough endoplasmic reticulum to facilitate intensive protein synthesis.
Incorrect (D): have a centrally placed nucleus is incorrect because plasma cells characteristically possess a eccentrically placed nucleus.
Incorrect (E): possess no rough endoplasmic reticulum is incorrect because the cytoplasm is nearly filled with extensive rough endoplasmic reticulum arrays needed for antibody manufacturing.

Question: The distinguishing feature of plasma cell is its:
A) cartwheel nucleus
B) cytoplasmic vacuole
C) membrane structure
D) shape
E) site of presence
Correct Option: A
Explanation:
Correct (A): The cartwheel nucleus, created by alternating clumps of heterochromatin and euchromatin, provides a definitive morphologic hallmark for identifying plasma cells under light microscopy.
Incorrect (B): cytoplasmic vacuole is incorrect because cytoplasmic vacuoles are not a defining diagnostic feature of plasma cells compared to their nuclear morphology.
Incorrect (C): membrane structure is incorrect because the basic plasma membrane architecture does not distinguish it from other mononuclear leukocytes.
Incorrect (D): shape is incorrect because an ovoid cell shape is shared by several other connective tissue cells and lacks diagnostic specificity.
Incorrect (E): site of presence is incorrect because plasma cells can be found in various loose connective tissues and lymphoid organs, making location non-pathognomonic.

Question: Transient (wandering cells) of CT are:

Question: The most generalized type of connective tissue (in the sense of possessing the greatest variety of connective tissue components) is:
A) adipose
B) dense
C) loose
D) mesenchyme
E) reticular
Correct Option: C
Explanation:
Correct (C): Loose connective tissue serves as the prototypical generalized template, containing an equal, diverse mix of multiple cell types, fibers, and ground substance components.
Incorrect (A): adipose is incorrect because adipose tissue is highly specialized and dominated almost exclusively by lipid-storing cells.
Incorrect (B): dense is incorrect because dense connective tissue is specialized for mechanical strength, being heavily dominated by packed fiber bundles with minimal cellular variety.
Incorrect (D): mesenchyme is incorrect because mesenchyme is an unspecialized embryonic tissue lacking mature fiber types and fully differentiated adult components.
Incorrect (E): reticular is incorrect because reticular tissue is specialized to form delicate structural frameworks for lymphoid organs using a restricted subset of type III collagen fibers.

Question: The precursor of the heparin is contained in:
A) the endothelial cells
B) the fibroblasts
C) the histiocytes
D) the mast cells
E) the plasma cell
Correct Option: D
Explanation:
Correct (D): The mast cells synthesize and store highly sulfated heparin glycosaminoglycan precursors within their basophilic cytoplasmic granules.
Incorrect (A): the endothelial cells is incorrect because endothelial cells produce anticoagulants like heparan sulfate and thrombomodulin but do not store granular heparin precursors.
Incorrect (B): the fibroblasts is incorrect because fibroblasts produce structural components like hyaluronic acid and chondroitin sulfate rather than heparin.
Incorrect (C): the histiocytes is incorrect because histiocytes specialize in lysosomal degradation enzymes rather than storing sulfated glycosaminoglycans like heparin.
Incorrect (E): the plasma cell is incorrect because plasma cells are dedicated to the synthesis of immunoglobulins, not secretory glycosaminoglycans.

Question: Dense regular connective tissue is present in:
A) basement membrane
B) capsules of organs
C) epidermis
D) skin
E) tendons
Correct Option: E
Explanation:
Correct (E): Tendons require the high tensile strength along a single axis provided by the parallel, densely packed collagen fibers of dense regular connective tissue.
Incorrect (A): basement membrane is incorrect because the basement membrane is an acellular matrix sheet composed of type IV collagen and laminin, not dense fibrillar bundles.
Incorrect (B): capsules of organs is incorrect because organ capsules are subjected to multidirectional forces and are composed of dense irregular connective tissue.
Incorrect (C): epidermis is incorrect because the epidermis is a stratified squamous keratinized epithelium, not a connective tissue.
Incorrect (D): skin is incorrect because the dermis layer of the skin is composed of dense irregular connective tissue to withstand forces from multiple angles.

Question: The fibrous component of loose connective tissue is composed of:

Incorrect (B): elastin, collagen, and glycoproteins is incorrect because glycoproteins are structural components of the ground substance, not fibers themselves.
Incorrect (C): type I collagen in fine fibers is incorrect because it accounts for only one portion of the total diverse fiber profile present in loose connective tissue.
Incorrect (D): type I collagen in loose bundles is incorrect because this description excludes the essential reticular and elastic networks that complete the loose CT architecture.
Incorrect (E): type II collagen in dense networks is incorrect because type II collagen is localized to cartilage matrix and does not form the fibrous networks of loose connective tissue.

Question: White adipocytes are derived developmentally from what precursor cells:
A) brown adipocytes
B) fibroblasts
C) histiocytes
D) mesenchymal cells
E) monocytes
Correct Option: D
Explanation:
Correct (D): Mesenchymal cells serve as the multipotent embryonic stem cells that differentiate directly into lipoblasts and subsequently mature into white adipocytes.
Incorrect (A): brown adipocytes is incorrect because brown adipocytes arise from a distinct myogenic precursor lineage (Myf5+) and do not transdifferentiate into adult white adipocytes.
Incorrect (B): fibroblasts is incorrect because fibroblasts represent a fully differentiated parallel resident stromal cell lineage.
Incorrect (C): histiocytes is incorrect because histiocytes are phagocytic cells originating from hematopoietic myeloid progenitors.
Incorrect (E): monocytes is incorrect because monocytes are circulating blood leukocytes that differentiate exclusively into macrophages and myeloid dendritic cells.
