
Question: Rotates the radius medially so that the palm of the hand faces posteriorly and its dorsum faces anteriorly: A. pronation. B. protraction. C. retraction. D. supination. E. upward rotation. Correct Option: A Explanation: Correct (A): Pronation rotates the radius medially causing palm to face posteriorly and dorsum anteriorly. Incorrect (B): Protraction is forward movement of scapula, not forearm rotation. Incorrect (C): Retraction is backward movement of scapula, not forearm rotation. Incorrect (D): Supination produces opposite rotation with palm facing anteriorly. Incorrect (E): Upward rotation refers to scapular movement, not radius rotation

Question: Movement by which the pad of the 1st digit (thumb) is brought to another digit pad: A. apposition. B. opposition. C. protraction. D. reposition. E. retraction. Correct Option: B Explanation: Correct (B): Opposition is thumb movement bringing its pad to other finger pads. Incorrect (A): Apposition is not a standard anatomical movement term for thumb-to-finger contact. Incorrect (C): Protraction is forward movement of scapula or jaw. Incorrect (D): Reposition is return movement after opposition. Incorrect (E): Retraction is backward movement of scapula or jaw.

Question 9. Circumduction is a movement that is best demonstrated at which type of joint?
 
A. Ball-and-socket joint
B. Condyloid joint
C. Hinge joint
D. Pivot joint
E. Saddle joint
 
Correct Option: A
Explanation:
 
Correct (A): Ball-and-socket joints allow multiaxial movement including circumduction.
Incorrect (B): Condyloid allows biaxial but limited circumduction.
Incorrect (C): Hinge allows only flexion-extension.
Incorrect (D): Pivot allows rotation only.
Incorrect (E): Saddle allows biaxial movement but less free circumduction than ball-and-socket.
