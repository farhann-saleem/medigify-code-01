Question 15. Epiphyseal growth plate is:
 
A. Gomphosis
B. Schindylesis
C. Synchondrosis
D. Syndesmosis
E. Suture
 
Correct Option: C
Explanation:
 
Correct (C): Epiphyseal plate is a temporary primary cartilaginous (hyaline) synchondrosis.
Incorrect (A): Gomphosis is tooth-socket fibrous joint.
Incorrect (B): Schindylesis is fibrous joint type.
Incorrect (D): Syndesmosis is ligamentous fibrous joint.
Incorrect (E): Suture is fibrous joint between skull bones.
