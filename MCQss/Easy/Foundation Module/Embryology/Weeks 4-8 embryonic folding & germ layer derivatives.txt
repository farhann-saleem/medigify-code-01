Question: The developing embryo has a distinct human appearance by the end of:
A. week 5
B. week 6
C. week 7
D. week 8
E. week 9
Correct Option: D
Explanation:
• Correct (D): By week 8, organogenesis is complete and human features are distinct.
• Incorrect (A): Week 5 still shows early limb and facial bud development.
• Incorrect (B): Week 6 features are still embryologically primitive.
• Incorrect (C): Week 7 shows continued differentiation but not full appearance.
• Incorrect (E): Week 9 is fetal period, after appearance is already established.

Question: At the end of week 2 of development (day 14), what is the composition of the embryonic disk:
a. ectoderm and endoderm.
b. ectoderm, mesoderm, and endoderm.
c. epiblast and hypoblast.
d. epiblast only.
e. mesoderm and endoderm.
Correct Option: C
Explanation:
Correct (C): By day 14 the embryonic disk is bilaminar, consisting of epiblast and hypoblast.
Incorrect (A): Definitive ectoderm and endoderm are established after gastrulation in week 3.
Incorrect (B): All three germ layers are not present until gastrulation occurs.
Incorrect (D): Hypoblast is also present at the end of week 2.
Incorrect (E): Mesoderm has not yet formed by day 14.

Question: Which germ layers are present at the end of week 3 of development (day 21):
a. ectoderm and endoderm.

e. mesoderm and endoderm.

Explanation:
Correct (B): By day 21 gastrulation has produced the trilaminar embryonic disk composed of ectoderm, mesoderm, and endoderm.
Incorrect (A): Mesoderm is also present by the end of week 3.

Question: The epiblast is capable of forming which germ layers:
a. ectoderm and endoderm only.
b. ectoderm and mesoderm only.
c. ectoderm only.
d. ectoderm, mesoderm, and endoderm.
e. mesoderm and endoderm only.
Correct Option: D
Explanation:
Correct (D): Cells of the epiblast give rise to all three definitive germ layers during gastrulation.
Incorrect (A): Mesoderm is also derived from migrating epiblast cells.
Incorrect (B): Endoderm also originates from the epiblast.
Incorrect (C): The epiblast forms far more than ectoderm alone.
Incorrect (E): Epiblast additionally forms ectoderm.
