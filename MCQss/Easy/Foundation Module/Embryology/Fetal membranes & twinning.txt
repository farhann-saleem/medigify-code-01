Question: The maternal and fetal components of the placenta are:
A. decidua basalis and chorion laeve.
B. decidua basalis and villous chorion.
C. decidua capsularis and secondary chorionic villi.
D. decidua parietalis and tertiary chorionic villi.
E. decidua vera and smooth chorion.
Correct Option: B
Explanation:
Correct (B): The placenta is formed by the maternal decidua basalis and the fetal villous chorion (chorion frondosum).
Incorrect (A): Chorion laeve is the smooth chorion that loses villi and does not form the placenta.
Incorrect (C): Decidua capsularis surrounds the embryo and is not the placental maternal component.
Incorrect (D): Decidua parietalis lines the remainder of the uterus rather than participating in placental formation.
Incorrect (E): Smooth chorion lacks chorionic villi and therefore does not contribute to the functional placenta.

Incorrect (D): Maternal blood is present, but fetal blood is also closely associated within villi, making this incomplete.

Chorionic villi containing fetal capillaries project into this space. Exchange of gases and nutrients occurs across the placental barrier without mixing maternal and fetal blood.
