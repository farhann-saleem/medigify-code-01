Question: Where does the blastocyst normally implant?
A. Basal layer of the endometrium.
B. Functional layer of the cervix.
C. Functional layer of the endometrium.
D. Myometrium.
E. Perimetrium.
Correct Option: C
Explanation:
Correct (C): The blastocyst normally implants in the functional layer of the endometrium.
Incorrect (A): The basal layer regenerates the endometrium and is not the implantation
site.
Incorrect (B): Implantation in the cervix is abnormal and ectopic.
Incorrect (D): The myometrium is the muscular uterine wall, not the implantation layer.
Incorrect (E): The perimetrium is the outer uterine covering and cannot support implantation.

Question: Which structure must degenerate for blastocyst implantation to occur?
A. Corona radiata
B. Cytotrophoblast
C. Functional layer of endometrium
D. Syncytiotrophoblast
E. Zona pellucida
Correct Option: E
Explanation:
Correct (E): Degeneration of the zona pellucida allows “hatching” of the blastocyst so trophoblast cells can attach to the endometrial epithelium for implantation.
Incorrect (A): Corona radiata cells are dispersed before fertilization and are not the barrier preventing implantation.
Incorrect (B): Cytotrophoblast forms part of the trophoblast and is essential for implantation rather than degenerating.
Incorrect (C): The functional layer of endometrium receives the implanting blastocyst and does not degenerate beforehand.
Incorrect (D): Syncytiotrophoblast actively invades the endometrium during implantation and must remain functional.

Question: The seven day blastocyst:
a. has a distinct primitive streak.
b. has an amniotic cavity.
c. is attached to endometrial epithelium.
d. is enclosed by chorionic villi.
e. is surrounded by zona pellucida.
Correct Option: C
Explanation:
Correct (C): By day 7 the blastocyst has attached to the endometrial epithelium, initiating implantation.
Incorrect (A): The primitive streak develops during week 3, not in a seven day blastocyst.
Incorrect (B): The amniotic cavity appears slightly later during the second week.
Incorrect (D): Chorionic villi have not yet formed at day 7.
Incorrect (E): The zona pellucida has already degenerated before implantation begins.

Question: First sign of pregnancy on ultrasound is the presence of:
a. embryonic fetus.
b. fetal heart movement.
c. intrauterine gestation sac.
d. intrauterine yolk sac.
e. placental circulation.
Correct Option: C
Explanation:
Correct (C): The earliest detectable ultrasonographic sign of pregnancy is the intrauterine gestation sac.
Incorrect (A): The embryonic fetus becomes visible later in development.
Incorrect (B): Fetal heart movement appears after the gestational sac is identified.
Incorrect (D): The intrauterine yolk sac develops after the gestational sac is seen.
Incorrect (E): Placental circulation is established later and is not the first ultrasound sign.
Tags: Easy, Embryology, Foundation Module, Week 2: Implantation & Bilaminar Disc

Question: At what location does the amniotic cavity develop:
a. between the cytotrophoblast and syncytiotrophoblast.
b. between the endoderm and mesoderm.
c. within the epiblast.
d. within the extraembryonic mesoderm.
e. within the hypoblast.
Correct Option: C
Explanation:
Correct (C): The amniotic cavity develops as a fluid-filled space within the epiblast during the second week.
Incorrect (A): The trophoblastic layers do not contain the developing amniotic cavity.
Incorrect (B): The cavity does not form between endoderm and mesoderm.
Incorrect (D): Extraembryonic mesoderm contributes to chorionic structures, not the amniotic cavity origin.
Incorrect (E): Hypoblast forms the yolk sac lining rather than the amniotic cavity.
Tags: Easy, Embryology, Foundation Module, Week 2: Implantation & Bilaminar Disc
