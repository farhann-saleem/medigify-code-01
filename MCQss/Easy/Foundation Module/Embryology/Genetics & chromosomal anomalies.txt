Question: Webbed neck and gonadal dysgenesis are the characteristic findings of?
A. Down syndrome
B. Edwards syndrome
C. Klinefelter syndrome
D. Patau syndrome
E. Turner syndrome
Correct Option: E
Explanation:
• Correct (E): Turner syndrome classically presents with webbed neck due to cystic hygroma and gonadal dysgenesis causing streak ovaries and primary amenorrhea.
• Incorrect (A): Down syndrome is characterized by trisomy 21 with intellectual disability and epicanthic folds, not gonadal dysgenesis.
• Incorrect (B): Edwards syndrome presents with clenched fists and rocker-bottom feet rather than webbed neck with streak gonads.
• Incorrect (C): Klinefelter syndrome causes testicular atrophy and tall stature in males, not webbed neck.
• Incorrect (D): Patau syndrome is associated with cleft lip/palate and holoprosencephaly, not gonadal dysgenesis.

Question: Deletion of the short arm of chromosome 5 is known as?
A. Angelman syndrome
B. Cri-du-chat syndrome
C. Miller-Dieker syndrome
D. Prader-Willi syndrome
E. Williams syndrome
Correct Option: B
Explanation:
• Correct (B): Cri-du-chat syndrome results from deletion of the short arm of chromosome 5 (5p deletion) causing a characteristic cat-like cry.
• Incorrect (A): Angelman syndrome is caused by maternal deletion of chromosome 15q affecting UBE3A.
• Incorrect (C): Miller-Dieker syndrome is associated with chromosome 17p deletion causing lissencephaly.
• Incorrect (D): Prader-Willi syndrome involves paternal deletion or maternal uniparental disomy of chromosome 15.
• Incorrect (E): Williams syndrome is due to deletion on chromosome 7 involving the elastin gene.
