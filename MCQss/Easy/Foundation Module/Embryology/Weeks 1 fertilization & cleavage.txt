Question: Which part of blastocyst gives rise to embryo:
A. chorionic mesoderm
B. cytotrophoblast
C. embryoblast
D. extraembryonic mesoderm
E. syncytiotrophoblast
Correct Option: C
Explanation:
• Correct (C): The embryoblast (inner cell mass) forms the embryo proper.
• Incorrect (A): Chorionic mesoderm forms fetal part of placenta, not embryo.
• Incorrect (B): Cytotrophoblast contributes to trophoblastic layers, not embryo.
• Incorrect (D): Extraembryonic mesoderm forms supporting membranes only.
• Incorrect (E): Syncytiotrophoblast forms invasive placental layer, not embryo.
