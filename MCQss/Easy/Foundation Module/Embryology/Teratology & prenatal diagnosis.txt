Question: The main deformity of fetal alcohol syndrome is?
A. cataract
B. cleft lip
C. intellectual disability
D. micrognathia
E. thymic aplasia
Correct Option: C
Explanation:
• Correct (C): Fetal alcohol syndrome most prominently causes intellectual disability due to alcohol-induced neurodevelopmental injury.
• Incorrect (A): Cataract is more strongly associated with congenital rubella syndrome than fetal alcohol syndrome.
• Incorrect (B): Cleft lip is not the principal deformity associated with prenatal alcohol exposure.
• Incorrect (D): Micrognathia may occur but is not the major manifestation of fetal alcohol syndrome.
• Incorrect (E): Thymic aplasia is characteristic of DiGeorge syndrome, not fetal alcohol syndrome.

Question: High level of hCG is found in:
A. ectopic pregnancy.
B. hydatidiform mole.
C. placenta accreta.
D. placenta previa.
E. sacrococcygeal teratoma.
Correct Option: B
Explanation:
Correct (B): Hydatidiform mole causes excessive trophoblastic proliferation with markedly elevated hCG levels.
Incorrect (A): Ectopic pregnancy usually has lower and abnormally rising hCG levels rather than extremely high values.
Incorrect (C): Placenta accreta is abnormal placental adherence without trophoblastic overproduction of hCG.
Incorrect (D): Placenta previa causes painless bleeding but not marked hCG elevation.
Incorrect (E): Sacrococcygeal teratoma is a germ cell tumor not characteristically associated with high hCG.

Question: Best indicator for assessment of fetal age in first trimester:
A. amniotic fluid amount
B. biparietal diameter
C. crown-rump length
D. femur length
E. head circumference
Correct Option: C
Explanation:
 * Correct (C): Crown-rump length provides the lowest biological variation and the most precise ultrasound estimation of fetal age during the first trimester.
 * Incorrect (A): Amniotic fluid amount varies widely due to maternal and placental factors and is never used as an indicator to assess gestational age.
 * Incorrect (B): Biparietal diameter is an ultrasound metric reserved for the assessment of fetal age in the second and third trimesters.
 * Incorrect (D): Femur length is utilized to calculate fetal growth and gestational age exclusively in the later trimesters, not the first trimester.
 * Incorrect (E): Head circumference is an anthropometric parameter used to track development in the second and third trimesters.

Question: The ultrasound marker measured before 12 weeks of gestation to assess fetal gestational age is:
A. abdominal circumference
B. biparietal diameter
C. crown-rump length
D. femur length
E. Head circumference
Correct Option: C
Explanation:
 * Correct (C): Crown-rump length is the definitive biometric parameter measured via ultrasound before 12 weeks of gestation for accurate pregnancy dating.
 * Incorrect (A): Abdominal circumference is a metric used primarily in the second and third trimesters to monitor fetal growth and asymmetric restriction, not early age.
 * Incorrect (B): Biparietal diameter cannot be reliably measured or used for precise age estimation before the cranial bones mature post-12 weeks.
 * Incorrect (D): Femur length is an indicator of skeletal growth that is only measured after the long bones ossify sufficiently during the second trimester.
 * Incorrect (E): Head circumference is utilized to evaluate cranial development and fetal age exclusively during the second and third trimesters.
