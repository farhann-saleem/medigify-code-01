Question: A normal somatic cell contains a total of 46 chromosomes. What is the normal complement of chromosomes found in sperm?
A. 22 autosomes.
B. 22 autosomes plus a sex chromosome.
C. 23 autosomes.
D. 23 autosomes plus a sex chromosome.
E. 46 chromosomes plus a sex chromosome.
Correct Option: B
Explanation:
Correct (B): Sperm are haploid gametes containing 22 autosomes plus a sex chromosome (X or Y).
Incorrect (A): 22 autosomes alone omits the required sex chromosome.
Incorrect (C): 23 autosomes excludes the sex chromosome and gives an incorrect autosomal count.
Incorrect (D): 23 autosomes plus a sex chromosome would total 24 chromosomes.
Incorrect (E): 46 chromosomes plus a sex chromosome is diploid and abnormal for sperm.
