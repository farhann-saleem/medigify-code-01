Question: Individual blastomeres were isolated from a blastula at the 4-cell stage. Each blastomere was cultured in vitro to the blastocyst stage and individually implanted into four pseudo-pregnant foster mothers. Which of the following would you expect to observe 9 months later:
A. birth of four genetically different babies.
B. birth of four genetically identical babies.
C. birth of four phenotypically abnormal babies.
D. birth of one baby.
E. birth of two genetically identical babies.
Correct Option: B
Explanation:
Correct (B): Blastomeres separated at the 4-cell stage are totipotent and derive from the same zygote, producing four genetically identical babies.
Incorrect (A): Different genetic makeup occurs in dizygotic twinning, not from one zygote split at cleavage stages.
Incorrect (C): Early isolated blastomeres can develop normally and are not inherently phenotypically abnormal.
Incorrect (D): Each implanted blastomere can independently form a viable embryo, not just one.
Incorrect (E): Separation of all four blastomeres would yield four identical offspring, not only two.

Question: Which event occurs during cleavage of the zygote in week 1 of development?
A. Decrease in size of blastomeres.
B. Increased cytoplasmic content.
C. Production of highly differentiated blastomeres.
D. Series of meiotic divisions.
E. Thickening of zona pellucida.
Correct Option: A
Explanation:
Correct (A): Cleavage produces progressively smaller blastomeres without increasing overall embryonic size.
Incorrect (B): Cytoplasmic volume does not increase during cleavage divisions.
Incorrect (C): Blastomeres remain relatively undifferentiated during early cleavage.
Incorrect (D): Cleavage consists of mitotic, not meiotic, divisions.
Incorrect (E): The zona pellucida thins before hatching rather than thickening.

Question: Where does fertilization occur?
A. Ampulla
B. Cervix
C. Infundibulum
D. Isthmus
E. Myometrium
Correct Option: A
Explanation:
Correct (A): Fertilization most commonly occurs in the ampulla, the widest segment of the uterine tube.
Incorrect (B): The cervix serves as a passageway for sperm but is not the site of fertilization.
Incorrect (C): The infundibulum captures the oocyte but is not the usual fertilization site.
Incorrect (D): The isthmus mainly transports the zygote toward the uterus.
Incorrect (E): The myometrium is the muscular uterine wall and has no role in fertilization.
Tags: Easy, Embryology, Foundation Module, Week 1: Fertilization & Cleavage

12. Question: When someone is “6 weeks” pregnant, this means that she has conceived ____ weeks previously:
 A. 3
 B. 4
 C. 5
 D. 6
 E. 7
 Correct Option: B
 Explanation:
 • Correct (B): Pregnancy dating counts from last menstrual period, so conception occurred about 2 weeks later, making 4 weeks previously.
 • Incorrect (A): 3 weeks is too short an interval from conception.
 • Incorrect (C): 5 weeks exceeds the expected lag.
 • Incorrect (D): 6
