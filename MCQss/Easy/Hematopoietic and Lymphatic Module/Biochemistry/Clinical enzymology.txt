Question: Serum acid phosphatase is raised in which condition?
 A. Acute pancreatitis
 B. Acute myocardial infarction
 C. Carcinoma of prostate
 D. Osteoblastic sarcoma
 E. Obstructive jaundice
 Correct Option: C
 Explanation:
 Correct (C): Prostate carcinoma elevates acid phosphatase (classical marker)
 Incorrect (A): Pancreatitis raises amylase/lipase
 Incorrect (B): MI raises CK-MB/troponin
 Incorrect (D): Osteoblastic lesions raise alkaline phosphatase
 Incorrect (E): Obstructive jaundice raises conjugated bilirubin/ALP
