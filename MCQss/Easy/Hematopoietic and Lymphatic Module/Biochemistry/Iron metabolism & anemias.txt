
Question: Erythroblastosis fetalis is caused by:
A. decreased albumin level in blood.
B. hepatitis.
C. increased glucuronyl transferase.
D. Rh incompatibility.
E. viral infection.
Correct Option: D
Explanation:
Correct (D): Rh incompatibility causes maternal anti-Rh antibodies to destroy fetal RBCs, producing erythroblastosis fetalis.
Incorrect (A): decreased albumin level in blood does not cause immune hemolysis of fetal RBCs.
Incorrect (B): hepatitis causes liver inflammation, not erythroblastosis fetalis.
Incorrect (C): increased glucuronyl transferase does not cause fetal hemolytic disease.
Incorrect (E): viral infection is not the classic cause of erythroblastosis fetalis.
