Question: Spleen rests on: Options: a) Splenorenal ligament b) Gastrosplenic ligament c) Phrenicocolic ligament d) 9-12 ribs Correct Option: Ans. Phrenicocolic ligament Explanation: The phrenicocolic ligament (also known as the sustentaculum lienis) is a fold of peritoneum extending from the splenic flexure of the colon to the diaphragm. Question: What is the anatomical location of spleen: Options: a) Left hypochondrium b) Right hypogastrium c) Left flank d) Right flank Correct Option: Ans. Left hypochondrium Explanation: The spleen is located in the left hypochondrium, tucked deep under the protection of the diaphragm. It is entirely covered by the rib cage (specifically ribs 9 through 11), making it non-palpable in a healthy individual unless it is significantly enlarged. Question: Spleen is supplied by splenic artery which is a branch of: Options: a) Superior mesenteric artery b) Right gastroepiploic artery c) Celiac trunk d) Left gastroepiploic artery Correct Option: Ans. Celiac trunk Explanation: The splenic artery is the largest and most tortuous branch of the celiac trunk. It travels along the superior border of the pancreas, eventually entering the lienorenal ligament to reach the splenic hilum, where it divides into several segmental branches. Question: Which of the following ligaments connects the spleen to the left kidney: Options: a) Gastrosplenic ligament b) Lienorenal ligament c) Splenocolic ligament d) Splenophrenic ligament Correct Option: Ans. Lienorenal ligament Explanation: The lienorenal (or splenorenal) ligament is a double fold of peritoneum that connects the hilum of the spleen to the left kidney. It is a vital surgical landmark because it serves as the "highway" for the splenic vessels and the tail of the pancreas. Tags: Easy, Anatomy, hematopoietic module, General Histology & Tissues

Question: Which of the following structures is located anterior to the spleen: Options: a) Left kidney b) Pancreas c) Stomach d) Transverse colon Correct Option: Ans. Stomach Explanation: The stomach is the primary structure located anterior to the spleen. The visceral surface of the spleen actually bears a "gastric impression" where it rests against the fundus and the greater curvature of the stomach. Question: Which of the following is a content of splenorenal ligament: Options: a) Renal artery b) Splenic artery c) Gastroepiploic artery d) Phrenic nerve Correct Option: Ans. Splenic artery Explanation: The splenic artery and vein are the most critical contents of the splenorenal ligament. Surgeons must be extremely careful when clamping this ligament during a splenectomy to avoid accidentally damaging the tail of the pancreas, which also sits within these folds.
