
Question: The left rib that lies parallel to the long axis of spleen: Options: a) 8th b) 9th c) 10th d) 11th Correct Option: Ans. 10th Explanation: The long axis of the spleen lies parallel to the 10th rib. This orientation is clinically significant because as the spleen enlarges (splenomegaly), it typically grows along this axis toward the right iliac fossa, crossing the midline of the abdomen. Question: Posteriorly, spleen is related to which ribs: Options: a) 9th - 11th ribs b) only 9th rib c) 10th - 12th ribs d) only 11th rib Correct Option: Ans. 9th - 11th ribs Explanation: Posteriorly, the spleen is related to the 9th, 10th, and 11th ribs on the left side. It is separated from them only by the diaphragm and the costodiaphragmatic recess of the pleura, which explains why fractured ribs in this area often result in splenic laceration.
