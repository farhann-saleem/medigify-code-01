Question: Nicotinic receptors mimic the action of:
  A. acetylcholine.
  B. adrenaline.
  C. dopamine.
  D. histamine.
  E. noradrenaline.
 Correct Option: A
 Explanation:
 •	Correct (A): Nicotinic receptors are cholinergic receptors that are directly activated by acetylcholine at autonomic ganglia and the neuromuscular junction.
 •	Incorrect (B): Adrenaline acts on adrenergic receptors, not nicotinic receptors.
 •	Incorrect (C): Dopamine acts on dopamine receptors, which are distinct from cholinergic receptors.
 •	Incorrect (D): Histamine acts on histamine receptors and has no direct action on nicotinic receptors.
 •	Incorrect (E): Noradrenaline primarily stimulates adrenergic receptors rather than nicotinic receptors.

Question: Full activation of the parasympathetic nervous system produces:

 •	Incorrect (B): Parasympathetic stimulation increases rather than decreases intestinal motility.
 •	Incorrect (C): Parasympathetic activation slows the heart rate instead of causing increased heart rate (tachycardia).
