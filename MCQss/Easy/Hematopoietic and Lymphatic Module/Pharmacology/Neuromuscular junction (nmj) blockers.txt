Question: Which receptors are present at the neuromuscular end plate:
  A. adrenergic.
  B. muscarinic.
  C. nicotinic.
  D. purinergic.
  E. serotonergic.
 Correct Option: C
 Explanation:
 •	Correct (C): The neuromuscular end plate contains nicotinic receptors that mediate skeletal muscle contraction in response to acetylcholine.
 •	Incorrect (A): Adrenergic receptors are not responsible for neurotransmission at the neuromuscular end plate.
 •	Incorrect (B): Muscarinic receptors are found on autonomic effector organs, not skeletal muscle end plates.
 •	Incorrect (D): Purinergic receptors do not mediate normal neuromuscular transmission.
 •	Incorrect (E): Serotonergic receptors are not present as the primary receptors at the neuromuscular end plate.
