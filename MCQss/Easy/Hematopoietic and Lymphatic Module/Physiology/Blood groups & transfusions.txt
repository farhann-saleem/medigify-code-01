
Question 5
Question: The universal donor blood group is:
A. blood group A.
B. blood group AB−.
C. blood group B.
D. blood group O+.
E. blood group O−.
Correct Option: E
Explanation:
Correct (E): Blood group O− lacks A, B, and Rh antigens, minimizing the risk of recipient antibody reactions.
Incorrect (A): Blood group A expresses A antigen.
Incorrect (B): Blood group AB− expresses both A and B antigens.
Incorrect (C): Blood group B expresses B antigen.
Incorrect (D): Blood group O+ expresses the Rh antigen.

Question 6
Question: Two antigens, type A and type B, are present:
A. as a part of agglutinins.
B. in plasma.
C. on the surface of RBCs.
D. with plasma albumin.
E. within platelets.
Correct Option: C
Explanation:
Correct (C): Type A and type B antigens are membrane antigens located on the surface of RBCs.
Incorrect (A): Agglutinins are antibodies present in plasma.
Incorrect (B): A and B antigens are not dissolved in plasma.
Incorrect (D): Plasma albumin does not carry ABO antigens.
Incorrect (E): ABO typing depends primarily on RBC surface antigens.
