Question: What is the most common type of MSD:
 Options:
 a) Carpal tunnel syndrome
 b) Tendinitis
 c) Back pain
 d) Tennis elbow
 Correct Option:
 Ans. Back Pain
 Explanation: Low back pain is the most prevalent musculoskeletal disorder worldwide, especially due to poor posture and prolonged sitting. It is highly emphasized in occupational health. Other options are specific localized disorders, but back pain affects a larger population.

Question: What is text neck:
 Options:
 a) A type of MSD caused by excessive texting
 b) A type of MSD caused by excessive use of mobile devices
 c) A type of MSD caused by poor posture
 d) A type of MSD caused by repetitive motions
 Correct Option:
 Ans. A type of MSD caused by excessive use of mobile devices
 Explanation: Text neck refers to neck strain from prolonged mobile use, especially looking downward. It combines repetitive strain + posture issues.
 Poor posture is partially true, but the key cause tested is mobile overuse.

Question: Which of the following is NOT a common cause of low back pain:
 Options:
 a) Herniated disc
 b) Osteoporosis
 c) Scoliosis
 d) Carpal tunnel syndrome
 Correct Option:
 Ans. Carpal tunnel syndrome
 Explanation: Carpal tunnel syndrome affects the wrist (median nerve compression), not the lower back. Other options (disc herniation, osteoporosis, scoliosis) directly affect the spine.

 Options:

 Ans. Muscle strain

Question: Which of the following is a common cause of low back pain in older adults:
 Options:
 a) Herniated disc
 b) Osteoporosis
 c) Scoliosis
 d) Muscle strain
 Correct Option:
 Ans. Osteoporosis
 Explanation: In the elderly, bone weakening (osteoporosis) leads to vertebral fractures and back pain. Muscle strain is more common in younger individuals.

Question: What is the epidemiology of MSDs related to mobile addiction:
 Options:
 a) It affects mostly young people
 b) It affects mostly older people
 c) It affects people of all ages
 d) It affects only people who use mobile devices excessively
 Correct Option:
 Ans. It affects mostly young people

Question: The basic definition of ergonomics is:
 Options:
 a) Using a relaxed posture
 b) Fitting the employee to the workstation
 c) Fitting the workstation to the employee
 d) Either B or C
 Correct Option:
 Ans. Fitting the workstation to the employee
 Explanation: Core principle, adapt the environment to the person, not vice versa. Option B is the opposite and incorrect.

Question: Signs and symptoms of MSDs include everything except:
 Options:
 a) Fatigue
 b) Tingling
 c) Boredom
 d) Loss of motion
 Correct Option:
 Ans. Boredom
 Explanation: MSDs cause pain, tingling, stiffness, and loss of movement. Boredom is psychological, not a physical symptom

Question: Ergonomics:
 Options:
 a) Reduces MSDs and absenteeism
 b) Increases worker productivity and morale
 c) Fits the task to the person

 Correct Option:
 Ans. All of the above
