Question: What are non-communicable diseases:
 Options:
 a) Diseases that are transmitted from person to person
 b) Diseases that are transmitted from animals to humans
 c) Diseases that are caused by lifestyle factors
 d) Diseases that are caused by genetic mutations
 Correct Option:
 Ans. Diseases that are caused by lifestyle factors
 Explanation: NCDs are non-infectious, long-term diseases linked to lifestyle (diet, smoking, inactivity). They are not transmitted person-to-person.

Question: What is the most common type of non-communicable disease:
 Options:
 a) Cardiovascular disease
 b) Cancer
 c) Chronic respiratory disease
 d) Diabetes
 Correct Option:
 Ans. Cardiovascular disease
 Explanation: CVDs (e.g., hypertension, ischemic heart disease) are the leading global cause of death. Cancer and diabetes are common but less prevalent overall.

Question: What is the main cause of non-communicable diseases:
 Options:
 a) Genetic factors
 b) Environmental factors
 c) Lifestyle factors
 d) Infectious agents
 Correct Option:
 Ans. Lifestyle factors
 Explanation: Smoking, poor diet, and sedentary life are major contributors. Genetics plays a role, but lifestyle is the dominant modifiable factor.

Question: What is the most common cause of osteoporosis:
 Options:
 a) Aging
 b) Malnutrition
 c) Lack of exercise
 d) Genetics
 Correct Option:
 Ans. Aging
 Explanation: Bone density naturally declines with age, especially post-menopause. Other factors contribute, but aging is the primary cause.
