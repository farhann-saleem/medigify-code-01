
Question: Which drug test is used to diagnose Myasthenia gravis:
A. atropine test
B. pilocarpine test
C. succinylcholine test
D. tensilon test
E. tubocurarine test
Correct Option: D
Explanation:

* ​Correct (D): The tensilon test utilizes edrophonium—a rapid, short-acting acetylcholinesterase inhibitor—to temporarily improve muscle weakness, confirming a diagnosis of Myasthenia gravis.
* ​Incorrect (A): Atropine test assesses sinus bradycardia and parasympathetic vagal tone, lacking any diagnostic utility for neuromuscular junction disorders.
* ​Incorrect (B): Pilocarpine test evaluates exocrine secretory function or pupillary constriction pathways, not somatic motor endplate function.
* ​Incorrect (C): Succinylcholine test would induce painful muscle fasciculations and prolonged paralysis, making it unsafe for diagnosing myasthenia gravis.
* ​Incorrect (E): Tubocurarine test would worsen weakness and cause respiratory failure due to competitive antagonism, making it dangerous and clinically obsolete.

Question: Neostigmine acts on:
A. acetylcholinesterase
B. choline acetyltransferase
C. ganglionic nicotinic receptors
D. muscarinic receptors
E. neuromuscular end plates
Correct Option: A
Explanation:

* ​Correct (A): Neostigmine acts on acetylcholinesterase by reversibly binding and carbamoylating the enzyme, preventing it from breaking down acetylcholine in the synaptic cleft.
* ​Incorrect (B): choline acetyltransferase is the intracellular enzyme responsible for synthesizing acetylcholine from choline and acetyl-CoA, and it is not targeted by neostigmine.
* ​Incorrect (C): ganglionic nicotinic receptors are post-synaptic receptors in the autonomic nervous system that neostigmine does not directly bind to or activate.
* ​Incorrect (D): muscarinic receptors are activated indirectly by the excess acetylcholine built up due to neostigmine, but the drug itself does not directly act on these receptors as its primary mechanism.
* ​Incorrect (E): neuromuscular end plates house nicotinic receptors that experience indirect stimulation from accumulated neurotransmitter, rather than serving as the direct enzymatic target of neostigmine.
