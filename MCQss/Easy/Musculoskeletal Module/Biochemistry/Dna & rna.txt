Question: Which one of the sugars is present in nucleotides:
A. fructose
B. galactose
C. glucose
D. mannose
E. ribose
Correct Option: E
Explanation:
Correct (E): Ribose is the signature aldopentose sugar that constructs the hydrophilic structural backbone of ribonucleotides and ribonucleic acid chains.
Incorrect (A): Fructose is a dietary ketohexose utilized primarily for metabolic energy production via glycolysis rather than structural nucleotide assembly.
Incorrect (B): Galactose is an aldohexose required for glycolipid synthesis and lactose production that does not integrate into genetic nucleotide structures.
Incorrect (C): Glucose is the principal energetic aldohexose of human cellular metabolism but does not directly serve as the structural sugar element inside nucleotides.
Incorrect (D): Mannose is an aldohexose stereoisomer involved primarily in the N-linked glycosylation of proteins rather than the production of core nucleotides.

Question: Sugar present in DNA is:
A. deoxyribose
B. inulin
C. ribose
D. xylose
E. xylulose
Correct Option: A
Explanation:
Correct (A): The specific sugar present in DNA is deoxyribose, a five-carbon monosaccharide that lacks a hydroxyl group at the C2 position to stabilize the phosphodiester backbone.
Incorrect (B): The plant-derived fructan polymer known as inulin serves as a storage carbohydrate and diagnostic marker for glomerular filtration rate rather than a component of nucleic acids.
Incorrect (C): The pentose molecule ribose is structurally distinct because it retains a C2 hydroxyl group, making it the characteristic sugar found exclusively in RNA rather than DNA.
Incorrect (D): The aldopentose xylose is a major structural component of hemicellulose in plant cell walls and is not utilized in the biosynthesis of human genetic material.
Incorrect (E): The ketopentose sugar xylulose functions strictly as an intermediate metabolic reactant within the pentose phosphate pathway and does not integrate into polynucleotide strands.
