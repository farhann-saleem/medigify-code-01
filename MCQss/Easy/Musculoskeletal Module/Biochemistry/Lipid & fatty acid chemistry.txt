Question: Glycerol is the alcohol present in all the following EXCEPT:
A. cardiolipin
B. cephalin
C. lecithin
D. plasmalogens
E. sphingomyelin
Correct Option: E
Explanation:
Correct (E): Sphingomyelin contains a complex, eighteen-carbon amino alcohol backbone termed sphingosine rather than a three-carbon glycerol backbone.
Incorrect (A): Cardiolipin is a specialized mitochondrial diphosphatidylglycerol lipid structured around three distinct glycerol molecules linked via phosphate bridges.
Incorrect (B): Cephalin is a glycerophospholipid structurally composed of a central glycerol backbone esterified to fatty acids and a phosphatidylethanolamine head group.
Incorrect (C): Lecithin is a classic phosphoglyceride built upon a central glycerol skeleton attached to a phosphatidylcholine functional head group.
Incorrect (D): Plasmalogens are ether-linked glycerophospholipids structurally containing a glycerol core anchored to an O-alkyl/O-alkenyl chain.
