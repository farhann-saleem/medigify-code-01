Question: A 77-year-old woman has a fainting episode. Echocardiography shows a 1-cm nodular lesion on the superior aspect of an intact anterior mitral valve leaflet and blood cultures are negative. Which of the following best describes this lesion:
 A. mural thrombus.
 B. myxoma.
 C. phlebothrombosis.
 D. vegetation.
 E. verruca.
 Correct Option: D
 Explanation:
 •	Correct (D): A nodular valvular lesion attached to a valve leaflet is termed a vegetation.
 •	Incorrect (A): Mural thrombi form on chamber walls rather than valve leaflets.
 •	Incorrect (B): Myxoma is a cardiac tumor, usually arising in the atrium.
 •	Incorrect (C): Phlebothrombosis refers to venous thrombosis.
 •	Incorrect (E): Verruca is a specific type of vegetation seen in rheumatic disease.
 Tags: Hard, Pathology, Cardiovascular, Systemic Pathology: Cardiovascular
