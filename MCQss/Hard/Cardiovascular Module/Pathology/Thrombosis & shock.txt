Question: In an experiment on vascular injury, propagation of thrombus to adjacent normal vessels is prevented by activation of protein C. Which of the following substances is responsible:
 A. calcium.
 B. fibrin.
 C. prothrombin.
 D. thrombomodulin.
 E. von Willebrand factor.
 Correct Option: D
 Explanation:
 •	Correct (D): Thrombomodulin binds thrombin and activates protein C, producing anticoagulant effects.
 •	Incorrect (A): Calcium participates in coagulation rather than activating protein C.
 •	Incorrect (B): Fibrin forms the clot matrix and promotes thrombosis.
 •	Incorrect (C): Prothrombin is a clotting factor precursor.
 •	Incorrect (E): von Willebrand factor mediates platelet adhesion.
