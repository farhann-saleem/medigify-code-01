Question: Why is moderate fluid retention by the kidneys considered a useful compensatory mechanism during the subacute phase of compensated heart failure?
 A. It decreases myocardial oxygen demand directly
 B. It increases mean systemic filling pressure to improve venous return
 C. It abolishes pulmonary capillary pressure gradients
 D. It causes direct vasodilation of coronary arteries
 E. It reduces adrenal catecholamine secretion
 Correct Option: B
 Explanation:
 • Correct (B): Increased blood volume raises mean systemic filling pressure, improving venous return and supporting stroke volume via Frank-Starling mechanism.
 • Incorrect (A): Oxygen demand is not directly reduced by renal fluid retention.
 • Incorrect (C): Pulmonary capillary gradients are not abolished by volume expansion.
 • Incorrect (D): No direct coronary vasodilation occurs from fluid retention.
 • Incorrect (E): Catecholamine secretion is not reduced; it is usually increased in compensation.
