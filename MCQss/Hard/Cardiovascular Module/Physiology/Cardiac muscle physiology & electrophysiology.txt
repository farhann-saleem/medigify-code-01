Question 17
During a cardiac surgical procedure, the patient's heart enters ventricular fibrillation. According to the biophysical principles of re-entry pathways (circus movements), which set of conditions directly promotes and sustains ventricular fibrillation?
a. lengthened pathway length, decreased conduction velocity, and shortened refractory period
b. shortened pathway length, increased conduction velocity, and prolonged refractory period
c. shortened pathway length, reduced conduction velocity, and prolonged refractory period
d. total block of calcium influx combined with accelerated sodium pump activity
e. uniform conduction velocity with complete absence of refractory recovery
Correct Option: a
Explanation:
Correct (a): Lengthened pathway length, decreased conduction velocity, and shortened refractory period favor sustained re-entry pathways (circus movements).
Incorrect (b): Increased conduction velocity and prolonged refractory period suppress re-entry.
Incorrect (c): A shortened pathway length reduces the likelihood of maintaining a re-entry circuit.
Incorrect (d): Calcium influx blockade and sodium pump activity do not define the classic requirements for ventricular fibrillation.
Incorrect (e): Re-entry requires recovery of excitability rather than complete absence of refractory recovery.
