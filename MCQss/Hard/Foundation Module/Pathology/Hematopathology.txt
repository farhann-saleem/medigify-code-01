Question: Which of the following pathological states combines a defect in primary hemostatic platelet function with an associated defect in secondary coagulation cascade kinetics?
 A. Factor V deficiency
 B. Factor VIII deficiency
 C. Hemophilia A
 D. Hemophilia B
 E. Von Willebrand disease
 Correct Option: E
 Explanation:
 •	Correct (E): Von Willebrand disease impairs platelet adhesion and reduces stabilization of Factor VIII.
 •	Incorrect (A): Factor V deficiency affects only secondary hemostasis.
 •	Incorrect (B): Factor VIII deficiency primarily affects secondary hemostasis.
 •	Incorrect (C): Hemophilia A is a coagulation factor disorder without a primary platelet defect.
 •	Incorrect (D): Hemophilia B is an isolated Factor IX deficiency.
