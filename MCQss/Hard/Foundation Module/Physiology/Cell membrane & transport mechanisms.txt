
Question 12
Question: Which mechanism explains why a substance moves against its electrochemical gradient without direct ATP consumption?
A. Facilitated diffusion
B. Primary active transport
C. Receptor-mediated endocytosis
D. Secondary active transport coupled to Na⁺ or H⁺ gradients
E. Simple diffusion
Correct Option: D
Explanation:
Correct (D): Secondary active transport coupled to Na⁺ or H⁺ gradients uses stored gradient energy rather than direct ATP hydrolysis.
Incorrect (A): Facilitated diffusion cannot move substances against a gradient.
Incorrect (B): Primary active transport directly consumes ATP.
Incorrect (C): Receptor-mediated endocytosis transports vesicles rather than using ion gradients.
Incorrect (E): Simple diffusion only moves down a gradient.

Question 16
Question: Which transporter is responsible for counter-transport of Ca²⁺ using the Na⁺ gradient in cardiac cells?
A. Ca²⁺ ATPase pump
B. Glucose transporter
C. Na⁺/Ca²⁺ exchanger
D. Na⁺/K⁺ ATPase
E. Na⁺-glucose symporter
Correct Option: C
Explanation:
Correct (C): The Na⁺/Ca²⁺ exchanger uses the Na⁺ gradient to extrude Ca²⁺ from cardiac cells.
Incorrect (A): Ca²⁺ ATPase uses ATP rather than the Na⁺ gradient.
Incorrect (B): Glucose transporters do not transport Ca²⁺.
Incorrect (D): Na⁺/K⁺ ATPase does not exchange Ca²⁺.
Incorrect (E): Na⁺-glucose symport transports glucose, not Ca²⁺.

Question 17
Question: Which factor has the greatest influence on the rate of simple diffusion of a solute across a lipid bilayer?
A. Concentration gradient and membrane thickness
B. Lipid solubility and membrane thickness
C. Membrane electrical potential
D. Number of carrier proteins
E. Surface receptor density
Correct Option: B
Explanation:
Correct (B): Lipid solubility and membrane thickness are the major determinants of simple diffusion across a lipid bilayer.
Incorrect (A): Concentration gradient is important but lipid solubility is the dominant membrane property.
Incorrect (C): Membrane electrical potential mainly affects charged ions.
Incorrect (D): Carrier proteins are not involved in simple diffusion.
Incorrect (E): Surface receptor density does not influence diffusion rate.
