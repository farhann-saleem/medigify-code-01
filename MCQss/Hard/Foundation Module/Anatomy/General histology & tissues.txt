A. A few pinocytotic vesicles 
B. Discontinuous basal lamina 
C. Fenestrae with diaphragms 
D. Fenestrae without diaphragms 
E. Wide intercellular clefts 
Correct Option: A 
Explanation:
* Correct (A): A few pinocytotic vesicles within the endothelial cytoplasm restricts the transcellular transport of large or hydrophilic molecules, ensuring structural integrity of the blood-brain barrier.
* Incorrect (B): Discontinuous basal lamina would cause high permeability, whereas blood-brain barrier capillaries possess a continuous and well-developed basal lamina.
* Incorrect (C): Fenestrae with diaphragms are characteristic of leaky visceral capillaries, which contradicts the tight barrier function of central nervous system capillaries.
* Incorrect (D): Fenestrae without diaphragms allow free diffusion of macromolecular substances, which is the exact opposite of blood-brain barrier restriction.
* Incorrect (E): Wide intercellular clefts would permit paracellular leakage, whereas blood-brain barrier capillaries are fused together by elaborate tight junctions (zonulae occludentes).
