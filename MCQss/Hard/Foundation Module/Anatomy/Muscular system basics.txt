Question 17. Which of the following is not an interosseous ligament?
 
A. Coracoclavicular ligament
B. Interosseous talocalcaneal ligament
C. Ligamentum flavum
D. Stylohyoid ligament
E. Tibiofibular interosseous ligament
 
Correct Option: D
Explanation:
 
Correct (D): Stylohyoid ligament is not interosseous; it connects styloid process to hyoid.
Incorrect (A): Coracoclavicular acts as interosseous stabilizer of scapula-clavicle.
Incorrect (B): Talocalcaneal interosseous ligament is true interosseous structure.
Incorrect (C): Ligamentum flavum connects vertebral laminae.
Incorrect (E): Tibiofibular interosseous ligament connects tibia and fibula.

Question 18. Which of the following is not a synchondrosis?
 
A. Between ethmoid and sphenoid
B. Between occipital and petrous temporal bone
C. Epiphyseal plate of a long bone
D. Manubriosternal joint
E. Spheno-occipital synchondrosis
 
Correct Option: D
Explanation:
 
Correct (D): Manubriosternal joint is a symphysis, not a synchondrosis.
Incorrect (A): Cranial base synchondrosis.
Incorrect (B): Cranial base synchondrosis.
Incorrect (C): Epiphyseal plate is synchondrosis.
Incorrect (E): Spheno-occipital is synchondrosis.
