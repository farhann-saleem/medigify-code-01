Question: Which of the following statements about nucleotide structure is NOT CORRECT?
 A. 3’,5’-cyclic AMP and GMP (cAMP and cGMP) serve as second messengers in human biochemistry.
 B. Caffeine and theobromine differ structurally solely with respect to the number of methyl groups attached to their ring nitrogen.
 C. NAD+, FMN, active methionine, and coenzyme A are derivatives of ribonucleotides.
 D. Nucleotides are polyfunctional acids.
 E. The atoms of the purine ring portion are numbered in the same direction as those of a pyrimidine.
 Correct Option: E
 Explanation:
 •	Correct (E): Purine and pyrimidine rings follow different numbering conventions.
 •	Incorrect (A): cAMP and cGMP are established intracellular second messengers.
 •	Incorrect (B): Caffeine contains one more methyl group than theobromine.
 •	Incorrect (C): These compounds contain ribonucleotide-derived components.
 •	Incorrect (D): Nucleotides contain multiple functional groups and behave as polyfunctional acids.
