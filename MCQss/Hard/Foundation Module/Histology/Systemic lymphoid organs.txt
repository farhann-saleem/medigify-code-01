Question: All of the following histological features are found in palatine tonsils except:
A. Connective tissue capsules.
B. Efferent lymphatic vessels.
C. Moist stratified squamous epithelium.
D. Non-keratinized epithelial crypts.
E. Secondary lymphatic nodules.
Correct Option: B
Explanation:
Correct (B): Palatine tonsils are unencapsulated or partially encapsulated mucosal structures that contain no afferent or efferent lymphatic vessels within their functional parenchyma.
Incorrect (A): A thick, distinct connective tissue capsule separates the deep aspect of the palatine tonsil from underlying structures.
Incorrect (C): The luminal surface of the palatine tonsil is characteristically lined by moist stratified squamous epithelium.
Incorrect (D): The surface lining invaginates deeply into the underlying lymphoid tissue to form prominent non-keratinized epithelial crypts.
Incorrect (E): The subepithelial lymphoid tissue contains abundant secondary lymphatic nodules characterized by active germinal centers.

Question: The thymus is described by each of the following except:
A. it possesses no afferent lymphatic vessels.
B. the cortex is less vascular than the medulla.
C. the medulla contains Hassall corpuscles.
D. the medulla contains epithelioreticular cells.
E. the stroma lacks a network of reticular fibers.
Correct Option: B
Explanation:
Correct (B): The thymic cortex is highly vascularized to support rapid T-cell proliferation and establish the blood-thymus barrier, making it more vascular than the medulla.
Incorrect (A): As a primary lymphoid organ that does not filter lymph, the thymus possesses no afferent lymphatic vessels.
Incorrect (C): Concentric arrangements of type VI epithelioreticular cells uniquely form Hassall corpuscles within the thymic medulla.
Incorrect (D): Type IV, V, and VI epithelioreticular cells are structural components that form the cytoreticulum within the thymic medulla.
Incorrect (E): Unlike secondary lymphoid organs, the thymic stroma relies on an epithelial cytoreticulum and completely lacks a network of reticular fibers.

Question 2
All of the following histological features are found in palatine tonsils except:
A. covered by a connective tissue layer
B. epitheloid reticular framework
C. moist stratified squamous epithelium
D. well-developed tonsillar crypts
E. unencapsulated lymphatic nodules
Correct Option: A
Explanation:
Correct (A): covered by a connective tissue layer is the exception because the palatine tonsils are covered on their deep, attached surface by a thick, distinct connective tissue capsule, not a simple layer.
Incorrect (B): epitheloid reticular framework is an accurate feature found in the thymus, not the palatine tonsils, making it structurally distinct.
Incorrect (C): moist stratified squamous epithelium accurately describes the luminal surface lining that covers the palatine tonsils.
Incorrect (D): well-developed tonsillar crypts are classic invaginating histological structures deeply penetrating the parenchyma of the palatine tonsils.
Incorrect (E): unencapsulated lymphatic nodules are the primary structural units that populate the lamina propria framework of the palatine tonsils.

Question 4
The thymus is described by each of the following except:
A. the cortex is more vascular than the medulla
B. the medulla contains Hassall's bodies
C. the medulla contains reticular cells
D. the medulla is more vascular than the cortex
E. there are absolutely no afferent lymphatics
Correct Option: D
Explanation:
Correct (D): the medulla is more vascular than the cortex is the exception because the thymic cortex actually maintains a significantly higher vascularity than the medulla to support intense T-cell proliferation and the blood-thymus barrier.
Incorrect (A): the cortex is more vascular than the medulla correctly describes the microvascular distribution needed to sustain high metabolic activity during positive selection.
Incorrect (B): the medulla contains Hassall's bodies is a true, classic diagnostic feature of the thymic medulla composed of concentric epithelial reticular cells.
Incorrect (C): the medulla contains reticular cells accurately reflects the presence of epithelial reticular cells supporting the thymic stromal architecture.
Incorrect (E): there are absolutely no afferent lymphatics is a true description since the thymus is a primary lymphoid organ that only possesses efferent lymphatics for lymphocyte exit.
