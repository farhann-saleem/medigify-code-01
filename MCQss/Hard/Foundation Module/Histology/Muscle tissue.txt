Question: A transverse sarcomere section through the I-band of a skeletal muscle would include:
A. Actin myofilaments only.
B. Both actin and myosin myofilaments.
C. Myosin myofilaments only.
D. Nebulin myofilaments only.
E. Z-lines and actin myofilaments.
Correct Option: A
Explanation:
Correct (A): The I-band represents the light band of the sarcomere and contains actin myofilaments only, without overlapping thick filaments.
Incorrect (B): Both actin and myosin myofilaments overlap exclusively within the lateral regions of the A-band.
Incorrect (C): Myosin myofilaments only are found within the central H-zone of the A-band.
Incorrect (D): Nebulin myofilaments only do not exist independently here, as nebulin runs alongside and stabilizes actin filaments rather than forming a distinct zone.
Incorrect (E): A transverse section through the I-band excludes the Z-lines, which bound the edges of the sarcomere rather than the interior of the I-band itself.

Question: Each of the following statements concerning smooth muscle cells is true except:
A. they contain many mitochondria.
B. they contain stable thick filaments.
C. they do not contain sarcomeres.
D. they exhibit automaticity features.
E. they have functional gap junctions.
Correct Option: B
Explanation:
Correct (B): Smooth muscle cells do not contain stable thick filaments; instead, their thick filaments are dynamic and undergo regulated assembly and disassembly during structural adjustments.
Incorrect (A): Smooth muscle cells contain many mitochondria to generate the ATP required to sustain prolonged, low-energy contractions.
Incorrect (C): Smooth muscle cells do not contain sarcomeres, which accounts for their lack of visible microscopic striations.
Incorrect (D): Smooth muscle cells exhibit automaticity features, allowing them to initiate spontaneous rhythmic contractions in organs like the gut.
Incorrect (E): Smooth muscle cells have functional gap junctions that allow direct ion passing to coordinate synchronized contractions across the tissue sheet.

Question: The neuromuscular junction is a specialized form of synaptic contact between motor neuron axon terminals and muscle. Which of the following properties distinguishes it from a central nervous system synapse:
A. the presence of a localized postsynaptic receptor membrane.
B. the presence of a continuous structural synaptic cleft.
C. the presence of deep junctional folds of the postsynaptic membrane.
D. the presence of dense synaptic vesicles within the presynaptic axon.
E. the presence of electrical gap junctions in the synaptic area.
Correct Option: C
Explanation:
Correct (C): The presence of deep junctional folds of the postsynaptic membrane is a unique structural specialization of the sarcolemma at the motor endplate designed to increase acetylcholine receptor density.
Incorrect (A): The presence of a localized postsynaptic receptor membrane is a feature shared by both neuromuscular junctions and central nervous system synapses to catch chemical transmitters.
Incorrect (B): The presence of a continuous structural synaptic cleft is a universal feature of all chemical synapses in both peripheral and central nervous systems.
Incorrect (D): The presence of dense synaptic vesicles within the presynaptic axon is a standard characteristic across all neurotransmitter-releasing neurons.
Incorrect (E): The presence of electrical gap junctions in the synaptic area characterizes electrical synapses, whereas the neuromuscular junction is strictly a chemical synapse.
