Question: Mental deficiency, retarded growth, slanting eyes, small ears, neural tube defects, and cardiac defects are the characteristic findings in?
A. Down syndrome
B. Edwards syndrome
C. Patau syndrome
D. Turner syndrome
E. Wolf-Hirschhorn syndrome
Correct Option: C
Explanation:
• Correct (C): Patau syndrome (trisomy 13) causes severe intellectual disability, growth retardation, small ears, neural tube defects, and congenital cardiac defects.
• Incorrect (A): Down syndrome commonly presents with hypotonia and atrioventricular septal defects but neural tube defects are not characteristic.
• Incorrect (B): Edwards syndrome is marked by clenched fists and rocker-bottom feet rather than slanting eyes with neural tube defects.
• Incorrect (D): Turner syndrome causes short stature and gonadal dysgenesis without severe neural tube defects.
• Incorrect (E): Wolf-Hirschhorn syndrome produces “Greek warrior helmet” facies rather than the multisystem pattern typical of trisomy 13.

8. Question: Concentration of α-fetoprotein higher than normal in the amniotic fluid may indicate?
 A. Gastroschisis
 B. Neural tube defect
 C. Omphalocele
 D. Sacrococcygeal teratoma
 E. Spina bifida
 Correct Option: B
 Explanation:
 • Correct (B): Neural tube defect causes leakage of α-fetoprotein into amniotic fluid, raising levels.
 • Incorrect (A): Gastroschisis elevates maternal serum α-fetoprotein but not amniotic fluid consistently.
 • Incorrect (C): Omphalocele involves herniation but does not specifically raise α-fetoprotein.
 • Incorrect (D): Sacrococcygeal teratoma alters growth but not α-fetoprotein levels.
 • Incorrect (E): Spina bifida is a neural tube defect subset but the broader category is the key.
