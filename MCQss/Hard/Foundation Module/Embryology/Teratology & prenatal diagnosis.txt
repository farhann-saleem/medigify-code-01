Question: A 42-year-old woman presents with complaints of severe headaches, blurred vision, slurred speech, and loss of muscle coordination. Her last pregnancy 5 years ago resulted in a hydatidiform mole. Laboratory results show a high hCG level. Which of the following conditions is a probable diagnosis:
A. choriocarcinoma.
B. hydatidiform mole.
C. membranous placenta.
D. placenta previa.
E. vasa previa.
Correct Option: A
Explanation:
Correct (A): Choriocarcinoma is a malignant trophoblastic tumor associated with markedly elevated hCG and hematogenous metastasis causing neurologic symptoms.
Incorrect (B): Hydatidiform mole usually presents during pregnancy with uterine enlargement and bleeding rather than metastatic neurologic manifestations years later.
Incorrect (C): Membranous placenta is a placental implantation abnormality without elevated hCG or metastatic spread.
Incorrect (D): Placenta previa causes painless vaginal bleeding in pregnancy, not neurologic deficits or persistent hCG elevation.
Incorrect (E): Vasa previa presents with fetal bleeding after membrane rupture, unrelated to trophoblastic malignancy.

Question: Concentration of alpha fetoprotein less than normal in the amniotic fluid may indicate:
A. CNS defect
B. colorectal cancer
C. down syndrome
D. liver cancer
E. neural tube defect
Correct Option: C
Explanation:
 * Correct (C): Decreased maternal serum or amniotic fluid alpha fetoprotein levels are classically associated with chromosomal abnormalities such as Down syndrome.
 * Incorrect (A): A CNS defect typically presents with elevated, rather than decreased, alpha fetoprotein levels due to leakage from the open central nervous system.
 * Incorrect (B): Colorectal cancer is associated with elevated carcinoembryonic antigen (CEA) levels in adults, not decreased alpha fetoprotein in amniotic fluid.
 * Incorrect (D): Liver cancer (hepatocellular carcinoma) is characterized by highly elevated alpha fetoprotein levels rather than a less than normal concentration.
 * Incorrect (E): A neural tube defect causes a leakage of fetal serum into the amniotic cavity, resulting in increased alpha fetoprotein levels.

Question: Concentration of alpha fetoprotein higher than normal in the amniotic fluid may indicate:
A. colorectal cancer
B. down syndrome
C. neural tube defect
D. poland syndrome
E. turner syndrome
Correct Option: C
Explanation:
 * Correct (C): An open neural tube defect allows alpha fetoprotein to leak directly from the fetal circulation into the surrounding amniotic fluid, elevating its concentration.
 * Incorrect (A): While colorectal cancer can express embryonic antigens in adults, it does not cause an elevation of alpha fetoprotein in prenatal amniotic fluid.
 * Incorrect (B): Down syndrome is characteristically associated with a lower than normal concentration of alpha fetoprotein in maternal serum and amniotic fluid.
 * Incorrect (D): Poland syndrome is a unilateral chest wall and limb anomaly that does not alter the systemic or amniotic fluid concentration of alpha fetoprotein.
 * Incorrect (E): Turner syndrome is a chromosomal abnormality that typically presents with decreased or normal alpha fetoprotein levels, not an elevated concentration.

Question: A 32-year-old pregnant woman at 30 weeks of gestation comes to her physician because of excess weight gain in a 2-week period. Ultrasonography reveals polyhydramnios. Which fetal abnormality is most likely responsible for the polyhydramnios: Options: a) Bilateral kidney agenesis b) Velamentous placenta c) Hypoplastic lungs d) Esophageal atresia Correct Option: Ans. Esophageal atresia Explanation: Polyhydramnios occurs when the fetus is unable to swallow amniotic fluid. In esophageal atresia, the esophagus is interrupted, so swallowed fluid cannot reach the stomach and be absorbed. This leads to accumulation of amniotic fluid in the uterine cavity.
