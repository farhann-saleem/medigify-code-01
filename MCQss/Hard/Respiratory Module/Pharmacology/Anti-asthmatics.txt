Question 15
 Question: Drug of choice for status asthmaticus:
 A. Antihistamine
  B. Inhaled corticosteroid
  C. Intravenous corticosteroid
  D. Long-acting β2 agonist
  E. Short-acting β2 agonist
 Correct Option: C
 Explanation:
 •	Correct (C): Intravenous corticosteroid is essential in status asthmaticus to suppress severe airway inflammation.
 •	Incorrect (A): Antihistamines have no major role in status asthmaticus.
 •	Incorrect (B): Inhaled corticosteroid is insufficient for severe life-threatening attacks.
 •	Incorrect (D): Long-acting β2 agonists are not emergency treatment.
 •	Incorrect (E): Short-acting β2 agonists are important but do not replace systemic corticosteroid therapy.

Question 19
 Question: A 9-year-old girl who required three hospitalizations during the past year for asthma has experienced a marked reduction in severe attacks after starting a new medication. Which drug is most likely responsible?
 A. Inhaled albuterol
  B. Inhaled fluticasone
  C. Inhaled ipratropium
  D. Oral theophylline
  E. Oral zafirlukast
 Correct Option: B
 Explanation:
 •	Correct (B): Inhaled fluticasone reduces airway inflammation and significantly decreases severe asthma exacerbations.
 •	Incorrect (A): Inhaled albuterol provides symptom relief but does not prevent severe attacks effectively.
 •	Incorrect (C): Inhaled ipratropium is mainly a bronchodilator adjunct.
 •	Incorrect (D): Oral theophylline has limited prophylactic benefit and significant toxicity.
 •	Incorrect (E): Oral zafirlukast is useful but generally less effective than inhaled corticosteroids.
 Tags: Hard, Pharmacology, Respiratory Pathology, Anti-asthmatics
