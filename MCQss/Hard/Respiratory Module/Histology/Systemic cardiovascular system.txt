Question: In large elastic arteries, each of the following is correct except:
A. endothelium is composed of a single layer of cells.
B. there is a thin basement membrane.
C. tunica adventitia is thin.
D. tunica intima lacks a subendothelial layer.
E. tunica media consists largely of elastic tissue.
Correct Option: D
Explanation:
Correct (D): The statement is false because the tunica intima of large elastic arteries possesses a well-developed, thick subendothelial layer containing collagen, elastic fibers, and smooth muscle cells.
Incorrect (A): It is a true statement that the endothelium is composed of a single layer of cells forming the standard vascular luminal boundary.
Incorrect (B): It is true that there is a thin basement membrane directly underlying and supporting the simple squamous endothelial layer.
Incorrect (C): It is true that the tunica adventitia is thin relative to the massive, expanded thickness of the adjacent tunica media.
Incorrect (E): It is true that the tunica media consists largely of elastic tissue organized into numerous fenestrated elastic lamellae.

Question: Sinusoids are correctly described by each of the following except they:
A. are lined by endothelial cells.
B. have a dense connective tissue wall.
C. have an irregular, tortuous lumen.
D. have large intercellular gaps.
E. have no phagocytic elements.
Correct Option: B
Explanation:
Correct (B): This statement is false because sinusoids have an extremely delicate structural wall supported by reticular fibers, completely lacking a dense connective tissue wall.
Incorrect (A): It is true that they are lined by endothelial cells that exhibit specialized fenestrations and large intercellular spaces.
Incorrect (C): It is true that sinusoids have an irregular, tortuous lumen that slows blood flow to maximize metabolic exchange in organs like the liver.
Incorrect (D): It is true that they have large intercellular gaps that permit the unhindered passage of large macromolecules and plasma proteins.
Incorrect (E): This statement is factually incorrect because tissue-specific macrophages (like Kupffer cells in liver sinusoids) are embedded within or directly associated with the wall.

Question: Which of the following statements about fenestrated capillaries is true:
A. Fenestrations are 150-200 nm in diameter.
B. Their basal lamina is discontinuous.
C. They are present in exocrine glands.
D. They lack endothelial diaphragms entirely.
E. They may be partially surrounded by pericytes.
Correct Option: C
Explanation:
Correct (C): Fenestrated capillaries are present in exocrine glands, endocrine glands, and intestinal mucosa to facilitate rapid macromolecular and fluid secretion/absorption.
Incorrect (A): The endothelial fenestrations are typically smaller, measuring approximately 60–80 nm rather than being 150-200 nm in diameter.
Incorrect (B): Their basal lamina is discontinuous is false; they maintain a continuous, fully intact basal lamina across all fenestrated regions except in renal glomeruli.
Incorrect (D): They lack endothelial diaphragms entirely is false; their fenestrations are typically spanned by thin, bridging endothelial diaphragms, with the renal glomerulus being the main exception.
Incorrect (E): They may be partially surrounded by pericytes is a defining characteristic of continuous capillaries rather than fenestrated capillaries.
Tags: Hard, Histology, Respiratory, Systemic: Cardiovascular System
