

Question: If compliance is normal but airway resistance increases, breathing becomes:
A. fast and deep
B. fast and shallow
C. normal
D. slow and deep
E. slow and shallow
Correct Option: D
Explanation:

Correct (D): To minimize the work of breathing against an increased airway resistance, the
respiratory system optimizes efficiency by adopting a slow and deep breathing pattern.

Incorrect (A): A fast and deep pattern would markedly elevate turbulent airflow and frictional
work, severely exacerbating the increased resistance.

Incorrect (B): A fast and shallow pattern is typically adopted to minimize elastic work when
lung compliance is reduced, not when airway resistance is elevated.

Incorrect (C): Breathing cannot remain normal because the metabolic work required to shift air
against elevated resistance forces a compensatory change in pattern.

Incorrect (E): A slow and shallow pattern would result in severe alveolar hypoventilation due
to anatomical dead space taking up too much of the small tidal volume.
