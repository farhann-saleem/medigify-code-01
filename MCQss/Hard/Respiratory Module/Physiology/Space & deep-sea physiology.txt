
Question 6:
 Acclimatization to high altitude causes all of the following except?
 A. Angiogenesis
 B. Increased pulmonary ventilation
 C. Polycythemia
 D. Reduced cellular oxidative enzymes
 E. Upregulation of oxidative enzymes
 Correct Option: D
 Explanation:
 • Correct (D): Reduced cellular oxidative enzymes is not part of acclimatization; enzymes are upregulated.
 • Incorrect (A): Angiogenesis occurs to improve tissue oxygen delivery.
 • Incorrect (B): Increased pulmonary ventilation is a key adaptation to hypoxia.
 • Incorrect (C): Polycythemia develops to enhance oxygen transport capacity.
 • Incorrect (E): Upregulation of oxidative enzymes supports efficient cellular metabolism under hypoxia.

Question 7:
 In chronic mountain sickness, which of the following does not occur?
 A. Acute pulmonary edema
 B. Increased RBC mass
 C. Pulmonary vasoconstriction
 D. Right heart hypertrophy
 E. Secondary polycythemia
 Correct Option: A
 Explanation:
 • Correct (A): Acute pulmonary edema is not a feature of chronic mountain sickness; it is an acute altitude illness.
 • Incorrect (B): Increased RBC mass is a classic finding due to erythropoietin stimulation.
 • Incorrect (C): Pulmonary vasoconstriction occurs from hypoxic pulmonary response.
 • Incorrect (D): Right heart hypertrophy develops secondary to pulmonary hypertension.
 • Incorrect (E): Secondary polycythemia is a hallmark of chronic mountain sickness.
