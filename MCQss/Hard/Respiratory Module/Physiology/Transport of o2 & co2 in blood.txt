
Question 2:
 Which of the following condition prevents tissues from utilizing oxygen despite adequate oxygen supply?
 A. Carbon monoxide poisoning
 B. Cyanide poisoning
 C. Decompression sickness
 D. Gas gangrene
 E. Hydrogen sulfide poisoning
 Correct Option: B
 Explanation:
 • Correct (B): Cyanide poisoning blocks cytochrome oxidase, preventing tissue oxygen utilization.
 • Incorrect (A): Carbon monoxide poisoning reduces oxygen delivery by binding Hb.
 • Incorrect (C): Decompression sickness causes gas emboli, not direct inhibition of oxygen use.
 • Incorrect (D): Gas gangrene impairs perfusion, not mitochondrial oxygen utilization.
 • Incorrect (E): Hydrogen sulfide poisoning depresses respiration but does not primarily block cytochrome oxidase.
