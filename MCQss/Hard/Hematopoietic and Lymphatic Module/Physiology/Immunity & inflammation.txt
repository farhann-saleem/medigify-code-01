
Question 9
Question: In severe combined immunodeficiency (SCID), a major complication of bone marrow transplantation is:
A. acute renal failure.
B. cardiovascular failure.
C. delayed-onset anaphylaxis.
D. graft-versus-host disease.
E. hemorrhagic shock.
Correct Option: D
Explanation:
Correct (D): Graft-versus-host disease occurs when donor T lymphocytes attack recipient tissues after bone marrow transplantation.
Incorrect (A): Acute renal failure is not the characteristic immune complication.
Incorrect (B): Cardiovascular failure is not a typical transplant complication.
Incorrect (C): Delayed-onset anaphylaxis is unrelated to bone marrow transplantation.
Incorrect (E): Hemorrhagic shock is not the major immune complication.

Question 15
Question: A patient undergoes a kidney transplant. After 48 hours, there is oliguria and increasing serum creatinine. The most likely cause is:
A. acute cellular rejection mediated by T lymphocytes.
B. chronic rejection due to fibrosis.
C. delayed hypersensitivity reaction.
D. hyperacute rejection due to preformed anti-donor antibodies.
E. ischemia-reperfusion injury.
Correct Option: A
Explanation:
Correct (A): Acute cellular rejection mediated by T lymphocytes typically develops within days after transplantation.
Incorrect (B): Chronic rejection due to fibrosis develops over months to years.
Incorrect (C): Delayed hypersensitivity reaction is not the standard classification of transplant rejection.
Incorrect (D): Hyperacute rejection occurs immediately after vascular anastomosis.
Incorrect (E): Ischemia-reperfusion injury is an early nonimmune complication.

Question 16
Question: Hyperacute rejection of a transplanted organ occurs immediately after vascular anastomosis. The underlying immune mechanism is:
A. immune complex deposition.
B. natural killer cell activation.
C. preformed antibodies against donor antigens activate complement.
D. T-cell-mediated delayed hypersensitivity.
E. T-regulatory cell dysfunction.
Correct Option: C
Explanation:
Correct (C): Hyperacute rejection occurs when preformed antibodies against donor antigens activate complement immediately after reperfusion.
Incorrect (A): Immune complex deposition is characteristic of Type III hypersensitivity.
Incorrect (B): Natural killer cell activation is not the primary mechanism.
Incorrect (D): T-cell-mediated delayed hypersensitivity develops later.
Incorrect (E): T-regulatory cell dysfunction is not responsible for hyperacute rejection.

Question 17
Question: Graft-versus-host disease occurs most commonly after bone marrow transplantation because:
A. complement activation destroys the graft.
B. donor T lymphocytes attack immunocompromised host tissues.
C. donor plasma antibodies attack host RBCs.
D. recipient B lymphocytes attack donor tissue.
E. recipient T cells attack donor tissue.
Correct Option: B
Explanation:
Correct (B): Graft-versus-host disease occurs when donor T lymphocytes attack immunocompromised host tissues.
Incorrect (A): Complement activation does not produce graft-versus-host disease.
Incorrect (C): Donor plasma antibodies are not the principal cause.
Incorrect (D): Recipient B lymphocytes do not mediate graft-versus-host disease.
Incorrect (E): Recipient T cells mediate graft rejection, not graft-versus-host disease.
