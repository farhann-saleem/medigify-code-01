
Question: All of the intrinsic small muscles of the hand receive their primary dermatomal and myotomal contributions from the spinal root of:
A. C5.
B. C6.
C. C7.
D. C8.
E. T1.
Correct Option: E
Explanation:
 * **Correct (E):** The T1 spinal nerve root provides the foundational myotomal contribution responsible for the motor innervation of all intrinsic hand muscles via the ulnar and median nerves.
 * **Incorrect (A):** The C5 spinal root primarily contributes to muscles controlling movements around the shoulder joint, such as abduction.
 * **Incorrect (B):** The C6 spinal root heavily mediates elbow flexion and wrist extension myotomes.
 * **Incorrect (C):** The C7 spinal root is the predominant driver for elbow extension and wrist flexion movements.
 * **Incorrect (D):** The C8 spinal root contributes heavily to finger flexion (long flexors in the forearm) rather than the localized intrinsic hand muscle group myotome.
