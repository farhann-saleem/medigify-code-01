
Question: Porphyria cutanea tarda is due to defect in:
A. coproporphyrinogen oxidase.
B. ferrochelatase.
C. protoporphyrinogen oxidase.
D. uroporphyrinogen decarboxylase.
E. uroporphyrinogen synthase.
Correct Option: D
Explanation:
Correct (D): uroporphyrinogen decarboxylase deficiency causes porphyria cutanea tarda with photosensitivity and uroporphyrin accumulation.
Incorrect (A): coproporphyrinogen oxidase deficiency causes hereditary coproporphyria, not porphyria cutanea tarda.
Incorrect (B): ferrochelatase deficiency causes erythropoietic protoporphyria, not porphyria cutanea tarda.
Incorrect (C): protoporphyrinogen oxidase deficiency causes variegate porphyria, not porphyria cutanea tarda.
Incorrect (E): uroporphyrinogen synthase is not the defective enzyme in porphyria cutanea tarda.

﻿Question: Porphyrins emit red fluorescence because of:
A. acetate groups
B. double bonds joining two pyrrole rings
C. iron bonded to globin
D. methyl groups
E. propionate groups
Correct Option: B
Explanation:
 * Correct (B): The extensive conjugation provided by the double bonds joining two pyrrole rings creates a resonance-stabilized macrocycle that absorbs light and emits characteristic red fluorescence.
 * Incorrect (A): Acetate groups serve as structural side chains on the porphyrin ring but do not possess the conjugated pi-electron systems required to emit fluorescence.
 * Incorrect (C): Iron bonded to globin forms functional hemoglobin, a molecule that actually quenches fluorescence rather than producing it.
 * Incorrect (D): Methyl groups are non-polar aliphatic side chains that modify the solubility and positioning of the ring but do not contribute to optical excitation or fluorescence.
 * Incorrect (E): Propionate groups are charged carboxylate side chains involved in structural alignment and interactions with proteins but do not mediate fluorescence.

Question: Delta aminolevulinic acid synthase activity:
A. catalyzes a rate limiting reaction in heme biosynthesis
B. is frequently decreased in patients being treated on CNS drugs
C. is strongly stimulated by heavy metals such as lead
D. occurs in the cytosol
E. requires biotin as coenzyme
Correct Option: A
Explanation:
 * Correct (A): Delta aminolevulinic acid synthase activity catalyzes the condensation of glycine and succinyl-CoA, serving as the primary, highly regulated rate limiting reaction in heme biosynthesis.
 * Incorrect (B): CNS drugs like barbiturates induce cytochrome P450 enzymes and deplete hepatic heme pools, which feeds back to cause an increase rather than a decrease in enzyme activity.
 * Incorrect (C): Heavy metals such as lead strongly inhibit downstream enzymes like ALA dehydratase and ferrochelatase, while indirect transcription effects upregulate rather than stimulate ALA synthase directly.
 * Incorrect (D): The reaction catalyzed by this enzyme occurs exclusively within the mitochondrial matrix, where its substrate succinyl-CoA is readily generated by the citric acid cycle.
 * Incorrect (E): This highly regulated condensation reaction requires pyridoxal phosphate (Vitamin B6) as an absolute cofactor to activate glycine, rather than biotin.

Question: A 3-year-old boy is brought with abdominal pain, mental changes, and fatigue. Lead poisoning is suspected. Which enzyme is interfered:
A. cytochrome oxidase
B. delta-ALA dehydratase
C. porphobilinogen deaminase
D. protoporphyrinogen oxidase
E. UMP synthase
Correct Option: B
Explanation:
 * Correct (B): Lead directly inhibits delta-ALA dehydratase by displacing zinc from its catalytic site, causing an accumulation of delta-aminolevulinic acid and toxic neurobehavioral symptoms.
 * Incorrect (A): Cytochrome oxidase is a terminal respiratory chain enzyme inhibited by cyanide and carbon monoxide, not the primary enzyme interfered with in clinical lead-induced anemia.
 * Incorrect (C): Porphobilinogen deaminase activity is genetically defective in acute intermittent porphyria and is not the physiological target of lead poisoning.
 * Incorrect (D): Protoporphyrinogen oxidase is the enzyme deficient in variegate porphyria and is not directly inactivated by heavy metal lead ions.
 * Incorrect (E): UMP synthase is an enzyme of pyrimidine biosynthesis defective in orotic aciduria and is unaffected by heavy metal lead toxicity.

A. Catalyzes a rate limiting reaction in porphyrin biosynthesis
B. Is frequently decreased in individuals treated with phenobarbital
C. Is strongly inhibited by heavy metal ions such as lead
D. Occurs entirely within the cellular cytosol
E. Requires the essential coenzyme biotin
Correct Option: A
Explanation:
 * Correct (A): Delta-aminolevulinic acid synthase catalyzes the condensation of glycine and succinyl CoA, which serves as the committed, rate-limiting reaction in porphyrin biosynthesis.
 * Incorrect (B): Phenobarbital induces cytochrome P450 enzymes, which depletes intracellular heme and consequently increases, rather than decreases, delta-aminolevulinic acid synthase expression.
 * Incorrect (C): Delta-aminolevulinic acid synthase is not the primary direct target of heavy metal ions such as lead; lead specifically inhibits delta-ALA dehydratase and ferrochelatase.
 * Incorrect (D): The reaction catalyzed by delta-aminolevulinic acid synthase takes place inside the mitochondria, meaning it does not occur entirely within the cellular cytosol.
 * Incorrect (E): This mitochondrial enzyme requires pyridoxal phosphate (Vitamin B6) as a cofactor, not the essential coenzyme biotin.
