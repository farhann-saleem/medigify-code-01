
Question: Primary conjugated bile acid is:
A. chenodeoxycholic acid.
B. cholic acid.
C. deoxycholic acid.
D. glycochenodeoxycholic acid.
E. lithocholic acid.
Correct Option: D
Explanation:
Correct (D): glycochenodeoxycholic acid is a conjugated primary bile acid formed from chenodeoxycholic acid with glycine.
Incorrect (A): chenodeoxycholic acid is a primary bile acid but not conjugated.
Incorrect (B): cholic acid is a primary bile acid but not conjugated.
Incorrect (C): deoxycholic acid is a secondary bile acid.
Incorrect (E): lithocholic acid is a secondary bile acid.
