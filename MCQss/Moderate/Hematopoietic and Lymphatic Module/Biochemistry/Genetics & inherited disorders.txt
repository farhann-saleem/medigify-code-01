
Question: Hb Bart’s hydrops fetalis is due to deletion of:
A. four alpha genes.
B. four beta genes.
C. one alpha gene.
D. three alpha genes.
E. two alpha genes.
Correct Option: A
Explanation:
Correct (A): deletion of four alpha genes prevents alpha-chain production, causing Hb Bart’s hydrops fetalis.
Incorrect (B): four beta genes are not the genetic basis of Hb Bart’s hydrops fetalis.
Incorrect (C): one alpha gene deletion causes silent carrier alpha-thalassemia, not Hb Bart’s hydrops fetalis.
Incorrect (D): three alpha genes deletion causes HbH disease, not Hb Bart’s hydrops fetalis.
Incorrect (E): two alpha genes deletion causes alpha-thalassemia trait, not Hb Bart’s hydrops fetalis.

Question: Porphyria cutanea tarda is due to defect in enzyme:
A. coproporphyrinogen oxidase
B. ferrochelatase
C. protoporphyrinogen oxidase
D. uroporphyrinogen decarboxylase
E. uroporphyrinogen synthase
Correct Option: D
Explanation:
 * Correct (D): Uroporphyrinogen decarboxylase deficiency prevents the conversion of uroporphyrinogen to coproporphyrinogen, causing an accumulation of highly carboxylated porphyrins that trigger cutaneous photosensitivity.
 * Incorrect (A): Coproporphyrinogen oxidase deficiency causes hereditary coproporphyria, a mixed porphyria presenting with distinct neurovisceral attacks and differing excretion profiles.
 * Incorrect (B): Ferrochelatase deficiency leads to erythropoietic protoporphyria, characterized by immediate painful burning of the skin upon sun exposure rather than the blistering lesions of porphyria cutanea tarda.
 * Incorrect (C): Protoporphyrinogen oxidase deficiency causes variegate porphyria, which presents with concurrent acute neurovisceral crises alongside prominent blistering cutaneous lesions.
 * Incorrect (E): Uroporphyrinogen synthase deficiency (also known as hydroxymethylbilane synthase) leads to acute intermittent porphyria, which presents purely with neurological symptoms and entirely lacks cutaneous lesions.
   Tags: Moderate, Biochemistry, Hematopoietic , Genetics & Inherited Disorders

Question: Acute intermittent porphyria is caused due to deficiency of:
A. ALA synthase
B. coproporphyrinogen oxidase
C. coproporphyrinogen synthase
D. uroporphyrinogen decarboxylase
E. uroporphyrinogen I synthase
Correct Option: E
Explanation:
 * Correct (E): Uroporphyrinogen I synthase (hydroxymethylbilane synthase) deficiency blocks the conversion of porphobilinogen, leading to accumulation of toxic neuroactive precursors that manifest as acute neurovisceral crises.
 * Incorrect (A): ALA synthase deficiency or down-regulation decreases porphyrin production, resulting in X-linked sideroblastic anemia rather than the neurovisceral crises seen in acute intermittent porphyria.
 * Incorrect (B): Coproporphyrinogen oxidase deficiency causes hereditary coproporphyria, which presents with cutaneous photosensitivity alongside neurological symptoms, distinct from the purely non-cutaneous phenotype of acute intermittent porphyria.
 * Incorrect (C): Coproporphyrinogen synthase is a non-existent enzyme term that incorrectly blends intermediate names within the standard porphyrin biosynthetic pathway.
 * Incorrect (D): Uroporphyrinogen decarboxylase deficiency is the underlying enzymatic defect responsible for porphyria cutanea tarda, a condition characterized entirely by cutaneous lesions without neurovisceral crises.
   Tags: Moderate, Biochemistry, Hematopoietic , Genetics & Inherited Disorders

Question: Variegate porphyria is:
A. acute intermittent porphyria
B. congenital erythropoietic porphyria
C. erythropoietic protoporphyria
D. hereditary hepatic porphyria
E. porphyria cutanea tarda
Correct Option: D
Explanation:
 * Correct (D): Variegate porphyria belongs to the class of hereditary hepatic porphyria disorders, presenting clinically with mixed neurovisceral crises and cutaneous photosensitivity.
 * Incorrect (A): Acute intermittent porphyria is a distinct hepatic porphyria caused by a deficiency of porphobilinogen deaminase, exhibiting no cutaneous lesions.
 * Incorrect (B): Congenital erythropoietic porphyria is an erythropoietic porphyria, not an inherited hepatic porphyria variant like variegate porphyria.
 * Incorrect (C): Erythropoietic protoporphyria is classified as an erythropoietic porphyria driven by ferrochelatase deficiency in bone marrow precursors.
 * Incorrect (E): Porphyria cutanea tarda is an acquired or familial cutaneous porphyria caused by uroporphyrinogen decarboxylase defects, distinct from variegate porphyria.
   Tags: Moderate, Biochemistry, Hematopoietic, Genetics & Inherited Disorders
