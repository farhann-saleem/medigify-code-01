Question: Erythroblastosis fetalis is caused by:
A. ABO incompatibility
B. glucose-6-phosphate dehydrogenase deficiency
C. hereditary spherocytosis
D. Rh incompatibility
E. sickle cell anemia
Correct Option: D
Explanation:
 * Correct (D): Erythroblastosis fetalis is classically caused by severe Rh incompatibility, where maternal anti-D IgG antibodies cross the placenta and destroy fetal Rh-positive red blood cells.
 * Incorrect (A): ABO incompatibility typically causes mild neonatal hemolytic disease and is rarely the physiological mechanism responsible for severe, classic erythroblastosis fetalis.
 * Incorrect (B): Glucose-6-phosphate dehydrogenase deficiency is an X-linked intrinsic red cell enzymopathy that induces episodic oxidative hemolysis, not immune-mediated erythroblastosis fetalis.
 * Incorrect (C): Hereditary spherocytosis is an inherited RBC membrane defect causing extravascular splenic clearance, distinct from maternal-fetal immune incompatibility.
 * Incorrect (E): Sickle cell anemia is a qualitative hemoglobinopathy caused by Hb S polymerisation, not an alloimmune hemolytic disease targeting fetal erythrocytes.

Question: Which of the following enzymes require iron as cofactor:
 A. carbonic anhydrase
 B. cytochrome oxidase
 C. DNA polymerase
 D. hexokinase
 E. pyruvate dehydrogenase complex
 Correct Option: B
 Explanation:
 Correct (B): Cytochrome oxidase contains iron within heme groups essential for electron transport.
 Incorrect (A): Carbonic anhydrase requires zinc rather than iron.
 Incorrect (C): DNA polymerase primarily requires magnesium ions for activity.
 Incorrect (D): Hexokinase depends mainly on magnesium for ATP binding.
 Incorrect (E): Pyruvate dehydrogenase complex uses thiamine pyrophosphate, lipoic acid, FAD, NAD⁺, and coenzyme A.
