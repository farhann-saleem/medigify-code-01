Question: Alkaline phosphatase is increased in:
 A. carcinoma head of pancreas
 B. carcinoma of prostate
 C. cholecystitis
 D. pancreatitis
 E. rickets
 Correct Option: E
 Explanation:
 Correct (E): Alkaline phosphatase rises markedly in rickets due to increased osteoblastic activity during defective bone mineralization.
 Incorrect (A): Carcinoma head of pancreas may elevate alkaline phosphatase only if biliary obstruction occurs, not as the classic association.
 Incorrect (B): Carcinoma of prostate is more strongly associated with acid phosphatase as a tumor marker.
 Incorrect (C): Cholecystitis may cause mild enzyme elevation but not the characteristic marked increase seen in bone disease.
 Incorrect (D): Pancreatitis primarily elevates amylase and lipase rather than alkaline phosphatase.
