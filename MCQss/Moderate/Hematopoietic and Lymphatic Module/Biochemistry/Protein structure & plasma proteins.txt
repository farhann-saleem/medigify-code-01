
Question: Which of the following forms of haemoglobin provides useful information for the management of diabetes mellitus:
A. HbA1.
B. HbA1c.
C. HbA2.
D. HbC.
E. HbS.
Correct Option: B
Explanation:
Correct (B): HbA1c reflects nonenzymatic glycation of haemoglobin and indicates average blood glucose control over the previous 2–3 months.
Incorrect (A): HbA1 is the major adult haemoglobin fraction and is not the specific diabetes monitoring marker.
Incorrect (C): HbA2 is a minor adult haemoglobin fraction, mainly relevant in β-thalassemia screening.
Incorrect (D): HbC is a structural haemoglobin variant and does not monitor diabetes mellitus.
Incorrect (E): HbS is the sickle haemoglobin variant and does not assess glycemic control.

Question: Which of the following is most useful for diagnosis of multiple myeloma?
 A. Albumin in urine
 B. Bence Jones proteins
 C. Plasma A/G ratio
 D. Serum creatinine
 E. Serum calcium
 Correct Option: B
 Explanation:
 Correct (B): Bence Jones proteins (light chains) are diagnostic hallmark
 Incorrect (A): Albumin in urine is not characteristic
 Incorrect (C): A/G ratio may change but is non-specific
 Incorrect (D): Creatinine reflects renal damage, not diagnosis
 Incorrect (E): Calcium rises but is secondary finding
 Tags: Moderate, Biochemistry, Hematopoietic, Protein Structure & Plasma Proteins
