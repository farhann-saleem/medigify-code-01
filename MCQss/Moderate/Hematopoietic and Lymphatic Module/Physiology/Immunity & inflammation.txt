
Question 8
Question: Cells mainly involved in tissue graft rejection are:
A. B cells.
B. natural killer cells.
C. neutrophils.
D. plasma cells.
E. T cells.
Correct Option: E
Explanation:
Correct (E): T cells are the principal mediators of cellular immune responses responsible for tissue graft rejection.
Incorrect (A): B cells mainly produce antibodies.
Incorrect (B): Natural killer cells contribute but are not the primary mediators.
Incorrect (C): Neutrophils mediate acute inflammation rather than graft rejection.
Incorrect (D): Plasma cells produce antibodies but are not the main cells in tissue graft rejection.

Question 18
Question: To prevent acute transplant rejection, immunosuppressive therapy primarily targets:
A. B-lymphocyte antibody production.
B. complement activation.
C. natural killer cell activity.
D. T-lymphocyte activation and proliferation.
E. tissue macrophage activation.
Correct Option: D
Explanation:
Correct (D): Acute transplant rejection is primarily T-cell mediated, so immunosuppressive therapy targets T-lymphocyte activation and proliferation.
Incorrect (A): B-lymphocyte antibody production is not the primary target in acute cellular rejection.
Incorrect (B): Complement activation is mainly involved in hyperacute rejection.
Incorrect (C): Natural killer cell activity is not the principal therapeutic target.
Incorrect (E): Tissue macrophage activation is not the primary driver of acute transplant rejection.
