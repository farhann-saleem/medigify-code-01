Question 1
Question: Red blood cells:
A. are biconcave disc-shaped.
B. are nucleated.
C. contain blood group antigens on their surface.
D. hemolyze in hypertonic saline.
E. lack membrane glycoproteins.
Correct Option: C
Explanation:
Correct (C): Red blood cells contain blood group antigens on their surface, which determine ABO and Rh blood groups.
Incorrect (A): Mature red blood cells are biconcave disc-shaped, but this is not the best answer because blood group antigens are the defining immunological feature.
Incorrect (B): Mature human red blood cells are anucleate.
Incorrect (D): Hypertonic saline causes red blood cells to shrink (crenate), not hemolyze.
Incorrect (E): Red blood cells possess membrane glycoproteins that carry many blood group antigens.

Question 2
Question: Stored blood plasma has a greater concentration of:
A. calcium.
B. potassium.
C. sodium.
D. urea.
E. uric acid.
Correct Option: B
Explanation:
Correct (B): Potassium concentration increases during blood storage because potassium leaks from red blood cells into the plasma.
Incorrect (A): Calcium does not increase during storage and may decrease due to citrate anticoagulant binding.
Incorrect (C): Sodium concentration does not significantly increase during storage.
Incorrect (D): Urea concentration is not significantly altered by blood storage.
Incorrect (E): Uric acid concentration is not characteristically increased in stored blood plasma.
Tags: Moderate, Physiology, Blood, Blood Groups & Transfusions

Question 3
Question: In Rh-negative blood, Rh antibodies are present:
A. normally in the plasma.
B. normally on the RBC membrane.
C. only in persons who have been sensitized by Rh-positive blood.
D. only in persons with blood group O.
E. they cannot cross the placenta.
Correct Option: C
Explanation:
Correct (C): Rh antibodies develop only after sensitization by exposure to Rh-positive blood through transfusion or pregnancy.
Incorrect (A): Rh antibodies are not naturally present in the plasma.
Incorrect (B): Rh antibodies circulate in plasma and are not normally attached to the RBC membrane.
Incorrect (D): Rh antibody formation is unrelated to ABO blood group.
Incorrect (E): Rh IgG antibodies readily cross the placenta.
Tags: Moderate, Physiology, Blood, Blood Groups & Transfusions
