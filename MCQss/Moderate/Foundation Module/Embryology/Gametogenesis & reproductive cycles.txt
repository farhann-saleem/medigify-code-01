Question: During meiosis, pairing of homologous chromosomes occurs, which permits large segments of DNA to be exchanged. What is this process called?
A. Alignment.
B. Crossing over.
C. Nondisjunction.
D. Recombination arrest.
E. Synapsis.
Correct Option: B
Explanation:
Correct (B): Crossing over exchanges large DNA segments between homologous chromosomes during meiosis I.
Incorrect (A): Alignment refers to chromosome arrangement, not DNA exchange.
Incorrect (C): Nondisjunction is failure of chromosome separation.
Incorrect (D): Recombination arrest prevents rather than permits exchange.
Incorrect (E): Synapsis is chromosome pairing without actual DNA segment exchange.

Question: During ovulation, the secondary oocyte resides at what specific stage of meiosis?
A. Metaphase of meiosis I.
B. Metaphase of meiosis II.
C. Prophase of meiosis I.
C. Prophase of meiosis II.
E. Telophase of meiosis II.
Correct Option: B
Explanation:
Correct (B): The secondary oocyte is arrested in metaphase of meiosis II at ovulation until fertilization occurs.
Incorrect (A): Meiosis I is completed just before ovulation.
Incorrect (C): Prophase of meiosis I arrest occurs during fetal life in primary oocytes.
Incorrect (D): The secondary oocyte is arrested specifically at metaphase, not prophase, of meiosis II.
Incorrect (E): Telophase of meiosis II occurs only after fertilization.

Question: When does a secondary oocyte complete its second meiotic division to become a mature ovum?
A. At fertilization
B. At implantation
C. At ovulation
D. At parturition
E. At puberty
Correct Option: A
Explanation:
Correct (A): The secondary oocyte completes meiosis II only after sperm entry during fertilization, producing the mature ovum and second polar body.
Incorrect (B): Implantation occurs days after meiosis II has already been completed.
Incorrect (C): At ovulation the oocyte is arrested in metaphase II and has not yet completed meiosis.
Incorrect (D): Parturition occurs months after fertilization and is unrelated to meiotic completion.
Incorrect (E): Puberty initiates cyclic ovulation but does not trigger meiosis II completion.

Question: In oogenesis, which event occurs immediately following completion of meiosis II?
A. Formation of the female pronucleus
B. Formation of the second polar body
C. Fusion of male and female pronuclei
D. Implantation into the endometrium
E. Penetration of the zona pellucida by sperm
Correct Option: C
Explanation:
Correct (C): After meiosis II is completed and pronuclei form, the next key fertilization event is fusion of male and female pronuclei to create the zygote.
Incorrect (A): Formation of the female pronucleus occurs before pronuclear fusion.
Incorrect (B): Formation of the second polar body is part of meiosis II completion itself, not the subsequent event.
Incorrect (D): Implantation occurs several days later after blastocyst formation.
Incorrect (E): Penetration of the zona pellucida occurs before meiosis II completion.
