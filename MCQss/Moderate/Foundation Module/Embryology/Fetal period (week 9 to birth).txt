
2. Question: Viable fetus is characterized by all of the following except?
 A. Crown–rump length > 200 mm
 B. Foot length > 40 mm
 C. Gestational age > 24 weeks
 D. Weight > 500 g
 E. Amniotic fluid > 200 ml
 Correct Option: E
 Explanation:
 • Correct (E): Amniotic fluid > 200 ml is not a defining criterion of viability; fluid volume varies independently.
 • Incorrect (A): Crown–rump length > 200 mm indicates advanced fetal development consistent with viability.
 • Incorrect (B): Foot length > 40 mm correlates with gestational maturity beyond viability threshold.
 • Incorrect (C): Gestational age > 24 weeks is the accepted clinical marker of viability.
 • Incorrect (D): Weight > 500 g is a standard viability criterion.
 Tags: Moderate, Embryology, Fetal Period, Fetal Period (Week 9 to Birth)

Question: When does surfactant production start:
A. 21st week
B. 24th week
C. 28th week
D. 32nd week
E. 36th week
Correct Option: B
Explanation:
• Correct (B): Type II pneumocytes begin surfactant production around 24 weeks.
• Incorrect (A): 21st week is too early for functional surfactant synthesis.
• Incorrect (C): 28th week is peak increase, not initiation.
• Incorrect (D): 32nd week is later maturation phase.
• Incorrect (E): 36th week is near-term with established surfactant already present.
Tags: Moderate, Embryology, Foundation Module, Fetal Period (Week 9 to Birth)

Question: When someone is "6 weeks" pregnant, this means that she has conceived ____ weeks previously:
A. 3
B. 4
C. 5
D. 6
E. 7
Correct Option: B
Explanation:
• Correct (B): Gestational age is ~2 weeks more than conception age, so 6 weeks GA ≈ 4 weeks post-conception.
• Incorrect (A): 3 weeks underestimates conception timing.
• Incorrect (C): 5 weeks overestimates conception age.
• Incorrect (D): 6 weeks confuses gestational with embryologic age.
• Incorrect (E): 7 weeks exceeds physiological dating difference.
Tags: Moderate, Embryology, Foundation Module, Fetal Period (Week 9 to Birth)

Question: When someone is "6 weeks" pregnant, this means that she has conceived ____ weeks previously:
A. 2
B. 3
C. 4
D. 5
E. 6
Correct Option: C
Explanation:
 * Correct (C): Gestational age is calculated from the last menstrual period, which occurs roughly 2 weeks before ovulation and fertilization; thus, a 6-week pregnancy corresponds to 4 weeks of true post-conception development.
 * Incorrect (A): Conceiving 2 weeks previously would equate to a gestational age of 4 weeks, not 6 weeks.
 * Incorrect (B): Conceiving 3 weeks previously would mean the patient is 5 weeks pregnant by clinical obstetrical dating calculations.
 * Incorrect (D): Conceiving 5 weeks previously would yield a clinical gestational age of 7 weeks from the last menstrual period.
 * Incorrect (E): Conceiving 6 weeks previously would mean the true embryonic age is 6 weeks, which translates to a clinical gestational age of 8 weeks.
   Tags: Moderate, Embryology, Foundation, Fetal Period (Week 9 to Birth)
