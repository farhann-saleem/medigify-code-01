
Question: A sperm is able to fertilize an oocyte only if it has undergone:
a. acrosome reaction.
b. capacitation.
c. cortical reaction.
d. hyperactivation.
e. zona reaction.
Correct Option: A
Explanation:
Correct (A): The acrosome reaction releases enzymes that enable penetration of the zona pellucida and direct fertilization of the oocyte.
Incorrect (B): Capacitation prepares the sperm membrane but does not itself accomplish fertilization.
Incorrect (C): Cortical reaction is an oocyte response that blocks polyspermy after fertilization begins.
Incorrect (D): Hyperactivation increases sperm motility but cannot independently permit fertilization.
Incorrect (E): Zona reaction hardens the zona pellucida to prevent entry of additional sperm.

Question: Acrosomal reaction is associated with:
a. acrosin activation.
b. corona radiata penetration.
c. hyaluronidase release.
d. sperm membrane fusion.
e. zona pellucida binding.
Correct Option: B
Explanation:
Correct (B): The acrosomal reaction facilitates penetration of the corona radiata and zona pellucida by releasing hydrolytic enzymes.
Incorrect (A): Acrosin activation is a biochemical component of the reaction rather than the associated functional event asked here.
Incorrect (C): Hyaluronidase release occurs during the reaction but specifically aids enzymatic dispersal rather than defining the associated process.
Incorrect (D): Sperm membrane fusion with the oocyte membrane occurs after zona penetration.
Incorrect (E): Zona pellucida binding precedes initiation of the acrosomal reaction.
Tags: Moderate, Embryology, Foundation Module, Week 1: Fertilization & Cleavage

Question: Individual blastomeres were isolated from a blastula at the 4-cell stage. Each blastomere was cultured in vitro to the blastocyst stage and individually implanted into four pseudopregnant foster mothers. Which of the following would you expect to observe 9 months later: Options: a) Birth of one baby b) Birth of four genetically different babies c) Birth of four genetically identical babies d) Birth of four grotesquely deformed babies Correct Option: Ans. Birth of four genetically identical babies Explanation: At the 4-cell stage, each blastomere is totipotent, meaning it can develop into a complete embryo. When separated, each forms a blastocyst independently. Since all arise from the same zygote, they have identical genetic material. Therefore, four genetically identical babies are produced.
