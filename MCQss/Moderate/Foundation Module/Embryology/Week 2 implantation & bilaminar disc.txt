Question: How soon after fertilization does the blastocyst begin implantation?
A. By day 1.
B. By day 2.
C. By day 5.
D. By day 7.
E. Within minutes.
Correct Option: D
Explanation:
Correct (D): Implantation of the blastocyst normally begins around day 6–7 after fertilization.
Incorrect (A): Day 1 corresponds to early cleavage stages only.
Incorrect (B): Day 2 is still within tubal cleavage stages.
Incorrect (C): Day 5 usually marks blastocyst formation before implantation begins.
Incorrect (E): Implantation does not occur immediately after fertilization.

Question: Which component plays the most active role in invading the endometrium during blastocyst implantation:
a. cytotrophoblast.
b. epiblast.
c. extraembryonic somatic mesoderm.
d. extraembryonic splanchnic mesoderm.
e. syncytiotrophoblast.
Correct Option: E
Explanation:
Correct (E): The syncytiotrophoblast is the invasive multinucleated trophoblastic layer that erodes endometrial tissue during implantation.
Incorrect (A): Cytotrophoblast mainly provides proliferative cells rather than direct invasive activity.
Incorrect (B): Epiblast forms embryonic tissues and does not invade the endometrium.
Incorrect (C): Extraembryonic somatic mesoderm contributes to chorionic structures, not implantation invasion.
Incorrect (D): Extraembryonic splanchnic mesoderm lines the yolk sac and is unrelated to endometrial invasion.

Question: Between which two layers is the extraembryonic mesoderm located:
a. cytotrophoblast and syncytiotrophoblast.
b. epiblast and hypoblast.
c. exocoelomic membrane and cytotrophoblast.
d. hypoblast and epiblast.
e. syncytiotrophoblast and endometrium.
Correct Option: C
Explanation:
Correct (C): Extraembryonic mesoderm develops between the exocoelomic membrane and cytotrophoblast.
Incorrect (A): The space between cytotrophoblast and syncytiotrophoblast is occupied by trophoblastic layers only.
Incorrect (B): Epiblast and hypoblast together form the bilaminar embryonic disk.
Incorrect (D): Hypoblast and epiblast are adjacent embryonic layers without extraembryonic mesoderm between them.
Incorrect (E): Syncytiotrophoblast directly invades the endometrium without interposed extraembryonic mesoderm.
