Question: Warfarin is contraindicated in pregnancy. Which of the following abnormalities is commonly associated with warfarin use in the first trimester?
A. Claw hand
B. Dextrocardia
C. Holoprosencephaly
D. Nasal hypoplasia
E. Renal agenesis
Correct Option: D
Explanation:
• Correct (D): First-trimester warfarin exposure causes fetal warfarin syndrome characterized by nasal hypoplasia and stippled epiphyses.
• Incorrect (A): Claw hand is not a recognized manifestation of warfarin embryopathy.
• Incorrect (B): Dextrocardia is a laterality defect unrelated to warfarin teratogenicity.
• Incorrect (C): Holoprosencephaly is more strongly linked to trisomy 13 and maternal diabetes.
• Incorrect (E): Renal agenesis is classically associated with ACE inhibitor exposure rather than warfarin use.
