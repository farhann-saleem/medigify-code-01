Question: When does neurulation complete:
A. by end of 3rd week
B. by end of 4th week
C. by end of 5th week
D. by end of 6th week
E. by end of 7th week
Correct Option: B
Explanation:
• Correct (B): Neural tube closure completes by end of 4th week.
• Incorrect (A): 3rd week is initiation of neural plate formation.
• Incorrect (C): 5th week is post-closure differentiation phase.
• Incorrect (D): 6th week is too late; closure already completed.
• Incorrect (E): 7th week is well into organogenesis.

Question: Melanocytes are derived from:
A. ectoderm
B. endoderm
C. mesoderm
D. neural crest
E. surface ectoderm
Correct Option: D
Explanation:
• Correct (D): Melanocytes originate from neural crest cells migrating during development.
• Incorrect (A): General ectoderm is too broad; melanocytes are specifically neural crest derived.
• Incorrect (B): Endoderm forms gut lining, not pigment cells.
• Incorrect (C): Mesoderm forms muscle and connective tissue, not melanocytes.
• Incorrect (E): Surface ectoderm forms epidermis but not pigment cells.

Question: Which of the following does not pass through the primitive umbilical ring:
A. allantois.
B. amnion.
C. connecting stalk.
D. omphaloenteric duct.
E. yolk sac.
Correct Option: B
Explanation:
Correct (B): The amnion surrounds the umbilical cord externally but does not pass through the primitive umbilical ring.
Incorrect (A): The allantois extends through the primitive umbilical ring into the connecting stalk.
Incorrect (C): The connecting stalk traverses the primitive umbilical ring and forms the umbilical cord.
Incorrect (D): The omphaloenteric duct passes through the primitive umbilical ring connecting midgut to yolk sac.
Incorrect (E): The yolk sac is connected to the embryo through structures traversing the primitive umbilical ring.
Tags: Moderate, Embryology, Foundation Module, Weeks 4-8: Embryonic Folding & Germ Layer Derivatives

Amnion Explanation: The primitive umbilical ring transmits structures connecting the embryo to the placenta, including the connecting stalk, yolk sac (vitelline duct), and allantois.
