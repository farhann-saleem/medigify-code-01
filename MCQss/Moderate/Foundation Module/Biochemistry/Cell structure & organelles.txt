Question: Which of the following statements is NOT correct for biological membranes:
 A. free movement of proteins across the membrane.
 B. prevention of free diffusion of ionic solutes.
 C. release of proteins when damaged.
 D. signal reception and transmission.
 E. site for biochemical reactions.
 Correct Option: A
 Explanation:
 Correct (A): Proteins in biological membranes have restricted lateral mobility and do not move freely across the membrane bilayer.
 Incorrect (B): Biological membranes prevent free diffusion of ionic solutes because of their hydrophobic core.
 Incorrect (C): Membrane damage can cause leakage and release of intracellular proteins.
 Incorrect (D): Membranes contain receptors involved in signal reception and transmission.
 Incorrect (E): Many biochemical reactions occur on membrane surfaces through membrane-bound enzymes.
