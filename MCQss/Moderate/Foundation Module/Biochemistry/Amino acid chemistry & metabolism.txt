
Question: Which of the following pairs of amino acids can be derived from essential amino acids:
A. alanine and serine
B. aspartate and glutamate
C. glycine and asparagine
D. proline and histidine
E. tyrosine and cysteine
Correct Option: E
Explanation:
Correct (E): Tyrosine derives from phenylalanine; cysteine derives from methionine
Incorrect (A): Alanine and serine are non-essential directly synthesized
Incorrect (B): Aspartate and glutamate are non-essential
Incorrect (C): Glycine and asparagine are non-essential
Incorrect (D): Proline is non-essential; histidine is essential itself

Question: Serine-cysteine-glutamate-proline-threonine-alanine-tyrosine peptide shows:
A. contains acidic amino acids
B. contains basic amino acids
C. contains no acidic amino acids
D. contains no neutral amino acids
E. contains no hydroxyl amino acids
Correct Option: A
Explanation:
Correct (A): Glutamate is an acidic amino acid with carboxyl side chain
Incorrect (B): No strongly basic residues like lysine/arginine present
Incorrect (C): Acidic amino acid is present (glutamate)
Incorrect (D): Many neutral amino acids exist in sequence
Incorrect (E): Serine, threonine, tyrosine are hydroxyl-containing

Question: All aminotransferases require the coenzyme:
 A. biotin
 B. FAD
 C. NAD
 D. pyridoxal phosphate
 E. thiamine pyrophosphate
 Correct Option: D
 Explanation:
 Correct (D): Pyridoxal phosphate, derived from vitamin B₆, is the essential coenzyme for aminotransferase reactions.
 Incorrect (A): Biotin functions in carboxylation reactions.
 Incorrect (B): FAD mainly participates in oxidation-reduction reactions.
 Incorrect (C): NAD acts as an electron carrier in dehydrogenase reactions.
 Incorrect (E): Thiamine pyrophosphate is involved in oxidative decarboxylation.
 Tags: Moderate, Biochemistry, Foundation, Amino Acid Chemistry & Metabolism
