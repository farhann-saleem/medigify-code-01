
Enzyme inhibited by lead is:
A. ALA dehydratase
B. ALA synthase
C. coproporphyrinogen oxidase
D. protoporphyrinogen oxidase
E. uroporphyrinogen synthase
Correct Option: A
Explanation:
Correct (A): Lead inhibits ALA dehydratase in heme synthesis, causing accumulation of ALA.
Incorrect (B): ALA synthase is the rate-limiting enzyme and is not directly inhibited by lead.
Incorrect (C): Coproporphyrinogen oxidase deficiency is linked to hereditary porphyrias, not lead poisoning.
Incorrect (D): Protoporphyrinogen oxidase is not the principal lead-sensitive enzyme.
Incorrect (E): Uroporphyrinogen synthase deficiency causes congenital erythropoietic porphyria.

Question: In sickle cell anaemia glutamic acid is replaced by:
A. aspartic acid
B. histidine
C. lysine
D. valine
E. arginine
Correct Option: D
Explanation:
Correct (D): Valine substitution creates hydrophobic patch causing HbS polymerization
Incorrect (A): Aspartic acid is still acidic, does not cause sickling
Incorrect (B): Histidine change is unrelated to HbS mutation
Incorrect (C): Lysine is basic, but not involved in HbS mutation
Incorrect (E): Arginine substitution is not seen in sickle cell disease
Tags: Moderate, Biochemistry, Foundation, Hemoglobin & Heme Synthesis
