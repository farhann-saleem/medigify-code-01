Question: In dialysis, the protein particles are separated from crystalloids based on:
A. charge gradient across the membrane.
B. electrical charge.
C. facilitated diffusion.
D. molecular size.
E. solubility.
Correct Option: D
Explanation:
Correct (D): Dialysis separates protein particles from crystalloids because proteins are too large to pass through the semipermeable membrane.
Incorrect (A): Charge gradient across the membrane is not the principal mechanism in dialysis separation.
Incorrect (B): Electrical charge alone does not determine passage through dialysis membranes.
Incorrect (C): Facilitated diffusion requires carrier proteins and is unrelated to dialysis.
Incorrect (E): Solubility differences are not the basis of dialysis separation.
