
Question: Synthesis of calcitriol is controlled by:
A. Calcitonin
B. Estrogen
C. Growth hormone
D. Parathyroid hormone
E. Insulin
Correct Option: D
Explanation:
Correct (D): PTH activates renal 1α-hydroxylase → calcitriol synthesis
Incorrect (A): Calcitonin lowers calcium, not calcitriol control
Incorrect (B): Estrogen affects bone, not vitamin D synthesis
Incorrect (C): Growth hormone is unrelated
Incorrect (E): Insulin has no role in vitamin D pathway
Tags: Moderate, Biochemistry, Foundation, Vitamins

Question: The nonstandard amino acid that contributes to prothrombin is:
A. beta-alanine
B. gamma-carboxyglutamate
C. hydroxyproline
D. methyl lysine
E. ornithine

Explanation:
Correct (B): Gamma-carboxyglutamate allows Ca²⁺ binding in clotting factors
Incorrect (A): Beta-alanine is unrelated to clotting proteins
Incorrect (C): Hydroxyproline is collagen-specific
Incorrect (D): Methyl lysine is histone-related
Incorrect (E): Ornithine is urea cycle intermediate

Question: A coenzyme required in the transfer of single carbon moieties is:
 A. biotin
 B. coenzyme A
 C. lipoic acid
 D. pyridoxal phosphate
 E. tetrahydrofolate
 Correct Option: E
 Explanation:
 Correct (E): Tetrahydrofolate transfers single carbon moieties in nucleotide and amino acid metabolism.
 Incorrect (A): Biotin functions mainly in carboxylation reactions involving CO₂ transfer.
 Incorrect (B): Coenzyme A transfers acyl groups rather than single carbon moieties.
 Incorrect (C): Lipoic acid participates in oxidative decarboxylation reactions.
 Incorrect (D): Pyridoxal phosphate is primarily involved in amino acid transamination.
 Tags: Moderate, Biochemistry, Foundation, Vitamins

 A. biotin
 B. folinic acid
 C. lipoic acid
 D. pyridoxal phosphate
 E. tetrahydrofolate

Question: Nucleic acid synthesis would be reduced most directly by a deficiency of:
 A. 11-cis retinal
 B. biotin
 C. folic acid
 D. potassium
 E. thiamine
 Correct Option: C
 Explanation:
 •	Correct (C): Folate is required for purine synthesis and dTMP formation.
 •	Incorrect (A): Retinal is for vision, not nucleotide synthesis.
 •	Incorrect (B): Biotin is a carboxylation cofactor.
 •	Incorrect (D): Potassium is an electrolyte, not a nucleotide precursor.
 •	Incorrect (E): Thiamine is for carbohydrate metabolism.
 Tags: Moderate, Biochemistry, Foundation, Vitamins
