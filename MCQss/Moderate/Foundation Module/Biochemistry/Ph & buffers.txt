Question: In dialysis, the protein particles are separated from crystalloids based on:
 A. charge gradient across the membrane.
 B. electrical charge.
 C. facilitated diffusion.
 D. molecular size.
 E. solubility.
 Correct Option: D
 Explanation:
 Correct (D): Dialysis separates protein particles from crystalloids because proteins are too large to pass through the semipermeable membrane.
 Incorrect (A): Charge gradient across the membrane is not the principal mechanism in dialysis separation.
 Incorrect (B): Electrical charge alone does not determine passage through dialysis membranes.
 Incorrect (C): Facilitated diffusion requires carrier proteins and is unrelated to dialysis.
 Incorrect (E): Solubility differences are not the basis of dialysis separation.

Question: The pressure that stops the osmosis of pure solvent into solution through a semipermeable membrane is known as:
 A. dialysis.
 B. diffusion.
 C. oncotic pressure.
 D. osmotic pressure.
 E. vapor pressure.
 Correct Option: D
 Explanation:
 Correct (D): Osmotic pressure is the hydrostatic pressure required to prevent net solvent movement across a semipermeable membrane.
 Incorrect (A): Dialysis refers to the separation of solutes by selective diffusion through a membrane.
 Incorrect (B): Diffusion is the passive movement of molecules down a concentration gradient.
 Incorrect (C): Oncotic pressure specifically refers to osmotic pressure generated by plasma proteins.
 Incorrect (E): Vapor pressure relates to the evaporation tendency of liquids, not osmosis.
 Tags: Moderate, Biochemistry, Foundation, Water, pH & Buffers
