Question: In relation to nucleic acid which of the following statement is NOT correct:
 A. base pairing occurs between a purine and pyrimidine base.
 B. guanine and cytosine are linked by two hydrogen bonds.
 C. RNA is an unbranched linear polymer of ribonucleotides.
 D. the sugar and phosphate groups play a structural role in DNA.
 E. the two polynucleotide chains in DNA show opposite polarity.
 Correct Option: B
 Explanation:

 •	Incorrect (D): Sugar and phosphate groups form the structural backbone of DNA.
 •	Incorrect (E): DNA strands run in opposite 5′→3′ directions.

Question: If the guanine content of a DNA molecule is 20% of the total bases, the thymine content will be?
 A. 10%
 B. 20%
 C. 30%
 D. 40%
 E. 50%
 Correct Option: C
 Explanation:
 •	Correct (C): By Chargaff’s rule, guanine equals cytosine; if guanine is 20%, cytosine is also 20%, leaving 60% for adenine and thymine, so thymine is 30%.
 •	Incorrect (A): 10% would require adenine and thymine together to account for only 20% of bases.
 •	Incorrect (B): 20% would leave an unequal distribution between adenine and thymine.
 •	Incorrect (D): 40% would make total bases exceed 100%.
 •	Incorrect (E): 50% is impossible because adenine must equal thymine.
 Tags: Moderate, Biochemistry, Foundation, Nucleotides, DNA & RNA

Question: A molecule of DNA having high GC content will have:
 A. basal melting temperature
 B. balanced melting temperature
 C. high melting temperature
 D. low melting temperature
 E. optimal melting temperature
 Correct Option: C
 Explanation:
 •	Correct (C): GC pairs form 3 hydrogen bonds, increasing thermal stability and melting temperature.
 •	Incorrect (A): Basal is non-specific and not thermodynamically defined.
 •	Incorrect (B): “Balanced” is not a biochemical thermodynamic category.
 •	Incorrect (D): Low Tm is characteristic of AT-rich DNA, not GC-rich DNA.
 •	Incorrect (E): “Optimal” is subjective, not a measurable DNA property.
