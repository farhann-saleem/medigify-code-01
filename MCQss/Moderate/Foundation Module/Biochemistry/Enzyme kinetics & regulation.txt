Question: Sulfonamides are competitive inhibitors of:
A. dihydropteroate synthase.
B. folic acid synthesis.
C. PABA (para-aminobenzoic acid).
D. tetrahydrofolate formation.
E. bacterial folate metabolism.
Correct Option: C
Explanation:
Correct (C): Sulfonamides are structural analogs of PABA (para-aminobenzoic acid) and competitively inhibit its utilization in bacterial folate synthesis.
Incorrect (A): Dihydropteroate synthase is the inhibited enzyme, not the competitive substrate itself.
Incorrect (B): Folic acid synthesis is the affected pathway rather than the direct competitive target.
Incorrect (D): Tetrahydrofolate formation is downstream of the primary sulfonamide action.
Incorrect (E): Bacterial folate metabolism describes the overall metabolic pathway, not the specific competitive inhibitor target.
Tags: Moderate, Biochemistry, Foundation, Enzyme Kinetics & Regulation

Question: In competitive inhibition, which of the following statements is true?
A. Both Km and Vmax are increased
B. Km is increased
C. Km is decreased
D. Vmax is decreased
E. Both Km and Vmax are unchanged
Correct Option: B
Explanation:
Correct (B): Competitive inhibition increases Km (↓ substrate affinity)
Incorrect (A): Vmax is unchanged, not increased
Incorrect (C): Km does not decrease in competitive inhibition
Incorrect (D): Vmax remains unchanged
Incorrect (E): Km is altered, so not unchanged

Explanation:
Correct (E): Oligomycin inhibits ATP synthase, not cytochrome oxidase

Incorrect (C): H₂S inhibits cytochrome oxidase
Incorrect (D): Azide blocks complex IV

Question: Competitive inhibition can be reversed by adding:
A. cofactor
B. enzyme
C. inhibitor
D. product
E. substrate
Correct Option: E
Explanation:
Correct (E): Increasing substrate concentration outcompetes the inhibitor at the enzyme active site in competitive inhibition.
Incorrect (A): Cofactor addition does not displace a competitive inhibitor.
Incorrect (B): Adding enzyme may increase reaction rate but does not specifically reverse competitive inhibition.
Incorrect (C): Additional inhibitor would worsen inhibition.
Incorrect (D): Product accumulation may inhibit rather than reverse the process.
Tags: Moderate, Biochemistry, Foundation, Enzyme Kinetics & Regulation

Question: In non-competitive enzyme inhibition which of the following is true:
A. decrease Km
B. decrease Vmax
C. increase Km
D. increase Vmax
E. no change in Vmax
Correct Option: B
Explanation:
Correct (B): Non-competitive inhibition decreases Vmax because inhibitor binding reduces functional enzyme concentration.
Incorrect (A): Km usually remains unchanged in pure non-competitive inhibition.
Incorrect (C): Increased Km is more characteristic of competitive inhibition.
Incorrect (D): Vmax cannot increase in enzyme inhibition.
Incorrect (E): Vmax is reduced due to impaired catalytic capacity.
Tags: Moderate, Biochemistry, Foundation, Enzyme Kinetics & Regulation

Question: The rate of enzyme-catalyzed reaction is directly proportional to:
A. enzyme concentration
B. hydrogen ion concentration
C. product concentration
D. substrate concentration
E. temperature
Correct Option: D
Explanation:
Correct (D): Reaction velocity increases directly with substrate concentration until enzyme saturation occurs.
Incorrect (A): Enzyme concentration affects maximum capacity but the classic direct proportionality described is with substrate concentration.
Incorrect (B): Hydrogen ion concentration affects pH sensitivity rather than direct proportionality.
Incorrect (C): Product accumulation may inhibit the reaction.
Incorrect (E): Excessive temperature denatures enzymes, so the relationship is not consistently direct.
Tags: Moderate, Biochemistry, Foundation, Enzyme Kinetics & Regulation

Question: Physostigmine, an anticholinesterase, is used to treat:
A. arthritis
B. glaucoma
C. hypertension
D. myasthenia gravis
E. parkinsonism
Correct Option: D
Explanation:
Correct (D): Physostigmine inhibits acetylcholinesterase, increasing acetylcholine availability at the neuromuscular junction in myasthenia gravis.
Incorrect (A): Arthritis is not treated with anticholinesterase drugs.
Incorrect (B): Although physostigmine may reduce intraocular pressure, myasthenia gravis is the more specific therapeutic indication here.
Incorrect (C): Anticholinesterases are not standard antihypertensive agents.
Incorrect (E): Parkinsonism is treated mainly with dopaminergic therapy rather than anticholinesterases.

B. alcohol + NAD+ → aldehyde + NADH + H+
C. histidine → histamine + CO2
D. L-alanine → D-alanine
E. triglyceride + H2O → diglyceride + fatty acid
Correct Option: A
Explanation:

Incorrect (B): This is an oxidoreductase reaction catalyzed by dehydrogenase.
Incorrect (C): This is a decarboxylation reaction catalyzed by a lyase.
Incorrect (D): This is an isomerization reaction.
Incorrect (E): This is a hydrolysis reaction catalyzed by a hydrolase.

Which one of the following statements is NOT correct for competitive inhibition:
A. allopurinol is a competitive inhibitor
B. inhibitor and substrate bind at different sites
C. Km increases
D. Vmax remains unchanged
E. competitive inhibition can be overcome by increasing substrate concentration
Correct Option: B
Explanation:
Correct (B): In competitive inhibition, inhibitor and substrate compete for the same active site rather than binding at different sites.
Incorrect (A): Allopurinol competitively inhibits xanthine oxidase.
Incorrect (C): Competitive inhibition increases apparent Km due to reduced substrate affinity.
Incorrect (D): Vmax remains unchanged because excess substrate can overcome inhibition.
Incorrect (E): Increasing substrate concentration reverses competitive inhibition.
Tags: Moderate, Biochemistry, Foundation, Enzyme Kinetics & Regulation

Which one of the following statements is NOT true about Vmax:
A. it depends on the affinity of the enzyme
B. it depends on the number of enzyme molecules
C. it is read on the Y-axis
D. it is the maximum velocity that can ever be reached
E. more the number of enzymes, higher will be the Vmax
Correct Option: A
Explanation:
Correct (A): Vmax depends on enzyme concentration and catalytic capacity, not on the affinity of the enzyme for substrate.
Incorrect (B): Increasing the number of enzyme molecules raises Vmax.
Incorrect (C): Vmax is plotted on the Y-axis of Michaelis-Menten graphs.
Incorrect (D): Vmax represents the maximum velocity achievable at substrate saturation.
Incorrect (E): More enzyme molecules produce a proportionately higher Vmax.
Tags: Moderate, Biochemistry, Foundation, Enzyme Kinetics & Regulation
