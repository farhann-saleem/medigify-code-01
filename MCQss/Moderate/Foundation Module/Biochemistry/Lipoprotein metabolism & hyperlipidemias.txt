
Which apoprotein acts as a cofactor activator of the enzyme lipoprotein lipase (LPL):
A. Apo B-100
B. Apo B-48
C. Apo C-II
D. Apo C-III
E. Apo E
Correct Option: C
Explanation:
Correct (C): Apo C-II acts as an essential cofactor activator of the enzyme lipoprotein lipase (LPL) for triglyceride hydrolysis.
Incorrect (A): Apo B-100 mediates LDL receptor binding.
Incorrect (B): Apo B-48 is required for chylomicron assembly.
Incorrect (D): Apo C-III inhibits lipoprotein lipase activity.
Incorrect (E): Apo E mediates remnant uptake by the liver.

Question: Which apoprotein acts as an activator of lipoprotein lipase (LPL):
A. apoc-iii
B. apoc-ii
C. apob-100
D. apob-48
E. apoe
Correct Option: B
Explanation:
Correct (B): apoc-ii is the essential cofactor that activates lipoprotein lipase for triglyceride breakdown.
Incorrect (A): apoc-iii inhibits lipoprotein lipase activity rather than activating it.
Incorrect (C): apob-100 is structural for LDL and VLDL, not an LPL activator.
Incorrect (D): apob-48 is involved in chylomicron assembly in intestine.
Incorrect (E): apoe mediates remnant uptake in liver, not LPL activation.
