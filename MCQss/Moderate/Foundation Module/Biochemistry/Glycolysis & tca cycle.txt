Question: Cytochrome oxidase is poisoned by all of the following except:
 A. Cyanide
 B. Carbon monoxide
 C. Hydrogen sulphide
 D. Azide
 E. Oligomycin
 Correct Option: E
 Explanation:
 Correct (E): Oligomycin inhibits ATP synthase, not cytochrome oxidase
 Incorrect (A): Cyanide blocks complex IV
 Incorrect (B): CO interferes with electron transport
 Incorrect (C): H₂S inhibits cytochrome oxidase
 Incorrect (D): Azide blocks complex IV
