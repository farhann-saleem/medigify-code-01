B. myocardium of heart 
C. sclera of eye 
D. spinal cord 
E. vermiform appendix 
Correct Option: D 
Explanation:
* Correct (D): Lymph vessels are not present in the spinal cord, as the central nervous system relies instead on the cerebrospinal fluid and glymphatic system for waste clearance.
* Incorrect (A): The gastrointestinal tract contains specialized lymph vessels called lacteals crucial for dietary fat absorption.
* Incorrect (B): The myocardium of heart possesses an extensive network of lymphatic vessels to drain interstitial fluid from the contracting cardiac tissue.
* Incorrect (C): While the cornea is avascular, lymphatic vessels are structurally absent in the deep sclera of eye, but the spinal cord represents a definitive avascular/alymphatic organ system.
* Incorrect (E): The vermiform appendix is a highly specialized lymphoid organ densely populated with intrinsic lymphatic vessels.
