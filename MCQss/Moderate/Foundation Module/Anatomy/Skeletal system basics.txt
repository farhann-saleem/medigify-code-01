
Question: Regarding bone, choose correct statement: A. Long bones have one epiphysis and two diaphyses B. Membranous bones ossify in cartilage C. Pneumatic bones contain large fluid space D. Sesamoid bone has no periosteum E. Short bones lack epiphysis Correct Option: D Explanation: Correct (D): Sesamoid bones lack periosteum because they are embedded in tendons and covered by articular cartilage. Incorrect (A): Long bones have one diaphysis and two epiphyses, not vice versa. Incorrect (B): Membranous bones form via intramembranous ossification, not cartilage. Incorrect (C): Pneumatic bones contain air-filled spaces, not fluid. Incorrect (E): Short bones are not defined by epiphyses/diaphysis structure.
