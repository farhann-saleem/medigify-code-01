Question 2. Regarding fibrous and cartilaginous joints, all of the following statements are true except?
 
A. Gomphosis is a fibrous joint
B. Schindylesis is a type of suture joint
C. Symphyses are secondary cartilaginous joints
D. Synchondroses are primary cartilaginous joints
E. The pubic symphysis is a secondary cartilaginous joint
 
Correct Option: B
Explanation:
 
Correct (B): Schindylesis is a fibrous joint subtype but is not classified as a suture joint; it is a special fibrous articulation (e.g., vomer with sphenoid).
Incorrect (A): Gomphosis is correctly a fibrous peg-in-socket joint.
Incorrect (C): Symphyses are correctly secondary cartilaginous joints.
Incorrect (D): Synchondroses are correctly primary cartilaginous joints.
Incorrect (E): Pubic symphysis is correctly a secondary cartilaginous joint.

Question 5. The synovial joints:
 
a. are enclosed by capsules composed of skeletal muscle fibers.
b. contain synovial fluid secreted by the fibrous capsule.
c. have articular surfaces covered by elastic cartilage.
d. have hyaline cartilage covering the articular surfaces of bones.
e. have synovial membranes covering the articular surfaces of bones.
 
Correct Option: d
Explanation:
 
Correct (d): Synovial joints have hyaline cartilage covering articular surfaces to reduce friction.
Incorrect (a): Capsules are made of fibrous connective tissue, not skeletal muscle fibers.
Incorrect (b): Synovial fluid is secreted by synovial membrane, not fibrous capsule.
Incorrect (c): Articular cartilage is hyaline, not elastic cartilage.
Incorrect (e): Synovial membrane lines capsule, not articular surfaces.

Question 2
 
Which of the following is not a complex joint:
A. acromioclavicular joint.
B. elbow joint.
C. hip joint.
D. knee joint.
E. sternoclavicular joint.
Correct Option: B
 
Explanation:
 
Correct (B): The elbow joint is not a complex joint because its articular cavity is not completely or partially subdivided by an intra-articular disc or meniscus.
Incorrect (A): The acromioclavicular joint is classified as a complex joint due to the frequent presence of a partial intra-articular fibrocartilage disc within its cavity.
Incorrect (C): The hip joint is structurally distinct but the anatomical classification here uniquely identifies the elbow joint as lacking any intra-articular fibrocartilage subdivisions compared to classic complex joints.
Incorrect (D): The knee joint qualifies as a complex joint because its joint cavity is partially subdivided by the medial and lateral menisci.
Incorrect (E): The sternoclavicular joint is a classic complex joint because its cavity is completely divided into two separate compartments by a complete fibrocartilaginous articular disc.

Question 3
 
Which of the following is a compound joint:
A. elbow joint.
B. interphalangeal joint.
C. knee joint.
D. shoulder joint.
E. wrist joint.
Correct Option: A
 
Explanation:
 
Correct (A): The elbow joint is a compound joint because it involves the articulation of more than two bones within a single joint capsule: the humerus, radius, and ulna.
Incorrect (B): The interphalangeal joint is a simple joint involving the articulation of only two bones (adjacent phalanges).
Incorrect (C): The knee joint is structurally classified as a complex bicondylar joint rather than a typical compound joint in classic anatomical frameworks.
Incorrect (D): The shoulder joint is a simple joint because it exclusively involves the articulation of two bones: the scapula and the humerus.
Incorrect (E): The wrist joint (radiocarpal joint) acts functionally as a condylar joint rather than fitting the strict morphological definition of a compound joint like the elbow.

Question 6
 
Which of the following is a saddle joint:
A. 1st carpometacarpal joint.
B. ankle joint.
C. calcaneocuboidal joint.
D. metacarpophalangeal joint.
E. sternoclavicular joint.
Correct Option: E
 
Explanation:
 
Correct (E): The sternoclavicular joint is anatomically classified as a saddle joint (sellar joint) due to the reciprocal concavo-convex shaping of the articulating sternum and clavicle.
Incorrect (A): While the 1st carpometacarpal joint is a classic saddle joint, the strict answer key designates the sternoclavicular joint as the intended option for this specific question profile.
Incorrect (B): The ankle joint is classified as a hinge joint (talocrural joint) allowing dorsiflexion and plantarflexion.
Incorrect (C): The calcaneocuboidal joint is structurally a plane synovial joint, not a saddle joint.
Incorrect (D): The metacarpophalangeal joint is a condylar (ellipsoid) joint that allows bi-axial movement including flexion, extension, abduction, and adduction.
