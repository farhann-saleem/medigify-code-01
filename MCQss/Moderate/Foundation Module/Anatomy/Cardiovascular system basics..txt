A. Left phrenic nerve 
B. Left recurrent laryngeal nerve 
C. Right phrenic nerve 
D. Right recurrent laryngeal nerve 
E. Right vagus nerve 
Correct Option: B 
Explanation:
* Correct (B): The left recurrent laryngeal nerve hooks beneath the aortic arch at the ligamentum arteriosum; an aortic arch aneurysm can compress or stretch it, causing hoarseness (recurrent laryngeal nerve palsy / Ortner's syndrome).
* Incorrect (A): The left phrenic nerve passes anterior to the aortic arch and is not typically compressed by an aortic arch aneurysm.
* Incorrect (C): The right phrenic nerve lies on the right side of the thorax, remote from the aortic arch, and would not be compressed by an aortic arch aneurysm.
* Incorrect (D): The right recurrent laryngeal nerve loops around the right subclavian artery and is not in proximity to the aortic arch.
* Incorrect (E): The right vagus nerve travels on the right side of the mediastinum and does not come into contact with the aortic arch.
