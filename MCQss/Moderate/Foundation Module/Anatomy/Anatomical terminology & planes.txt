A. arterial anastomosis 
B. arteriovenous shunt 
C. capillary plexus 
D. potential anastomosis 
E. venous anastomosis 
Correct Option: B 
Explanation:
* Correct (B): An arteriovenous shunt forms the core structure of glomus bodies in skin, which are specialized neuromyoarterial organs that dynamically regulate heat dissipation.
* Incorrect (A): An arterial anastomosis describes a connection between two distinct arteries and does not form the specialized structural complex of a glomus body.
* Incorrect (C): A capillary plexus is a standard network of microvessels designed for metabolic exchange rather than the direct shunting of blood.
* Incorrect (D): A potential anastomosis refers to dormant collateral channels between arteries that open only during pathology, not a permanent structural cutaneous shunt.
* Incorrect (E): A venous anastomosis is a simple structural connection between two veins to optimize blood return to the heart.

Question: Surgical incisions made parallel to Kraissl’s lines result in: 
A. faster healing with fine scar formation 
B. faster healing with no scar formation 
C. slowed healing with fine scar formation 
D. slowed healing with minimal scar formation E. slowed healing with no scar formation Correct Option: A
Explanation:
* Correct (A): Incisions made parallel to Kraissl's lines experience minimal lateral skin tension, which facilitates optimal wound edge approximation, resulting in faster healing with fine scar formation.
* Incorrect (B): Complete absence of a scar is biologically impossible in adult cutaneous surgical wounds, as some degree of collagen deposition and remodeling always occurs.
* Incorrect (C): Incisions aligned with tension lines accelerate rather than slow the healing process because the wound edges naturally approximate without distracting forces.
* Incorrect (D): Aligning incisions parallel to Kraissl's lines optimizes the rate of tissue repair, making slowed healing with minimal scar formation incorrect.
* Incorrect (E): Cutting parallel to tension lines does not prolong the recovery period, and it cannot entirely eliminate scar tissue to yield no scar formation.
