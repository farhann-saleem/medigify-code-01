Question: A macrophage has all of the following features except:
A) An irregularly shaped nucleus
B) Few microfilaments
C) Many lysosomes
D) Many surface projections
E) Many tonofilaments
Correct Option: E
Explanation:
Correct (E): Many tonofilaments are characteristic of epithelial cells (keratin intermediate filaments) rather than phagocytic macrophages.
Incorrect (A): An irregularly shaped nucleus is incorrect because macrophages typically possess an indented, kidney-shaped, or irregular nucleus.
Incorrect (B): Few microfilaments is incorrect because macrophages contain a modest network of microfilaments, making this a true baseline feature rather than an absolute exception compared to tonofilaments.
Incorrect (C): Many lysosomes is incorrect because abundant lysosomes are required for the macrophage's primary function of phagocytosis and enzymatic degradation.
Incorrect (D): Many surface projections is incorrect because pseudopodia and irregular surface projections are structurally necessary for macrophage motility and antigen engulfment.

Question: Which one of the following cells located in the connective tissue arises from myeloid stem cells:
A) eosinophils
B) fibroblasts
C) osteoblasts
D) osteocytes
E) pericytes
Correct Option: A
Explanation:
Correct (A): Eosinophils are granulocytic leukocytes that differentiate in the bone marrow from a shared myeloid progenitor cell line.
Incorrect (B): fibroblasts is incorrect because fibroblasts originate locally from undifferentiated mesenchymal stem cells.
Incorrect (C): osteoblasts is incorrect because osteoblasts develop from local osteoprogenitor cells derived from the mesenchyme.
Incorrect (D): osteocytes is incorrect because osteocytes are mature bone cells derived from entrapped osteoblasts of mesenchymal lineage.
Incorrect (E): pericytes is incorrect because pericytes are periendothelial cells that develop from local mesenchymal stem cells.

Question: Which cells are not resident cells of Connective tissue:
A) adipocytes
B) fibroblasts

Correct Option: C
Explanation:
Correct (C): Macrophages are derived from circulating blood monocytes that migrate into tissues, classifying them immunologically and kinetically as transient or wandering cells under many conditions, unlike fixed resident populations.

Incorrect (D): myofibroblasts is incorrect because myofibroblasts are resident contractile cells that differentiate locally from existing fibroblasts during tissue repair.
Incorrect (E): pericytes is incorrect because pericytes are fixed resident cells that remain associated with the walls of small blood vessels.

Question: Which one of the following statements concerning loose connective tissue is true:
A) it acts as a medium for exchange of nutrients and wastes between the blood and tissues
B) it has a lower proportion of cells to fibers than does dense connective tissue
C) it is completely devoid of any elastic or reticular fibers
D) it is less abundant than dense connective tissue
E) it provides structural support for organs
Correct Option: A
Explanation:
Correct (A): The abundant, watery ground substance of loose connective tissue creates an ideal hydrophilic diffusion medium for nutrients and metabolic wastes traveling between capillaries and cells.
Incorrect (B): it has a lower proportion of cells to fibers than does dense connective tissue is incorrect because loose connective tissue actually features a much higher cell-to-fiber ratio than dense connective tissue.
Incorrect (C): it is completely devoid of any elastic or reticular fibers is incorrect because loose connective tissue characteristically contains all three fiber types intermeshed together.
Incorrect (D): it is less abundant than dense connective tissue is incorrect because loose connective tissue is the most ubiquitous and widely distributed connective tissue type in the human body.
Incorrect (E): it provides structural support for organs is incorrect because dense regular or irregular connective tissue, rather than loose, is primarily specialized for heavy structural and mechanical support.

Question: Fascia lata is an example of:
A) aponeurosis
B) dense irregular CT
C) dense regular CT
D) elastic CT
E) loose CT
Correct Option: C
Explanation:
Correct (C): The fascia lata is a deep investments of the thigh composed of dense regular CT arranged in tightly packed, parallel sheets of collagen to resist directional muscular tensile stress.
Incorrect (A): aponeurosis is incorrect because while structurally similar, an aponeurosis refers specifically to flat, sheet-like tendons that attach muscle to bone, whereas the fascia lata functions as an investing deep fascial sheath.
Incorrect (B): dense irregular CT is incorrect because the structural collagen fiber bundles in the fascia lata are highly ordered and parallel rather than randomly interwoven.
Incorrect (D): elastic CT is incorrect because the fascia lata is structurally reinforced by inextensible collagen fibers rather than elastic fibers.
Incorrect (E): loose CT is incorrect because loose CT contains abundant ground substance and sparse fibers, which cannot provide the dense mechanical containment of the fascia lata.

Question: Important target cells of leptin are found in which organ:
A) hypothalamus
B) large intestine
C) small intestine
D) thyroid gland
E) white adipose tissue
Correct Option: A
Explanation:
Correct (A): The hypothalamus contains abundant leptin receptors within the arcuate nucleus to regulate energy homeostasis, satiety, and metabolic rate.
Incorrect (B): large intestine is incorrect because the large intestine functions primarily in water absorption and fecal compaction, not as a primary neurological target for leptin satiety signaling.
Incorrect (C): small intestine is incorrect because while it secretes some secondary satiety peptides, it is not the primary anatomical target regulating central energy balance via leptin.
Incorrect (D): thyroid gland is incorrect because the thyroid is regulated primarily by TSH from the anterior pituitary, not directly by leptin targeting.
Incorrect (E): white adipose tissue is incorrect because white adipose tissue acts as the endocrine site of production and secretion for leptin, rather than its primary central destination.
