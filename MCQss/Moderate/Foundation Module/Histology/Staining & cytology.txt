Question 3
The following cell organelles are subjected to the enzyme histochemistry for the detection of acid phosphatase. Which organelle will show highest concentration of this enzyme?
A. Lysosomes
B. Plasma membrane
C. Ribosomes
D. Rough endoplasmic reticulum
E. Smooth endoplasmic reticulum
Correct Option: A
Explanation:
Correct (A): Lysosomes contain a high concentration of acid phosphatase, which serves as a classic histochemical marker for this organelle.
Incorrect (B): Plasma membrane is rich in enzymes like alkaline phosphatase and sodium-potassium ATPase, not acid phosphatase.
Incorrect (C): Ribosomes are composed of RNA and proteins and lack hydrolytic enzymes like acid phosphatase.
Incorrect (D): Rough endoplasmic reticulum is specialized for protein translation and folding, lacking lysosomal hydrolases.
Incorrect (E): Smooth endoplasmic reticulum is primarily involved in lipid synthesis and detoxification, not acid hydrolysis.

Question 4
Autophagy is the process by which cells:
A. generate localized tissue inflammation.
B. neutralize ingested chemical toxins.
C. target and destroy their own organelles.
D. trigger programmed systemic apoptosis.
E. undergo rapid spontaneous self-lysis.
Correct Option: C
Explanation:
Correct (C): Autophagy is a regulated, catabolic mechanism where cells target and destroy their own organelles or cytoplasmic components via lysosomal degradation.
Incorrect (A): Autophagy is an intracellular recycling pathway that typically limits or prevents the generation of localized tissue inflammation.
Incorrect (B): Cells neutralize ingested chemical toxins predominantly via cytochrome P450 enzymes in the smooth endoplasmic reticulum.
Incorrect (D): Autophagy is primarily a cell survival mechanism, distinct from triggering programmed systemic apoptosis.
Incorrect (E): Autophagy is a controlled, progressive degradation process rather than an uncoordinated, rapid spontaneous self-lysis.

Question: Actin is a microfilament that is involved with which of the following activities:
A. cell locomotion.
B. ciliary beating.
C. cytoplasmic streaming.
D. mitochondrial division.
E. mitotic spindle formation.
Correct Option: C
Explanation:
Correct (C): Cytoplasmic streaming relies on actin microfilaments interacting with myosin motor proteins to move organelles and cytosol through the cell.
Incorrect (A): Cell locomotion via pseudopodia or lamellipodia requires coordinated actin polymerization at the leading edge rather than simple actin-filament pathways.
Incorrect (B): Ciliary beating is driven by a microtubule-based axoneme structure powered by dynein motor proteins.
Incorrect (C): Mitochondrial division relies primarily on specialized dynamin-related GTPase proteins rather than an actin-mediated pathway.
Incorrect (E): Mitotic spindle formation is mediated entirely by microtubules radiating from chromosomes to separate sister chromatids.
