Question: One type of epithelium may change into another by a process called:
A. Amitosis
B. Atrophy
C. Metaplasia
D. Meiosis
E. Regeneration
Correct Option: C
Explanation:
Correct (C): Metaplasia is the reversible conversion of one mature epithelial cell type into another mature epithelial cell type, typically as an adaptive response to chronic environmental stress.
Incorrect (A): Amitosis refers to direct, non-mitotic cell division where the nucleus cleaves without spindle formation or chromosome condensation.
Incorrect (B): Atrophy represents a decrease in cell size and mass due to loss of substance or lack of use, rather than a phenotype switch.
Incorrect (D): Meiosis is a specialized form of cell division that reduces the chromosome number by half to produce haploid gametes.
Incorrect (E): Regeneration involves the replacement of damaged or lost tissue by the proliferation of the same cell type to restore original architecture.

Explanation:

Question 14
Using immunohistochemistry a population of cells is shown to be positive for the protein connexin. From this we can infer that the cells are connected by what type of junction?
A. Desmosomes (macula adherens)
B. Gap junctions (nexus)
C. Hemidesmosomes

Explanation:

Incorrect (A): Desmosomes (macula adherens) are structurally composed of desmogleins and desmocollins, which are cadherin family proteins rather than connexins.

Incorrect (D): Tight (occluding) junctions are composed of transmembrane proteins such as claudins and occludins to seal intercellular spaces.
Incorrect (E): Zonula adherens relies on classical E-cadherin proteins to anchor adjacent actin filament networks.
