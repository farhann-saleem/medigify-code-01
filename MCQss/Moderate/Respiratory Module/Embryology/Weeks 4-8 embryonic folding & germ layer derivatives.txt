A. 1st and 2nd pharyngeal arch
B. 2nd and 3rd pharyngeal arch
C. 3rd and 4th pharyngeal arch
D. 4th and 6th pharyngeal arch
E. 5th pharyngeal arch
Correct Option: D
Explanation:
* Correct (D): The intrinsic muscles of the larynx develop from the mesoderm of the 4th and 6th pharyngeal arches, innervated by branches of the vagus nerve.
* Incorrect (A): The 1st and 2nd pharyngeal arches give rise to muscles of mastication and facial expression, respectively.
* Incorrect (B): The 2nd and 3rd arches give rise to facial expression muscles and the stylopharyngeus muscle, respectively.
* Incorrect (C): The 3rd arch forms the stylopharyngeus muscle, while only the 4th arch forms the cricothyroid muscle.
* Incorrect (E): The 5th pharyngeal arch is a rudimentary structure that rapidly degenerates and contributes no lasting structures to the larynx.
