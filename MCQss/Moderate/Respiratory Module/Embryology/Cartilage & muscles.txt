A. the clavicle and neurocranium
B. the maxilla and mandible
C. the ribs and sternum
D. the ribs, sternum, and clavicle
E. the ribs, sternum, clavicle, and vertebrae Correct Option: C
Explanation:
* Correct (C): The ribs and sternum develop entirely through endochondral ossification, where a hyaline cartilage model is gradually replaced by bone.
* Incorrect (A): The clavicle and neurocranium primarily develop via intramembranous ossification, though parts of the cranial base use endochondral paths.
* Incorrect (B): The maxilla and mandible are components of the viscerocranium that develop predominantly via intramembranous ossification.
* Incorrect (D): The ribs, sternum, and clavicle is incorrect because the clavicle relies on intramembranous ossification for its medial and lateral development.
* Incorrect (E): The ribs, sternum, clavicle, and vertebrae is incorrect because the inclusion of the clavicle violates the pure endochondral criteria.
