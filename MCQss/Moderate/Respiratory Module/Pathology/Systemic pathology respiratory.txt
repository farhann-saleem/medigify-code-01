 Question: When IgE on a mast cell Fcε receptor is cross linked by an allergen, the mast cell responds by:
  A. presenting antigen to Th cells.
  B. secreting histamine.
  C. secreting more IgE.
  D. synthesizing complement proteins.
  E. undergoing apoptosis.
 Correct Option: B
 Explanation:
 •	Correct (B): Cross-linking of IgE bound to Fcε receptors triggers mast-cell degranulation with rapid release of histamine.
 •	Incorrect (A): Mast cells are not the principal antigen-presenting cells for Th-cell activation.
 •	Incorrect (C): IgE is produced by plasma cells, not mast cells.
 •	Incorrect (D): Complement proteins are synthesized mainly by hepatocytes rather than mast cells.
 •	Incorrect (E): Allergen-mediated activation causes degranulation, not apoptosis.

 Question: Which one of the following is associated with increased lung compliance:
  A. emphysema.
  B. kyphosis.
  C. pleural fibrosis.
  D. pulmonary edema.
  E. pulmonary fibrosis.
 Correct Option: A
 Explanation:
 •	Correct (A): Emphysema increases lung compliance because alveolar wall destruction reduces elastic recoil.
 •	Incorrect (B): Kyphosis restricts chest wall expansion and decreases overall compliance.
 •	Incorrect (C): Pleural fibrosis stiffens the lungs and chest wall.
 •	Incorrect (D): Pulmonary edema reduces compliance by filling alveoli with fluid.
 •	Incorrect (E): Pulmonary fibrosis markedly decreases lung compliance.
 Tags: Moderate, Pathology, Respiratory Module, Systemic Pathology: Respiratory

 Question: Bronchogenic carcinoma has increased incidence in pneumoconiosis. Most likely association is with:
  A. asbestosis.
  B. bagassosis.
  C. berylliosis.
  D. silicosis.
  E. talcosis.
 Correct Option: A
 Explanation:
 •	Correct (A): Asbestosis markedly increases the risk of bronchogenic carcinoma, especially in smokers.
 •	Incorrect (B): Bagassosis is a hypersensitivity pneumonitis without a strong carcinoma association.
 •	Incorrect (C): Berylliosis is mainly associated with granulomatous lung disease.
 •	Incorrect (D): Silicosis has a stronger association with tuberculosis than bronchogenic carcinoma.
 •	Incorrect (E): Talcosis is not classically linked to bronchogenic carcinoma.
 Tags: Moderate, Pathology, Respiratory Module, Systemic Pathology: Respiratory

 Question: In the pathogenesis of bronchiectasis, susceptibility to infection is due to:
  A. accumulation of thick and viscid secretions obstructing airways.
  B. chronic inflammation causing epithelial sloughing.
  C. immunodeficient status.
  D. progressive fibrosis of lung parenchyma.
  E. smooth muscle hypertrophy of bronchi.
 Correct Option: A
 Explanation:
 •	Correct (A): Accumulation of thick and viscid secretions obstructing airways impairs clearance and promotes recurrent infection.
 •	Incorrect (B): Epithelial sloughing is largely a consequence rather than the primary cause of susceptibility.
 •	Incorrect (C): Immunodeficient status may predispose but is not the general mechanism described.
 •	Incorrect (D): Progressive fibrosis of lung parenchyma is not the key initiating factor.
 •	Incorrect (E): Smooth muscle hypertrophy does not explain recurrent infection.
 Tags: Moderate, Pathology, Respiratory Module, Systemic Pathology: Respiratory
