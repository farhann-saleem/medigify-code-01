
Question 7:
 FEV₁/FVC ratio?
 A. Decreases in obstructive lung diseases
 B. Decreases in restrictive lung diseases
 C. Decreases in old age
 D. Is normally about 50%
 E. Is normally about 80%
 Correct Option: A
 Explanation:
 • Correct (A): Obstructive lung diseases reduce FEV₁ disproportionately, lowering FEV₁/FVC ratio.
 • Incorrect (B): Restrictive lung diseases reduce both FEV₁ and FVC proportionally, ratio remains normal or high.
 • Incorrect (C): Old age reduces lung elasticity but does not specifically lower FEV₁/FVC ratio.
 • Incorrect (D): Normal ratio is not 50%; this is too low.
 • Incorrect (E): Normal ratio is about 80%, but this is not the best distinguishing feature.

Question: Vital capacity minus expiratory reserve volume is equal to:
A. functional residual capacity
B. inspiratory capacity
C. inspiratory reserve volume
D. residual volume
E. total lung capacity
Correct Option: B
Explanation:

Correct (B): The inspiratory capacity equals tidal volume plus inspiratory reserve volume,
which matches the exact volume remaining when expiratory reserve volume is subtracted from
vital capacity.

Incorrect (A): The functional residual capacity is comprised of expiratory reserve volume plus
residual volume, which cannot be derived from subtracting expiratory reserve volume from vital
capacity.

Incorrect (C): The inspiratory reserve volume is only one component of inspiratory capacity,
lacking the tidal volume component.

Incorrect (D): The residual volume is not a component of vital capacity at all, making its
derivation via this subtraction impossible.

Incorrect (E): The total lung capacity requires the addition of residual volume to vital capacity,
rather than a subtraction of expiratory reserve volume.
