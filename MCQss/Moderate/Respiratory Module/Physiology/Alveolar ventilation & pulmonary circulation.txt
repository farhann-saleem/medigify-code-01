​Question: The apex compared with the base of the upright lung has:
A. higher compliance
B. higher PCO₂
C. higher ventilation-perfusion ratio
D. lower compliance
E. lower PCO₂
Correct Option: C
Explanation:
​Correct (C): Gravity causes both ventilation and perfusion to decrease from base to apex, but perfusion drops more drastically, resulting in a higher ventilation-perfusion ratio at the apex.
​Incorrect (A): The alveoli at the apex are already highly distended by gravitational intrapleural pressure, giving them a lower compliance rather than a higher compliance.
​Incorrect (B): Increased ventilation relative to perfusion causes greater clearance of carbon dioxide, resulting in a lower PCO₂ rather than a higher PCO₂.
​Incorrect (D): While the apex does have lower compliance, this choice is incorrect because a higher ventilation-perfusion ratio is the primary and definitive physiological hallmark compared to the base.
​Incorrect (E): While a lower PCO₂ is present at the apex, a higher ventilation-perfusion ratio is the direct physiological cause and the most comprehensive comparative parameter.

Question: Volume of dead space remains unchanged in:
A. old age
B. pneumonectomy
C. shallow breathing
D. standing
E. tracheostomy
Correct Option: D
Explanation:

Correct (D): While postural changes from lying down to standing slightly increase anatomical
dead space due to gravitational lung traction, the functional volume of anatomical dead space
remains essentially unchanged and physiological compared to the drastic structural alterations
seen in the other choices.

Incorrect (A): In old age, age-related dilation of alveolar ducts and loss of elastic tissue
increases the physiological dead space.

Incorrect (B): A pneumonectomy removes an entire lung, radically altering the total volume of
dead space and remaining conducting airways.

Incorrect (C): Shallow breathing increases the ratio of dead space to tidal ventilation, shifting
the relative physiological dead space fraction.

Incorrect (E): A tracheostomy bypasses the upper airway conducting zone, which significantly
decreases anatomical dead space volume.
