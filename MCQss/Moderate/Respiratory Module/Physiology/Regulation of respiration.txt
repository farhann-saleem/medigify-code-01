Question 2:
 A person shifts from Peshawar to an area of Swat valley about 10,000 feet above sea level. Which physiological change increases most rapidly to resist hypoxia?
 A. Arterial chemoreceptor activity
 B. Growth of systemic capillaries
 C. Pulmonary diffusing capacity
 D. RBC formation
 E. Ventricular hypertrophy
 Correct Option: A
 Explanation:
 • Correct (A): Arterial chemoreceptor activity increases immediately, driving hyperventilation to counter hypoxia.
 • Incorrect (B): Growth of systemic capillaries occurs slowly over weeks, not rapidly.
 • Incorrect (C): Pulmonary diffusing capacity improves gradually, not acutely.
 • Incorrect (D): RBC formation requires days to weeks via erythropoietin stimulation.
 • Incorrect (E): Ventricular hypertrophy develops only in chronic hypoxic stress, not rapidly.
 Tags: Moderate, Physiology, Respiratory, Regulation of Respiration

Question 3:
 At high altitudes?
 A. Chemoreceptors become more sensitive to CO₂
 B. Chemoreceptors become more sensitive to H⁺
 C. Chemoreceptors become more sensitive to O₂
 D. Chemoreceptors become more sensitive to O₂, CO₂ and H⁺
 E. Chemoreceptors become less sensitive to O₂
 Correct Option: A
 Explanation:
 • Correct (A): Chemoreceptors become more sensitive to CO₂, enhancing ventilatory drive under hypoxia.
 • Incorrect (B): Sensitivity to H⁺ does not increase significantly at altitude.
 • Incorrect (C): Sensitivity to O₂ is already high but does not increase further.
 • Incorrect (D): Combined sensitivity to O₂, CO₂ and H⁺ is not uniformly enhanced.
 • Incorrect (E): Chemoreceptors do not become less sensitive to O₂; hypoxia heightens their role.

Question 5:
 Which of the following is a common cause of hypercapnia?
 A. Hyperventilation
 B. Hypoventilation
 C. Increased oxygen delivery to tissues
 D. Increased oxygen saturation of Hb
 E. Reduced metabolic rate
 Correct Option: B
 Explanation:
 • Correct (B): Hypoventilation reduces CO₂ clearance, leading to hypercapnia.
 • Incorrect (A): Hyperventilation lowers CO₂, causing hypocapnia.
 • Incorrect (C): Increased oxygen delivery does not affect CO₂ retention.
 • Incorrect (D): Increased oxygen saturation of Hb does not alter CO₂ elimination.
 • Incorrect (E): Reduced metabolic rate decreases CO₂ production, not increases it.

 Tags: Moderate, Physiology, Respiratory, Regulation of Respiration
