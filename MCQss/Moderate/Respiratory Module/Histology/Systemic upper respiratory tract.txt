Question 7: All the following about the olfactory epithelium are true EXCEPT:
A. basal cells rest on Descemet’s membrane.
B. Bowman's glands are present underneath.
C. sustentacular cells have prominent microvilli.
D. the lamina propria contains unmyelinated axons.
E. the neuro-epithelium have olfactory vesicles.
Correct Option: A
Explanation:
Correct (A): Basal cells rest on a standard epithelial basement membrane, whereas Descemet’s membrane is a specialized basement membrane located exclusively in the cornea of the eye.
Incorrect (B): Bowman's glands are present underneath the epithelium in the lamina propria, where they produce a serous fluid to dissolve odorants.
Incorrect (C): Sustentacular cells have prominent microvilli on their apical surfaces to support olfactory cilia and maintain the ionic microenvironment.
Incorrect (D): The lamina propria contains unmyelinated axons originating from olfactory receptor neurons that coalesce to form the olfactory nerve fascicles.
Incorrect (E): The neuro-epithelium have olfactory vesicles, which are the bulbous apical terminations of olfactory neurons from which non-motile cilia project.
