Question: Which of the following enzyme is the drug of choice in acute myocardial infarction used for thrombolysis?
 A. Asparaginase
 B. Hyaluronidase
 C. Streptokinase
 D. Tissue plasminogen activator
 E. Urokinase
 Correct Option: C
 Explanation:
 Correct (C): Streptokinase activates plasminogen → plasmin → fibrin clot breakdown in MI
 Incorrect (A): Asparaginase is used in acute lymphoblastic leukemia
 Incorrect (B): Hyaluronidase increases tissue permeability, not thrombolysis
 Incorrect (D): tPA is effective but not classical “enzyme of choice” in many exam keys
 Incorrect (E): Urokinase is thrombolytic but less commonly first-line in standard exams
 Tags: Moderate, Biochemistry, Cardiovascular, Clinical Enzymology

Question: A 55-year-old patient with chest pain radiating to left shoulder, with nausea and sweating. Which serum enzyme or isoenzyme is most appropriate in early assessment of MI?
 A. AST
 B. CK-MM
 C. CK-MB
 D. LDH
 E. Myoglobin
 Correct Option: C
 Explanation:
 Correct (C): CK-MB rises early and is relatively cardiac-specific
 Incorrect (A): AST rises later and is non-specific
 Incorrect (B): CK-MM is skeletal muscle isoenzyme
 Incorrect (D): LDH rises late and lacks early diagnostic value
 Incorrect (E): Myoglobin rises earliest but is not enzyme or specific
 Tags: Moderate, Biochemistry, Cardiovascular, Clinical Enzymology

Question: Which enzyme is specifically raised in myocardial infarction?
 A. LDH
 B. SGPT
 C. Alkaline phosphatase
 D. Amylase
 E. Lipase
 Correct Option: A
 Explanation:
 Correct (A): LDH rises after MI (especially LDH1 flip)
 Incorrect (B): SGPT is liver enzyme
 Incorrect (C): ALP is biliary/bone marker
 Incorrect (D): Amylase is pancreatic
 Incorrect (E): Lipase is pancreatic
 Tags: Moderate, Biochemistry, Cardiovascular, Clinical Enzymology

Question: In muscle disorders, all of the following will increase except:
 A. acid phosphatase
 B. aldolase
 C. aspartate transaminase
 D. creatine phosphokinase
 E. lactate dehydrogenase
 Correct Option: A
 Explanation:
 Correct (A): Acid phosphatase is primarily associated with prostate tissue and lysosomes, not skeletal muscle injury.
 Incorrect (B): Aldolase increases in muscle damage due to release from injured muscle fibers.
 Incorrect (C): Aspartate transaminase may rise because it is present in skeletal muscle.
 Incorrect (D): Creatine phosphokinase is a major marker of muscle injury.
 Incorrect (E): Lactate dehydrogenase increases following muscle cell breakdown.
 Tags: Moderate, Biochemistry, Cardiovascular, Clinical Enzymology
