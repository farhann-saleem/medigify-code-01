
Question: According to Einthoven’s law, if lead I is 0.5 mV and lead III is 0.7 mV, what is lead II voltage:
  A. 0.2 mV.
  B. 0.6 mV.
  C. 0.9 mV.
  D. 1.2 mV.
  E. 1.5 mV.
  Correct Option: D
  Explanation:
 •	Correct (D): Lead II equals Lead I + Lead III, giving 1.2 mV.
 •	Incorrect (A): 0.2 mV ignores additive limb lead relationship.
 •	Incorrect (B): 0.6 mV underestimates summed potentials.
 •	Incorrect (C): 0.9 mV is mathematically incorrect sum.
 •	Incorrect (E): 1.5 mV overestimates combined voltage.
Tags: Moderate, Physiology, Cardiovascular System, Electrocardiogram (ECG) Principles

Question: The Q-T interval represents the approximate time of:
  A. atrial contraction.
  B. ventricular contraction.
  C. AV nodal delay.
  D. atrial relaxation.
  E. atrial depolarization only.
  Correct Option: B
  Explanation:
 •	Correct (B): QT interval reflects total ventricular electrical activity and mechanical contraction phase.
 •	Incorrect (A): Atrial contraction corresponds to P wave.

 •	Incorrect (D): Atrial relaxation is not represented as QT interval.
 •	Incorrect (E): Atrial depolarization is limited to P wave only.

Question 8
A patient with severe pulmonary emphysema has an ECG that displays low-voltage QRS complexes across all limb and chest leads. What is the biophysical explanation for this low voltage?
a. heart muscle undergoes atrophy due to chronic hypoxemia
b. high potassium levels in the lungs short-circuit the limb leads
c. hyperinflated lungs contain excess air that acts as an electrical insulator, reducing current transmission to the skin
d. resting membrane potential of cardiac muscle becomes hyperpolarized to -110 mV
e. ventricular depolarization becomes restricted to the interventricular septum
Correct Option: c
Explanation:
Correct (c): Hyperinflated lungs contain excess air that acts as an electrical insulator, reducing current transmission to the skin and lowering ECG voltage.
Incorrect (a): Chronic hypoxemia does not produce the diffuse low-voltage pattern through cardiac atrophy.
Incorrect (b): Potassium levels in the lungs do not short-circuit ECG leads.
Incorrect (d): Cardiac muscle does not normally hyperpolarize to -110 mV in emphysema.
Incorrect (e): Ventricular depolarization remains widespread and is not limited to the interventricular septum.

Question 11

a. first-degree atrioventricular block

Explanation:

Incorrect (d): Premature atrial contraction is an early ectopic atrial beat.
Incorrect (e): Third-degree complete AV block shows complete AV dissociation.

Question 15
A standard ECG displays a bizarre, widened QRS complex (0.15 seconds) with an inverted T wave, occurring prematurely without a preceding P wave. The patient's underlying sinus rhythm is otherwise regular. This single beat is a:
a. junctional escape beat
b. mobitz II block variant
c. premature atrial contraction
d. premature ventricular contraction (PVC)
e. ventricular escape beat
Correct Option: d
Explanation:
Correct (d): A premature ventricular contraction (PVC) produces a premature, bizarre, widened QRS complex without a preceding P wave.
Incorrect (a): Junctional escape beats are usually narrow and occur late rather than prematurely.
Incorrect (b): Mobitz II is a conduction block, not an isolated premature ventricular beat.
Incorrect (c): Premature atrial contractions usually have an associated ectopic P wave and narrow QRS complex.
Incorrect (e): Ventricular escape beats occur after pauses, not prematurely.
