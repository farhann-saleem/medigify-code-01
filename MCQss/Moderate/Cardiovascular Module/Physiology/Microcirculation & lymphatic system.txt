Question: What clinical sign is characteristically seen in advanced right-sided heart failure but is absent in isolated early left-sided failure?
 A. Cheyne-Stokes respiration
 B. Jugular venous distension with peripheral pitting edema
 C. Pink frothy sputum
 D. Prolonged PR interval on ECG
 E. Pulmonary crackles with basal crepitations
 Correct Option: B
 Explanation:
 • Correct (B): Right-sided failure causes systemic venous congestion leading to jugular venous distension and dependent peripheral edema.
 • Incorrect (A): Cheyne-Stokes respiration occurs in severe heart failure but is not specific to right-sided failure.
 • Incorrect (C): Pink frothy sputum is a hallmark of acute left-sided pulmonary edema.
 • Incorrect (D): PR prolongation is a conduction abnormality, not a heart failure sign.
 • Incorrect (E): Basal crackles are typical of left-sided pulmonary congestion.
