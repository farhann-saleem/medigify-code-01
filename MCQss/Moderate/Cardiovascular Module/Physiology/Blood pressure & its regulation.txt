Question: What is the immediate baseline response of the nervous system within seconds of acute myocardial damage?
 A. Activation of the parasympathetic vagal output
 B. Activation of the sympathetic nervous system
 C. Complete shutdown of the vasomotor center
 D. Immediate inhibition of baroreceptor firing
 E. Suppression of epinephrine release from adrenal medulla
 Correct Option: B
 Explanation:
 • Correct (B): Acute myocardial damage lowers cardiac output and arterial pressure, triggering baroreceptor-mediated sympathetic activation within seconds to maintain perfusion.
 • Incorrect (A): Parasympathetic vagal output decreases rather than increases in hypotension.
 • Incorrect (C): The vasomotor center is activated, not shut down, in acute hypotension.
 • Incorrect (D): Baroreceptor firing decreases due to low stretch; it is not the primary active response.
 • Incorrect (E): Epinephrine release is stimulated, not suppressed, in sympathetic activation.
