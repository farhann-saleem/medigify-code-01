Question: The total number of isomers of glucose is:
A. 2
B. 4
C. 8

Explanation:

 * Incorrect (A): The value 2 represents only a single stereochemical option pair (such as D versus L configurations) rather than the complete structural suite.
 * Incorrect (B): The value 4 corresponds to a carbohydrate structure possessing only 2 asymmetric chiral centers (2^2=4).
 * Incorrect (C): The value 8 is characteristic of an aldopentose sugar configuration containing exactly 3 asymmetric centers (2^3=8).
 * Incorrect (E): The value 32 corresponds to an expanded molecular skeleton with 5 asymmetric centers (2^5=32), which exceeds the structural limits of an aldohexose.

Incorrect (C): Glyceraldehyde is a three-carbon triose sugar that must exist exclusively in an open-chain conformation because it contains too few carbons to cyclize.

Question: Mutarotation of a sugar can be best described as:
A. interconversion of aldose and ketose forms
B. interconversion of D- and L- forms
C. interconversion of dextrorotatory and levorotatory forms
D. interconversion of pyranose and furanose forms
E. rearrangement of groups at the anomeric carbon via the open-chain form
Correct Option: E
Explanation:
Correct (E): Mutarotation of a sugar can be best described as rearrangement of groups at the anomeric carbon via the open-chain form, resulting in an equilibrium shift between the \alpha and \beta anomers and a corresponding change in optical rotation.
Incorrect (A): The interconversion of aldose and ketose forms describes structural isomerization catalyzed by isomerase enzymes rather than spontaneous anomeric mutarotation.
Incorrect (B): The interconversion of D- and L- forms represents enantiomeric racemization, which requires breaking and reforming covalent bonds at all chiral centers.
Incorrect (C): The interconversion of dextrorotatory and levorotatory forms refers to optical racemization of a bulk mixture rather than the specific equilibrium change between cyclic anomers.
Incorrect (D): The interconversion of pyranose and furanose forms describes a alteration in overall ring size rather than the configuration change localized exclusively to the anomeric carbon.
