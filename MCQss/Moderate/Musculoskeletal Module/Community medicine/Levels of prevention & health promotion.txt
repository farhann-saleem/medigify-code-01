Question: What is the most effective way to prevent MSDs related to mobile addiction:
 Options:
 a) Limiting mobile device use
 b) Taking regular breaks
 c) Maintaining good posture
 d) All of the above
 Correct Option:
 Ans. All of the above
 Explanation: Prevention requires a multifactorial approach: limiting use, taking breaks, and correcting posture. Each option alone helps, but the combined intervention is most effective.

Question: What is the proper way to lift heavy objects to prevent low back pain:
 Options:
 a) Bend your knees and keep your back straight
 b) Bend your back and twist your body
 c) Lift with your arms
 d) Use a lifting belt
 Correct Option:
 Ans. Bend your knees and keep your back straight
 Explanation: This distributes weight to strong leg muscles, reducing strain on the spine. Bending the back increases the risk of disc prolapse and injury.
 Tags: Moderate, Community Medicine, Occupational & Environmental Health, Levels of Prevention & Health Promotion

Question: What is the most effective way to prevent MSDs in child laborers:
 Options:
 a) Providing ergonomic workstations
 b) Limiting work hours
 c) Providing regular breaks
 d) All of the above
 Correct Option:
 Ans. All of the above
 Explanation: Ergonomics requires a safe environment + reduced workload + rest breaks. Single measures are insufficient; prevention must be comprehensive.

Question: Replacing manual labor with machines is an example of which level of prevention against musculoskeletal disorders:
 Options:
 a) Primary prevention
 b) Secondary prevention
 c) Tertiary prevention
 d) Quaternary prevention
 Correct Option:
 Ans. Primary prevention
 Explanation: Primary prevention aims to prevent disease before it occurs by removing risk factors. Mechanization reduces physical strain, which prevents MSD development.
 Tags: Moderate, Community Medicine, Occupational & Environmental Health, Levels of Prevention & Health Promotion
