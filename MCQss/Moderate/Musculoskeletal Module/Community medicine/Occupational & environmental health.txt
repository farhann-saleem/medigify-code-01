Question: What is the most common risk factor for MSDs related to excessive mobile usage:
 Options:
 a) Repetitive motions
 b) Forceful exertions
 c) Awkward postures
 d) Poor ergonomics
 Correct Option:
 Ans. Awkward postures
 Explanation: Sustained neck flexion and poor posture are the main drivers of “text neck”. Repetition contributes, but posture is the dominant factor.

Question: Select the correct combination of common ergonomic risk factors for office workers:
 Options:
 a) Repetition, awkward postures, and contact stress
 b) Lifting, lighting, and noise
 c) Vibration, temperature extremes, and forceful exertions
 d) Pushing, bending, and static posture
 Correct Option:
 Ans. Repetition, awkward postures, and contact stress
 Explanation: These are the classic triad in ergonomics. Other options include unrelated or incomplete risk factors.
