Question: A girl presented with ear scar after piercing ear. This scar may contain:
A. calcified bone tissue
B. elastic cartilage tissue
C. fibrocartilage tissue
D. hyaline cartilage tissue
E. dense fibrous connective tissue
Correct Option: E
Explanation:
Correct (E): Cutaneous wound healing and keloid or hypertrophic scar formation following trauma are driven by dense fibrous connective tissue characterized by unorganized type I collagen deposition.
Incorrect (A): Calcified bone tissue forms via osteoblast activity during skeletal mineralization and does not compose soft tissue cutaneous scars.
Incorrect (B): Elastic cartilage tissue forms the natural, flexible structural framework of the pinna but does not regenerate to form cutaneous post-traumatic scars.
Incorrect (C): Fibrocartilage tissue is restricted to high-stress fibrocartilaginous joints and symphyses and is not synthesized during dermal scar formation.
Incorrect (D): Hyaline cartilage tissue serves as skeletal templates or articular coverings and does not participate in cutaneous soft-tissue fibroproliferative repair.
