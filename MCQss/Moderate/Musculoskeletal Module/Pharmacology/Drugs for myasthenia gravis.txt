
Question: In patients with myasthenia, an acute worsening of symptoms is usually relieved by:
A. cholinergic antagonist
B. increasing cholinesterase inhibitor
C. nicotinic antagonist
D. NMJ polarizing agent
E. reversible muscarinic blocker
Correct Option: B
Explanation:

* ​Correct (B): Increasing cholinesterase inhibitor doses stops the breakdown of acetylcholine, amplifying its concentration at the NMJ to help overcome the autoimmune loss of nicotinic receptors.
* ​Incorrect (A): Cholinergic antagonist drugs block acetylcholine receptors, which further worsens flaccid skeletal muscle weakness in a myasthenic patient.
* ​Incorrect (C): Nicotinic antagonist drugs block the remaining functional motor endplate receptors, which can trigger acute respiratory failure or a severe paralytic crisis.
* ​Incorrect (D): NMJ polarizing agent describes depolarizing neuromuscular blockers like succinylcholine, which induce paralysis rather than therapeutic muscle strength.
* ​Incorrect (E): Reversible muscarinic blocker agents like atropine reduce autonomic side effects but do not improve skeletal muscle neuromuscular transmission.

Question: Neostigmine differs from pilocarpine (muscarinic agonist) in having effects on:
A. bladder tone
B. bowel motility
C. skeletal muscle
D. sweat gland
E. vascular tone
Correct Option: C
Explanation:

* ​Correct (C): Neostigmine inhibits acetylcholinesterase globally, allowing acetylcholine to accumulate at both muscarinic and nicotinic sites, which produces prominent functional effects on skeletal muscle.
* ​Incorrect (A): Bladder tone is regulated by muscarinic receptors, meaning both neostigmine and pilocarpine will stimulate detrusor smooth muscle contraction.
* ​Incorrect (B): Bowel motility is driven by enteric muscarinic activation, so both drugs enhance peristaltic contractions in a similar manner.
* ​Incorrect (D): Sweat gland cells use sympathetic cholinergic muscarinic pathways, making them highly responsive to both of these agents.
* ​Incorrect (E): Vascular tone responds to muscarinic endothelial activation via nitric oxide release, a path stimulated by both direct agonists and cholinesterase inhibitors.

Question: Mr Green has just been diagnosed with dysautonomia. Pyridostigmine and neostigmine may cause which one of the following in this patient:
A. bronchodilation
B. cycloplegia
C. diarrhea
D. mydriasis
E. xerostomia
Correct Option: C
Explanation:

* ​Correct (C): diarrhea is a direct result of increased parasympathetic tone and hypermotility in the gastrointestinal tract driven by acetylcholinesterase inhibition.
* ​Incorrect (A): bronchodilation is a sympathetic response, whereas pyridostigmine and neostigmine promote parasympathetic bronchoconstriction via muscarinic stimulation.
* ​Incorrect (B): cycloplegia refers to paralysis of accommodation (loss of near vision), whereas these drugs cause ciliary muscle contraction leading to accommodation for near vision.
* ​Incorrect (D): mydriasis is pupillary dilation mediated by sympathetic activation, whereas these cholinomimetics induce pupillary constriction (miosis).
* ​Incorrect (E): xerostomia refers to dry mouth, whereas acetylcholinesterase inhibitors significantly elevate salivary production, resulting in sialorrhea.
    Tags: Moderate, Pharmacology, Musculoskeletal, Drugs for Myasthenia Gravis
