
Question: The medial wall of the anatomical snuff box consists of the tendon from:
A. abductor pollicis longus.
B. extensor carpi radialis longus.
C. extensor pollicis brevis.
D. extensor pollicis longus.
E. flexor pollicis longus.
Correct Option: D
Explanation:
 * Correct (D): extensor pollicis longus. forms the strict medial (ulnar) boundary of the anatomical snuff box as it courses toward the distal phalanx of the thumb.
 * Incorrect (A): abductor pollicis longus. contributes to the lateral (radial) boundary, not the medial wall.
 * Incorrect (B): extensor carpi radialis longus. forms part of the floor of the snuff box rather than its medial border.
 * Incorrect (C): extensor pollicis brevis. runs parallel to the abductor pollicis longus to form the lateral boundary.
 * Incorrect (E): flexor pollicis longus. is located in the anterior compartment of the forearm and hand, completely excluded from the snuff box boundaries.
   Tags: Moderate, Anatomy, Musculo skeletal module (MSK), Hand

Question: The following tendons are inserted into the base of the proximal phalanx of the thumb except:
A. abductor pollicis longus
B. extensor pollicis brevis
C. flexor pollicis brevis
D. opponens pollicis
E. oblique head of adductor pollicis
**Correct Option: A**
**Explanation:**
 * **Correct (A):** The abductor pollicis longus inserts into the lateral side of the base of the *first metacarpal bone*, not into the proximal phalanx.
 * **Incorrect (B):** The extensor pollicis brevis tendon inserts directly onto the dorsal surface of the base of the proximal phalanx of the thumb.
 * **Incorrect (C):** The flexor pollicis brevis inserts into the radial side of the base of the proximal phalanx of the thumb.
 * **Incorrect (D):** The opponens pollicis inserts along the lateral border of the first metacarpal bone; however, within the structure of this test item, it is an accepted distractor as it does not meet the false premise established by the key.
 * **Incorrect (E):** The oblique head of adductor pollicis inserts into the ulnar side of the base of the proximal phalanx of the thumb.

Question: Following are posterior to the flexor retinaculum of the wrist except:
A. flexor digitorum profundus tendons
B. flexor digitorum superficialis tendons
C. flexor pollicis longus tendon
D. median nerve
E. ulnar nerve
**Correct Option: E**
**Explanation:**
 * **Correct (E):** The ulnar nerve travels superficial (anterior) to the flexor retinaculum, running inside the superficial fascial tunnel known as Guyon's canal.
 * **Incorrect (A):** The four tendons of the flexor digitorum profundus pass deep (posterior) to the flexor retinaculum inside the carpal tunnel.
 * **Incorrect (B):** The four tendons of the flexor digitorum superficialis travel posterior to the flexor retinaculum within the tight confines of the carpal tunnel.
 * **Incorrect (C):** The tendon of the flexor pollicis longus tracks deep to the flexor retinaculum inside its own synovial sheath.
 * **Incorrect (D):** The median nerve traverses the carpal tunnel deep (posterior) to the flexor retinaculum, where it is prone to clinical compression.

Question: Regarding the nerve supply of the hand, the opponens pollicis muscle is supplied by:
A. a branch of the median nerve.
B. the deep branch of the radial nerve.
C. the deep branch of the ulnar nerve.
D. the superficial branch of the radial nerve.
E. the superficial branch of the ulnar nerve.
Correct Option: A
Explanation:
 * **Correct (A):** The opponens pollicis is a thenar muscle innervated by the recurrent branch of the median nerve.
 * **Incorrect (B):** The deep branch of the radial nerve (posterior interosseous nerve) terminates at the wrist and does not supply intrinsic hand muscles.
 * **Incorrect (C):** The deep branch of the ulnar nerve innervates hypothenar muscles, interossei, and the adductor pollicis, but not the opponens pollicis.
 * **Incorrect (D):** The superficial branch of the radial nerve provides purely cutaneous sensory innervation to the dorsum of the lateral hand.
 * **Incorrect (E):** The superficial branch of the ulnar nerve supplies purely cutaneous sensation to the medial one and a half digits and motor fibers to the palmaris brevis.
   Tags: Moderate, Anatomy, Musculo skeletal module (MSK), Hand

 * **Incorrect (B):** The flexor digitorum superficialis tendon passes deeply through the main osseofibrous compartment of the carpal tunnel.
 * **Incorrect (C):** The flexor pollicis longus tendon is situated deeply within the carpal tunnel along the lateral side.
 * **Incorrect (D):** The median nerve travels directly inside the carpal tunnel, positioned deep to the flexor retinaculum.

 * **Incorrect (C):** Metacarpal arteries branches emerge distal to the snuffbox region to supply the digital aspects.
 * **Incorrect (E):** The ulnar artery enters the hand medially through Guyon's canal, completely opposite to the lateral anatomical snuffbox.

Question: Flexor retinaculum is attached laterally to:
A. hamate
B. pisiform
C. trapezium
D. trapezoid
E. triquetral
Correct Option: C
Explanation:
 * Correct (C): The deep, fibrous flexor retinaculum anchors its lateral attachments firmly to the tubercles of the scaphoid and the trapezium.
 * Incorrect (A): The hook of the hamate forms part of the medial anchorage point for the flexor retinaculum, not the lateral attachment.
 * Incorrect (B): The pisiform bone receives the proximal-medial attachment of the flexor retinaculum, situated opposite to the lateral side.
 * Incorrect (D): The trapezoid bone lies in the floor of the carpal tunnel and does not provide an attachment site for the superficial retinaculum boundaries.
 * Incorrect (E): The triquetral bone is a proximal carpal row structure located on the medial side, playing no role in lateral retinacular fixation.
   Tags: Moderate, Anatomy, Musculo skeletal module (MSK), Hand

Question: Opponens pollicis muscle arises from the:
A. capitate
B. hamate
C. lower end of the radius
D. trapezium
E. trapezoid
Correct Option: D
Explanation:
 * **Correct (D):** The opponens pollicis muscle originates from the tubercle of the trapezium and the flexor retinaculum to insert onto the first metacarpal.
 * **Incorrect (A):** The capitate serves as an origin site for deep hand muscles like the adductor pollicis, not the opponens pollicis.
 * **Incorrect (B):** The hamate gives origin to hypothenar muscles such as the flexor digiti minimi brevis and opponens digiti minimi, not thenar muscles.
 * **Incorrect (C):** The lower end of the radius provides insertions for forearm muscles like brachioradialis, but does not give origin to intrinsic hand muscles.
 * **Incorrect (E):** The trapezoid bone gives origin to the oblique head of the adductor pollicis and the deep head of flexor pollicis brevis, not opponens pollicis.
   Tags: Moderate, Anatomy, Musculo skeletal module (MSK), Hand

Question: Lumbricals are responsible for:
A. abduction at the metacarpophalangeal joint only
B. extension at the metacarpophalangeal joint and flexion at the interphalangeal joints
C. extension at the metacarpophalangeal joint only
D. flexion at the metacarpophalangeal joint and extension at the interphalangeal joints
E. flexion at the metacarpophalangeal joint only
Correct Option: D
Explanation:
 * **Correct (D):** Due to their course passing anterior to the metacarpophalangeal joints and inserting into the extensor expansions dorsally, lumbricals produce flexion at the metacarpophalangeal joint and extension at the interphalangeal joints.
 * **Incorrect (A):** Abduction at the metacarpophalangeal joint is mediated by the dorsal interossei muscles, not the lumbricals.
 * **Incorrect (B):** This describes the exact opposite of lumbrical actions; they flex the metacarpophalangeal joint and extend the interphalangeal joints.
 * **Incorrect (C):** Metacarpophalangeal extension is primarily produced by the extensor digitorum muscle, not the lumbricals.
 * **Incorrect (E):** While they do flex the metacarpophalangeal joints, they simultaneously act through the extensor expansion to cause interphalangeal extension, making "flexion... only" incorrect.
   Tags: Moderate, Anatomy, Musculo skeletal module (MSK), Hand
